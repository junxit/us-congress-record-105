# REPORT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 2

```
  REPORT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 2

  Thee PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the requirements of section 657 of the Department 
of Energy Organization Act (Public Law 95-91; 42 U.S.C. 7267), I 
transmit herewith the 31st Annual Report of the Department of Energy, 
which covers the years 1994 and 1995.
                                                    William J. Clinton.
  The White House, January 7, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
