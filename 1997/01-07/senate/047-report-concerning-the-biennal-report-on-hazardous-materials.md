# REPORT CONCERNING THE BIENNAL REPORT ON HAZARDOUS MATERIALS--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 3

```
 REPORT CONCERNING THE BIENNAL REPORT ON HAZARDOUS MATERIALS--MESSAGE 
                        FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 3

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with Public Law 103-272, as amended (49 U.S.C. 
5121(e)), I transmit herewith the Biennial Report on Hazardous 
Materials Transportation for Calendar Years 1994-1995 of the Department 
of Transportation.
                                                    William J. Clinton.
  The White House, January 7, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
