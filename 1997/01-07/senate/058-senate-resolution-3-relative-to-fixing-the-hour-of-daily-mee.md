# SENATE RESOLUTION 3--RELATIVE TO FIXING THE HOUR OF DAILY MEETING

**Measures:** S.Res. 3

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 3--RELATIVE TO FIXING THE HOUR OF DAILY MEETING

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
passed.

                               S. Res. 3

       Resolved, That the hour of daily meeting of the Senate be 
     12 o'clock meridian unless otherwise ordered.

                          ____________________
```
