# SENATE RESOLUTION 4--RELATIVE TO ELECTING SENATOR STROM THURMOND AS PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 4

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 4--RELATIVE TO ELECTING SENATOR STROM THURMOND AS 
                         PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
passed.

                               S. Res. 4

       Resolved, That Strom Thurmond, a Senator from the State of 
     South Carolina, be, and he is hereby, elected President of 
     the Senate pro tempore, to hold office during the pleasure of 
     the Senate, in accordance with rule I, paragraph 1, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
