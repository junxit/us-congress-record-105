# SENATE RESOLUTION 5--RELATIVE TO NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 5

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 5--RELATIVE TO NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE 
                  ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
passed.

                               S. Res. 5

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of Strom Thurmond, a Senator from 
     the State of South Carolina, as President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
