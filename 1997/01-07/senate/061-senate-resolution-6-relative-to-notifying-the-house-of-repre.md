# SENATE RESOLUTION 6--RELATIVE TO NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 6

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 6--RELATIVE TO NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES 
               OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
passed.

                               S. Res. 6

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of Strom Thurmond, a Senator from the State of 
     South Carolina, as President pro tempore.
```
