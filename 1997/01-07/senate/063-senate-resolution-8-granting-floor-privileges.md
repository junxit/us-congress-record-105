# SENATE RESOLUTION 8--GRANTING FLOOR PRIVILEGES

**Measures:** S.Res. 8

```
             SENATE RESOLUTION 8--GRANTING FLOOR PRIVILEGES

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and passed.

                               S. Res. 8

       Resolved, That an employee in the office of Senator Max 
     Cleland, to be designated from time to time by Senator 
     Cleland, shall have the privilege of the Senate floor during 
     any period when Senator Cleland is in the Senate chamber 
     during the 105th Congress.
```
