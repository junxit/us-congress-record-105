# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Livingston, Bob (L000371) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 25

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. LIVINGSTON. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican 
Conference, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 25) and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 25

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and they are 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees:
       Committee on Science: Mr. Sensenbrenner, Chairman.
       Committee on Small Business: Mr. Talent, Chairman.
       Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Mr. Stump, Chairman.

  The resolution was agreed to. A motion to reconsider was laid on the 
table.

                          ____________________
```
