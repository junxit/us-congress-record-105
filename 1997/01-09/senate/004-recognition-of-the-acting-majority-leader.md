# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT], Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The acting majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, until the leader takes the floor of the 
U.S. Senate, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
