# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of January 20 through January 24

  Monday, Inauguration Ceremonies.

  Committee on International Relations, January 21, to meet for 
organizational purposes, 3 p.m., 2172 Rayburn.

meeting, 9 a.m., 2141 Rayburn.
  January 22, Subcommittee on the Constitution, hearing regarding 
limiting terms of office for Members of the U.S. Senate and the U.S. 
House of Representatives, 9:30 a.m., 2141 Rayburn.
