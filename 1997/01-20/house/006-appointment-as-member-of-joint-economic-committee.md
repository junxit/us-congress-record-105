# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

```
           APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of 15 U.S.C. 
1024(a) and the order of House of Tuesday, January 7, 1997, authorizing 
the Speaker and the minority leader to accept resignations and to make 
appointments authorized by law or by the House, the Speaker, on 
Thursday, January 9, 1997, appointed to the Joint Economic Committee 
the following Member of the House: Mr. Saxton, New Jersey.
```
