# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 8

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  At the conclusion of the inaugural ceremonies (at 12 o'clock and 48 
minutes p.m.), the House, without returning to its Chamber, pursuant to 
House Resolution 8, stood in adjournment until Tuesday, January 21, 
1997, at 12 noon.
```
