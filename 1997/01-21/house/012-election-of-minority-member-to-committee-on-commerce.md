# ELECTION OF MINORITY MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 33

```
          ELECTION OF MINORITY MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE

  Mr. FAZIO of California. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution 
(H. Res. 33) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 33

       Resolved, That the following named Member, be elected to 
     the Committee on Commerce, be designated to rank on that 
     committee as follows:

[[Page H236]]

       Tom Sawyer of Ohio, to rank directly below Eliot Engel of 
     New York.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
