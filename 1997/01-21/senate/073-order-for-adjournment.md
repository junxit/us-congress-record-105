# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN], Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
previous order, following the remarks of Senator Murray of Washington.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
