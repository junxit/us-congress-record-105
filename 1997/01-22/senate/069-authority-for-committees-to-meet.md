# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet on Wednesday, January 22, 1997, 
at 10:30 a.m. in open session, to consider the nomination of William S. 
Cohen to be Secretary of Defense.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
