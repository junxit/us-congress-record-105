# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M., THURSDAY, JANUARY 23, 1997

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 22

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M., THURSDAY, JANUARY 23, 1997

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. If there is no further business, Mr. President, to 
come before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in adjournment under the previous order in accordance with the 
provisions of Senate Resolution 22.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:30 p.m., adjourned until 
Thursday, January 23, 1997, at 11 a.m.
```
