# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, JANUARY 28, 1997

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, JANUARY 28, 1997

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands adjourned until 10 a.m., Tuesday, January 28, 1997.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:59 and 30 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, January 28, 1997, at 10 a.m.
```
