# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The able acting majority leader, Senator 
McCain, is recognized.


                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I would 
like to announce today's schedule. In a moment, the Senate will proceed 
to executive session to begin 30 minutes of debate on the nomination of 
William Daley, to be Secretary of Commerce.
  At the expiration of that debate time, the Senate will vote on the 
confirmation of that nomination. Therefore, all Senators should expect 
a rollcall vote this morning at approximately 10 a.m. Senator Lott has 
announced that this vote will be the last rollcall vote of the week. 
However, the Senate may be asked to consider additional executive or 
legislative matters that can be cleared.

                          ____________________
```
