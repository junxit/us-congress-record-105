  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, the Committee on Appropriations held its 
organizational meeting on January 28. Among other business conducted, 
the Committee approved subcommittee assignments for the 105th Congress. 
I submit a list of the subcommittees and their membership for the 105th 
Congress, and ask unanimous consent that it be printed in the Record.
  There being no objection, the list was ordered to be printed in the 
Record, as follows:
