# PERMISSION FOR SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTION TO SUSPEND RULES ON WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 1997

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
    PERMISSION FOR SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTION TO SUSPEND RULES ON 
                      WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 1997

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order on Wednesday, February 5, 1997, for the Speaker to entertain a 
motion to suspend the rules and pass a bill or resolution relating to

gentleman from South Dakota?
```
