# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Rogan, James E. (R000386) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 35

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. ROGAN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 35, I move that 
the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable Frank Tejeda.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 30 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 35, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, February 5, 1997, at 11 a.m., in memory of the 
late Honorable Frank Tejeda of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
