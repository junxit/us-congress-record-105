# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 43

```
          ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 43) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 43

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and he is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives: Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Mr. 
     LaHood.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
