# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 161(a) of the Trade Act

[[Page H396]]

of 1974, (19 U.S.C. 2211), and upon the recommendation of the chairman 
of the Committee on Ways and Means, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
selection of the following members of that committee to be accredited 
by the President as official advisers to the U.S. delegations to 
international conferences, meetings and negotiation sessions relating 
to trade agreements during the first session of the 105th Congress:
  Mr. Archer of Texas; Mr. Crane of Illionis; Mr. Thomas of California; 
Mr. Rangel of New York; and Mr. Matsui of California.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
