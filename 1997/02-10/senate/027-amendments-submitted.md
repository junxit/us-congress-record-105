# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 1

```
                          AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

                                 ______
                                 

              THE BALANCED BUDGET CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                       WELLSTONE AMENDMENT NO. 3

  Mr. WELLSTONE proposed an amendment to the joint resolution (S.J. 
Res. 1) proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United States 
to require a balanced budget; as follows:

       Redesignate section 8 as section 9 and after section 7 add 
     the following:
       ``Section 8. It is the policy of the United States that, in 
     achieving a balanced budget, Federal outlays must not be 
     reduced in a manner that disproportionately affects outlays 
     for education, nutrition, and health programs for poor 
     children.''
```
