# READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, notwithstanding 
the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, that on Monday, February 
24, 1997, immediately following the prayer and the disposition of the 
Journal, the traditional reading of Washington's Farewell Address take 
place and that the Chair be authorized to appoint a Senator to perform 
that task.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
