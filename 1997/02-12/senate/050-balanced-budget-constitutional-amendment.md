# BALANCED BUDGET CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 1

```
                BALANCED BUDGET CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          BYRD AMENDMENT NO. 6

  Mr. BYRD proposed an amendment to the joint resolution (S.J. Res. 1) 
proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United States to 
require a balanced budget; as follows:

       On page 3, strike lines 12 through 14 and insert the 
     following:
       ``Section 6. The Congress shall implement this article by 
     appropriate legislation.

                          ____________________
```
