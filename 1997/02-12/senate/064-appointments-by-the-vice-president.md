# APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to Public Law 94-304, as amended by Public Law 99-7, appoints 
the Senator from New York [Mr. D'Amato] as Chairman of the Commission 
on Security and Cooperation in Europe.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, as modified by 
the order of February 10, 1997, appoints the Senator from Tennessee 
[Mr. Frist] to read Washington's Farewell Address on Monday, February 
24, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
