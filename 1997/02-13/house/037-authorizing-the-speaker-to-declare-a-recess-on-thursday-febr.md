# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 1997, TO RECEIVE HIS EXCELLENCY EDUARDO FREI, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF CHILE

**Speaking:** Knollenberg, Joe (K000288) [R-MI]

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 
1997, TO RECEIVE HIS EXCELLENCY EDUARDO FREI, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC 
                                OF CHILE

  Mr. KNOLLENBERG. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be 
in order anytime on Thursday, February 27, 1997, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting his Excellency, Eduardo Frei, President of 
the Republic of Chile.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
