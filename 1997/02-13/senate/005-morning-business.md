# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Roberts). Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 
p.m. The time between 11 and 12 noon shall be under the control of 
Senator Thomas or his designee.

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I believe we have special order time 
scheduled from 11 to 12 noon. I would designate the time allocated to 
Senator Thomas to me.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
