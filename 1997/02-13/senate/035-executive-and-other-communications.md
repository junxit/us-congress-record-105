                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, which were referred

     transmitting, pursuant to law, Revenue Procedure 97-17

     Forestry.
       EC-1096. A communication from the Assistant Secretary of

     the Committee on Foreign Relations.
       EC-1097. A communication from the Assistant Secretary of

       EC-1098. A communication from the Managing Director of the

       EC-1100. A communication from the Director of the Office of

       EC-1101. A communication from the Director of the Office of 
     Congressional Affairs, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission,

       EC-1102. A communication from the Director of the Office of
