# APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
               APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Public Law 96-388, as amended by Public Law 97-84, 
appoints the following Senators to the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Council: 
The Senator from California [Mrs. Boxer], vice the former Senator from 
Rhode Island, Mr. Pell, and the Senator from New Jersey [Mr. 
Lautenberg].
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to Public 
Law 96-388, as amended by Public Law 97-84, reappoints the following 
Senators to the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Council: The Senator from Utah 
[Mr. Hatch], the Senator from Iowa [Mr. Grassley], and the Senator from 
Alaska [Mr. Murkowski].

                          ____________________
```
