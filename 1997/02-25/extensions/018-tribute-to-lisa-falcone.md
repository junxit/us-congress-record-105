# TRIBUTE TO LISA FALCONE

**Speaking:** Towns, Edolphus (T000326) [D-NY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LISA FALCONE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EDOLPHUS TOWNS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 25, 1997

  Mr. TOWNS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Lisa Falcone, a 
native of Brooklyn, N.Y. who has been dedicated to public service.

[[Page E304]]

  Presently, Lisa works for New York State Senator John Sampson, and 
serves as his district director. Formerly, she was employed in my 
office and that of former Congressman Stephen Solarz.
  Lisa distinguished herself as a student at Queens College where she 
received a bachelor of arts degree in English. A life-long resident of 
Carnarsie, she has been dedicated to public and community service.
  As a devoted mother, wife, and daughter, I am happy to acknowledge 
Lisa Falcone for her tireless efforts as a public servant.

                          ____________________
```
