# APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 10 U.S.C. 6968(a), appoints the Senator from Arizona [Mr. 
McCain], from the Committee on Armed Services, to the Board of Visitors 
of the U.S. Naval Academy.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to 10 U.S.C. 
9355(a), appoints the Senator from Idaho [Mr. Kempthorne], from the 
Committee on Armed Services, to the Board of the U.S. Air Force 
Academy.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to 10 U.S.C. 
4355(a), appoints the Senator from Indiana [Mr. Coats], from the 
Committee on Armed Services, to the Board of Visitors of the U.S. 
Military Academy.

                          ____________________
```
