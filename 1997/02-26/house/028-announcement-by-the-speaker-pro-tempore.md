# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore [Mr. Shays]. Pursuant to the provisions of 
clause 5, rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a minimum 
of 5 minutes the period of time within which a vote by electronic 
device may be taken on each additional motion to suspend the rules on 
which the Chair has postponed further proceedings.
```
