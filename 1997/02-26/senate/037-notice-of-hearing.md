# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet in SR-301, Russell Senate Office 
Building, on Wednesday, March 5, 1997, at 9:30 a.m. to hold an 
oversight hearing to review the budget and operations of the Secretary 
of the Senate, Sergeant at Arms, Architect of the Capitol, and the 
National Gallery of Art.
  For further information concerning this hearing, please contact Ed 
Edens of the committee staff on 224-6678.
```
