# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 1

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COATS. For the information of all Senators, by previous order, 
tomorrow morning the Senate will begin 90 minutes of debate on the 
Graham amendment. Following the vote on or in relation to the Graham 
amendment, the Senate will then debate several amendments to the 
balanced budget amendment with rollcall votes occurring on those 
amendments later in the afternoon. Following those stacked votes later 
in the afternoon, the Senate will debate Senator Bumper's amendment to 
Senate Joint Resolution 1. Therefore, all members can expect additional 
votes during Thursday's session.

                          ____________________
```
