# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 211, S. 363

```
[[Page S1800]]



                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 211

  At the request of Mr. Wellstone, the name of the Senator from Iowa 
[Mr. Harkin] was added as a cosponsor of S. 211, a bill to amend title 
38, United States Code, to extend the period of time for the 
manifestation of chronic disabilities due to undiagnosed symptoms in 
veterans who served in the Persian Gulf war in order for those 
disabilities to be compensable by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.


                                 S. 363

  At the request of Mr. Hollings, the name of the Senator from Kentucky 
[Mr. Ford] was added as a cosponsor of S. 363, a bill to amend the 
Communications Act of 1934 to require that violent video programming is 
limited to broadcast after the hours when children are reasonably 
likely to comprise a substantial portion of the audience, unless it is 
specifically rated on the basis of its violent content so that it is 
blockable by electronic means specifically on the basis of that 
content.
```
