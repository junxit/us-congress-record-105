# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
              RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Hutchinson). The acting Democratic leader.
  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I concur with what the majority leader said 
about being on the floor for the vote. I concur. I think it is an 
extremely important one. Also, under our unanimous-consent agreement, I 
will be controlling the time for the Democratic side. A number of 
Senators on our side have asked for specific carve-outs of time other 
than what has been set in the unanimous consent. I urge Senators who 
wish to speak to come to the floor and be prepared to speak.
  I see my distinguished friend from Utah, who will be handling that 
side. We have all been able to work things out as traffic cops on this, 
but I hope everyone who wishes to speak will have the opportunity.
  I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
