# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 5

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I 
announce that following morning business today, the Senate will begin 
consideration of Senate Joint Resolution 5, the waiver resolution for 
the Barshefsky nomination, at 1 p.m. Under a previous order, there will 
be 3 hours of debate on the Hollings amendment and 1 hour of debate 
following the resolution itself. Following disposition of the amendment 
and the resolution, the Senate will proceed to a vote on the nomination 
of Charlene Barshefsky, to be U.S. Trade Representative. Therefore, 
Senators can expect several rollcall votes throughout Wednesday's 
session of the Senate.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
