# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the order, there will now be a period 
for the transaction of morning business, not to extend beyond 1:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for 5 minutes.
  Under the order, the Senator from Ohio [Mr. DeWine] is recognized to 
speak for up to 20 minutes.
```
