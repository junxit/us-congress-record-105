# REPORT CONCERNING THE TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 21

```
  REPORT CONCERNING THE TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND THE TRADE AGREEMENTS 
               PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 21

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1997 Trade Policy Agenda and 1996 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 6, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
