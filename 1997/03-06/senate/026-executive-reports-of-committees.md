                    EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following executive report of committees was submitted:

     Resources:
       Federico Pena, of Colorado, to be Secretary of Energy.

       Merrick B. Garland, of Maryland, to be United States 
     Circuit Judge for the District of Columbia Circuit.
       Rose Ochi, of California, to be Director, Community 
     Relations Service, for a term of four years.
       Colleen Kollar-Kotelly, of the District of Columbia, to be 
     United States District Judge for the District of Columbia.
       Lyle Weir Swenson, of South Dakota, to be United States 
     Marshal for the District of South Dakota for the term of four 
     years.

  (The above nominations were reported with the recommendation that 
they be confirmed.)
