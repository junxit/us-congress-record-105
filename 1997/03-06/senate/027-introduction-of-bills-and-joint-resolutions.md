# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 409, S. 410, S. 411

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. COATS:
       S. 409. A bill to amend the Communications Act of 1934 to 
     provide for the implementation of systems for rating the 
     specific content of specific television programs; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. D'AMATO (for himself, Mr. Gramm, Mr. Sarbanes, 
             and Mr. Dodd):
       S. 410. A bill to extend the effective date of the 
     Investment Advisers Supervision Coodination Act; to the 
     Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
           By Mrs. HUTCHISON (for herself, Mr. Abraham, Mr. 
             Campbell, Mr. D'Amato, Ms. Moseley-Braun, and Mr. 
             Specter):
       S. 411. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide a tax credit for investment necessary to 
     revitalize communities within the United States, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
