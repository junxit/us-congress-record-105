**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on the Executive Calendar: Calendar Nos. 27 through 33, and 
all nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, 
Marine Corps, and Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, 
that the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, that any 
statements relating to the nominations appear at this point in the 
Record, that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and that the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
