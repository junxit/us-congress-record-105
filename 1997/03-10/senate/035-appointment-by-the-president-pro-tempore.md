# APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
                APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, upon the recommendation of the Democratic leader, pursuant to 
22 U.S.C. 2761, appoints the Senator from West Virginia [Mr. Byrd] as 
vice chairman of the Senate delegation to the British-American 
Interparliamentary Group during the 105th Congress.
```
