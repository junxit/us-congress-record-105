# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 852, PAPERWORK ELIMINATION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 88, H.R. 852

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 852, PAPERWORK 
                        ELIMINATION ACT OF 1997

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-15) on the resolution (H.Res. 88) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 852) to amend chapter 35 of title 44, 
United States Code, popularly known as the Paperwork Reduction Act, to 
minimize the burden of Federal paperwork demands upon small businesses, 
educational and nonprofit institutions, Federal contractors, State and 
local governments, and other persons through the sponsorship and use of 
alternative information technologies, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
