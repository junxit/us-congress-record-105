# GRANTING CONSENT TO CERTAIN AMENDMENTS ENACTED BY HAWAIIAN LEGISLATURE TO HAWAIIAN HOMES COMMISSION ACT OF 1920

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 32

```
GRANTING CONSENT TO CERTAIN AMENDMENTS ENACTED BY HAWAIIAN LEGISLATURE 
                TO HAWAIIAN HOMES COMMISSION ACT OF 1920

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is the question de novo 
of suspending the rules and passing the joint resolution, House Joint 
Resolution 32.
  The Clerk read the title of the joint resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from California [Mr. Doolittle] that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the joint resolution, House Joint Resolution 32.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the joint resolution was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
