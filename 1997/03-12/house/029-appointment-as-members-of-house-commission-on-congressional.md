# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF HOUSE COMMISSION ON CONGRESSIONAL MAILING STANDARDS

```
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF HOUSE COMMISSION ON CONGRESSIONAL MAILING 
                               STANDARDS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 5(b) of Public Law 93-191, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the House to the 
Commission on Congressional Mailing Standards:
  Mr. Thomas of California, chairman;
  Mr. Ney of Ohio,
  Mr. Boehner of Ohio,
  Mr. Hoyer of Maryland,
  Mr. Clay of Missouri, and
  Mr. Frost of Texas.

[[Page H924]]

  There was no objection.
```
