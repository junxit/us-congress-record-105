# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Levin, Sander M. (L000263) [D-MI]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SANDER M. LEVIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 13, 1997

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to indicate that on Thursday, March 6, 
I accompanied the President of the United States to my home State of 
Michigan where he discussed education and the challenge of moving 
people from welfare to work.
  As a result, I missed rollcall votes 32 through 35. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``nay'' on rollcall votes 32 and 35, and 
``yea'' on rollcall votes 33 and 34.

                          ____________________
```
