# APPOINTMENT TO NATIONAL COMMITTEE ON VITAL AND HEALTH STATISTICS

```
    APPOINTMENT TO NATIONAL COMMITTEE ON VITAL AND HEALTH STATISTICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 306(k)(3)(a) of the Public Health Service Act (42 
U.S.C. 242k(k)), as amended by section 263 of Public Law 104-191, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of Mr. Jeffrey S. Blair of 
Atlanta, Georgia to the National Committee on Vital and Health 
Statistics on the part of the House.
  There was no objection.
```
