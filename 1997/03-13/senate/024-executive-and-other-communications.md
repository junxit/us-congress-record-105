                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

       EC-1410. A communication from the General Counsel of the

       EC-1411. A communication from the Assistant Secretary of

     the report of a memorandum of justification and a schedule of 
     proposed obligations; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
       EC-1412. A communication from the Assistant Attorney 
     General, transmitting, a draft of proposed legislation 
     entitled ``The Saving Law Enforcement Officers' Lives Act of

       EC-1413. A communication from the Managing Director of the

     law, seven rules received on March 11, 1997; to the Committee

       EC-1414. A communication from the Administrator of the
