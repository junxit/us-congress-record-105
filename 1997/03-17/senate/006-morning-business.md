# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 1 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each. Under the previous order, there will be 30 minutes under 
the control of the Senator from Wyoming [Mr. Thomas]. The Senator from 
Wyoming is recognized.
  Mr. THOMAS. I thank the Chair.
```
