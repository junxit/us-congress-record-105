# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Friday, March 14, 
1997, the Federal debt stood at $5,362,748,754,102.53.
  One year ago, March 14, 1996, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,035,166,000,000.
  Twenty-five years ago, March 14, 1972, the Federal debt stood at 
$428,412,000,000 which reflects a debt increase of nearly $5 trillion-- 
$4,934,336,754,102.53--during the past 25 years.

                          ____________________
```
