# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1, WORKING FAMILIES FLEXIBILITY ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.Res. 99

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1, WORKING 
                    FAMILIES FLEXIBILITY ACT OF 1997

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-31) on the resolution (H. Res. 99) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1) to amend the Fair

in the private sector, which was referred to the House Calendar and
```
