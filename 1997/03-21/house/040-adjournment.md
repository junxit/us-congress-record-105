# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Saxton, Jim (S000097) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** S. 14

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SAXTON. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 14, 
105th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hayworth). Pursuant to the provisions of 
Senate Concurrent Resolution 14, 105th Congress, the House stands 
adjourned until 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 8, 1997, for morning hour 
debates.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 1 minute p.m.), pursuant to Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 14, the House adjourned until Tuesday, April 8, 
1997, at 12:30 p.m. for morning hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
