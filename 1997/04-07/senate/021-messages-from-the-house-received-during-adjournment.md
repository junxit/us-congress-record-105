# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 14, S. 410, H.R. 514

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on March 21, 1997, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House has agreed to the following concurrent 
resolution, with amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 14. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate the House of 
     Representatives.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on March 21, 1997, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bills:

       H.R. 514. An act to permit the waiver of the District of 
     Columbia residency requirements for certain employees of the 
     Office of the Inspector General of the District of Columbia.
       S. 410. An act to extend the effective date of the 
     Investment Advisers Supervision Coordination Act; to the 
     Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the enrolled bills were signed on March 21, 1997, during the 
adjournment of the Senate by the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].

                          ____________________
```
