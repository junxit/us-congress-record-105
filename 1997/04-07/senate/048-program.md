# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 104

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow, following the party conferences, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 104, the Nuclear Policy 
Act. By previous consent, a cloture vote on the motion to proceed to S. 
104 will occur at 5:15 p.m., Tuesday afternoon. In addition, the time 
between 2:15 and 5:15 has been set aside for debate on the motion, with 
time equally divided between the proponents and opponents of the 
legislation.
```
