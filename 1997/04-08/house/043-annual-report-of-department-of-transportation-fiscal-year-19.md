# ANNUAL REPORT OF DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, FISCAL YEAR 1995-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
   ANNUAL REPORT OF DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, FISCAL YEAR 1995--
            MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 308 of Public Law 97-449 (49 U.S.C. 308(a)), I 
transmit herewith the Annual Report of the Department of 
Transportation, which covers fiscal year 1995.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, April 8, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
