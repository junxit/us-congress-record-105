# EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

**Speaking:** Pomeroy, Earl (P000422) [D-ND]

```
                         EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. EARL POMEROY

                            of north dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 1997

  Mr. POMEROY. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I was in North Dakota 
participating in the emergency relief efforts that are underway to help 
the victims of the latest winter storm to hit the Upper Great Plains. 
As a result, I was absent for rollcall votes No. 72 and 73. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``aye'' on both measures.

                          ____________________
```
