# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--SENATE RESOLUTION 70

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S.Res. 70

```
            UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--SENATE RESOLUTION 70

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the immediate consideration of Senate Resolution 70, submitted 
earlier today by Senator Daschle and others, that the resolution and 
preamble be agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table en bloc. Further, that any statements relating thereto be placed 
in the Record at the appropriate place.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, I object at this time on 
behalf of some Members on our side of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
