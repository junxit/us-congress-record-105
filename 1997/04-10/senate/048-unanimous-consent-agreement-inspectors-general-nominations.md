# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--INSPECTORS GENERAL NOMINATIONS

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

```
      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--INSPECTORS GENERAL NOMINATIONS

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, as in executive session, I 
ask unanimous consent that nominations to the Office of Inspector 
General, excepting the Office of Inspector General for the Central 
Intelligence Agency, be referred during the 105th Congress in each case 
to the committee having substantive jurisdiction over the department, 
agency or entity, and if and when reported in each case, then to the 
Committee on Governmental Affairs for not to exceed 20 calendar days.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
