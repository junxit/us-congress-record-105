# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 62, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 62, H.Res. 113

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT 
         RESOLUTION 62, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-54) on the resolution (H. Res. 113) 
providing for consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 62) 
proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United States with 
respect to tax limitations, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
