# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT], Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      subcommittee on immigration

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, there will be a hearing held by the 
Subcommittee on Immigration, Senate Committee on the Judiciary, on 
Tuesday, April 15, 1997, at 10:30 a.m., in room 226, Senate Dirksen 
Building, on ``Immigrant Entrepreneurs, Job Creation, and the American 
Dream.''


           committee on agriculture, nutrition, and forestry

  Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry will hold a full 
committee hearing on Thursday, April 24, 1997, at 9 a.m. in SR-328A to 
receive testimony regarding U.S. agricultural exports.
```
