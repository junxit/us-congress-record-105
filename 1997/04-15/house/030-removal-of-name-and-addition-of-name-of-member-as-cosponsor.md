# REMOVAL OF NAME AND ADDITION OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1200

**Speaking:** McDermott, Jim (M000404) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1200

```
  REMOVAL OF NAME AND ADDITION OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 
                                  1200

  Mr. McDERMOTT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to remove the 
gentleman from Oklahoma, Mr. J.C. Watts, as a cosponsor of H.R. 1200 
and to add the name of the gentleman from North Carolina, Mr. Mel Watt, 
to the bill. I inadvertently got the wrong name.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Washington?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
