# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 495

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow, following the period of morning business, it is hoped that 
the Senate will be able to begin consideration of S. 495. That bill, 
which will be discharged from the Judiciary Committee, is regarding the 
unlawful use or transfer of chemical weapons. It is hoped that we will 
be able to reach an agreement on that bill which would allow the Senate 
to complete action of S. 495 following a couple of hours of debate. All 
Senators can therefore expect rollcall votes on Thursday, possibly mid 
to late afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
