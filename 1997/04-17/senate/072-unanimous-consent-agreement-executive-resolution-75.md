# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE RESOLUTION 75

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

```
          UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE RESOLUTION 75

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, as in executive 
session, that if executive resolution 75 is defeated, the Senate then 
agree to the motion to reconsider that vote and the resolution then be 
pending once again.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
