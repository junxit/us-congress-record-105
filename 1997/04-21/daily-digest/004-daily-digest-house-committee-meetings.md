# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--DEBT COLLECTION IMPROVEMENT ACT
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: On April 18, the 
Subcommittee on Government Management, Information, and Technology held 
a hearing on ``Oversight of the Debt Collection Improvement Act''. 
Testimony was heard from the following officials of the Department of 
the Treasury: Lawrence Summers, Deputy Secretary; Jerry Hawke, Under 
Secretary; and Gerald Murphy, Assistant Fiscal Secretary; John 
Koskinen, Deputy Director, OMB; Ted David, Chief Financial Officer, 
USDA; Steven McNamara, Assistant Inspector General, Audit, Department 
of Education; D. Mark Catlett, Chief, Financial Officer, Department of 
Veterans Affairs; and Anne Donovan, Office of Child Support 
Enforcement, Department of Health and Human Services.
```
