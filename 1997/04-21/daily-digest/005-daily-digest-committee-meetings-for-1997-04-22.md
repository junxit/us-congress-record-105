# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1997-04-22

```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                             APRIL 22, 1997

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on VA, HUD, and 
Independent Agencies, to hold hearings on proposed budget estimates

Office of Science and Technology Policy, 9:30 a.m., SD-192.

  Committee on Armed Services, Subcommittee on SeaPower, to resume 
hearings on S. 450, the National Defense Authorization Act for

222.

  Committee on Indian Affairs, to hold hearings on S. 459, to 
authorize funds for and extend the Native American Programs Act of 
1974, 9:30 a.m., SR-485.

                                 House
```
