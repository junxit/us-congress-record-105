# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, APRIL 22, 1997

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, APRIL 22, 1997

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in adjournment until the hour of 10 a.m. on Tuesday, April 22, 
1997.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 20 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Tuesday, April 22, 1997, at 10 a.m.
```
