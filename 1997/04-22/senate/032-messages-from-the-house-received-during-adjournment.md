# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1003

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 18, 1997, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1003. An act to clarify Federal law with respect to 
     restricting the use of Federal funds in support of assisted 
     suicide.


[[Page S3418]]


  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the enrolled bill was signed on April 21, 1997, during the adjournment 
of the Senate by the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].

                          ____________________
```
