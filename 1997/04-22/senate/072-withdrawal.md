# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on April 
18, 1997, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                          central intelligence

       I withdraw the nomination of Anthony Lake, of 
     Massachusetts, to be Director of Central Intelligence, vice 
     John M. Deutch, resigned, which was sent to the Senate on 
     January 9, 1997.
```
