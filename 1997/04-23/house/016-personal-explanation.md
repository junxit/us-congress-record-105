# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Smith, Robert (S000607) [R-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 400

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. SMITH of Oregon. Mr. Speaker, unfortunately, I was unable to be 
present during consideration of H.R. 400 today. As a cosponsor of this 
bill, however, I feel it is important for me to let my intentions be 
known on this important matter. Therefore, I would like to state for 
the Record that, had I been present, I would have voted against all of 
the amendment to H.R. 400 and supported final passage of the bill.
```
