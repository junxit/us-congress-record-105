# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1271, FAA RESEARCH, ENGINEERING, AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 125, H.R. 1271

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1271, FAA 
    RESEARCH, ENGINEERING, AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1997

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-70) on the resolution (H. Res. 125) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1271) to authorize the Federal Aviation 
Administration's research, engineering, and development programs for 
fiscal years 1998 through 2000, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
