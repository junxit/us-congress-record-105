# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1274, NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF STANDARDS AND TECHNOLOGY AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 127, H.R. 1274

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1274, NATIONAL 
    INSTITUTE OF STANDARDS AND TECHNOLOGY AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1997

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-72) on the resolution (H. Res. 127) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1274) to authorize appropriations for

1998 and 1999, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House
```
