# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I have a unanimous-consent request that has 
been cleared on both sides. I ask unanimous consent that 1 hour of the 
2 hours devoted to the closed session not be counted against the 10-
hour debate time as provided in the consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
