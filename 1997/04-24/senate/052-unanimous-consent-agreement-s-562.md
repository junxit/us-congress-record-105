# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 562

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 562

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 562

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 11:30 
a.m., on Friday, April 25, the Banking Committee be discharged from 
further consideration of S. 562 and the Senate then immediately begin 
consideration under the following limitation: 1 hour for debate on the 
bill equally divided in the usual form, there will be no amendments in 
order to the bill, and following the conclusion or yielding back of 
time the bill will be read for a third time with no intervening action 
or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
