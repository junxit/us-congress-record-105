# CHILDREN OF ST. ANN

**Speaking:** Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN]

```
                          CHILDREN OF ST. ANN

  Mr. WELLSTONE. First of all, Mr. President, I would like to welcome, 
so that this would be part of the Congressional Record, the really 
beautiful children of St. Ann Episcopal Church in the South Bronx who 
came here yesterday for a visit. These are wonderful children. They 
were written about in Jonathan Kozol's book ``Amazing Grace: The Lives 
of Children in the Conscience of a Nation.'' I wish to let those 
children know that it was really wonderful to have them here in the 
Nation's Capitol and I am sure that someday some of them will serve in 
the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
