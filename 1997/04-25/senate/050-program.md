# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 543

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, for the information of all 
Senators, following the period of morning business on Monday, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 543, 
the Volunteer Protection Act. Under the order previously agreed to, 
there will be a cloture vote on the motion to proceed to S. 543 at 2:15 
p.m. on Tuesday, April 29. There will be no rollcall votes on Monday, 
April 28. However, the Senate will continue to debate the motion to 
proceed to the volunteer protection legislation.
```
