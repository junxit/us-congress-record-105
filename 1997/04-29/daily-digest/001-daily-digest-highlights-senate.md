                                            Tuesday, April 29, 1997

[[Page D399]]

                              Daily Digest


HIGHLIGHTS

      The House passed S. 305, to authorize the President to award a 
      gold medal on behalf of the Congress to Francis Albert ``Frank'' 
      Sinatra--clearing the measure for the President.
      The House passed H.R. 1271, to authorize the Federal Aviation 
      Administration's research, engineering, and development programs 
      for fiscal years 1998 through 2000.
