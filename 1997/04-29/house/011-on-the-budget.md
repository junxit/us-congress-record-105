# ON THE BUDGET

**Speaking:** Bliley, Tom (B000556) [R-VA]

```
                             ON THE BUDGET

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Burton of Indiana). Under the Speaker's 
announced policy of January 21, 1997, the gentleman from Virginia [Mr. 
Bliley] is recognized during morning hour debates for 1 minute.
  Mr. BLILEY. Mr. Speaker, I would say to the gentleman from Maine who 
preceded me in the well that I appreciate his remarks. It is time that 
we get moving on the budget and that we reach agreement.
  But I would suggest firmly that he address his comments to his 
leadership in both bodies who have criticized the President recently 
for his willingness to work with the Republicans and to reach 
compromise. I think that would be more productive.

                          ____________________
```
