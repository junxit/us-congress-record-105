# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 867, ADOPTION PROMOTION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 134, H.R. 867

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 867, ADOPTION 
                         PROMOTION ACT OF 1997

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio (during the special order of the gentlewoman from 
Maryland, Mrs. Morella) from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-82) on the resolution (H. Res. 134)
```
