# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSE OVERSIGHT

**Speaking:** Barrett, Bill (B000179) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.Res. 137

```
           ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSE OVERSIGHT

  Mr. BARRETT of Nebraska. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican 
conference, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 137) and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 137

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and he is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives: Committee on House Oversight: Mr. 
     Mica.

  The resolution was agreed to. A motion to reconsider was laid on the 
table.

                          ____________________
```
