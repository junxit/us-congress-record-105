# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 543

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will immediately 
resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 543, the Volunteer 
Protection Act. By previous order, at 11:15 a.m. the Senate will vote 
on the second cloture motion to proceed to this bill, S. 543. As a 
reminder to all Senators, if that cloture vote fails, two additional 
cloture motions were filed last night and would be voted on, on 
Thursday. It is still my hope that the Senate will be allowed to move 
forward and begin consideration of this important legislation. In 
addition, the Senate could also be asked to turn to any other 
Legislative or Executive Calendar items that may be cleared.

                          ____________________
```
