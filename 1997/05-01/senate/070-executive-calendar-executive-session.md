**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on the Executive Calendar: Calendar Nos. 52 through 60, and 
all nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, 
Marine Corps, and Navy.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that the 
nominations be confirmed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, any statements relating to the nominations appear at the 
appropriate place in the Record, the President be immediately notified 
of the Senate's action, and the Senate then return to legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
