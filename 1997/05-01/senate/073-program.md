# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will not 
be in session on Friday of this week in order to accommodate a meeting 
scheduled for the Democratic Members of the Senate. The Senate will 
reconvene on Monday at 12 noon. After a period of morning business, at 
1 p.m., the Senate will begin consideration of the supplemental 
appropriations bill. Amendments are expected to be offered on Monday. 
Therefore, votes could occur, but will not occur prior to the hour of 5 
p.m., on Monday. We will notify Members as early as possible on Monday 
with respect to rollcall votes occurring on that day.
  The Senate could also be asked to turn to any other Legislative or 
Executive Calendar items that can be cleared for action on Monday.

                          ____________________
```
