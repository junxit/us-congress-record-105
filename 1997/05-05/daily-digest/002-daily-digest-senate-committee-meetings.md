# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

NEW MEXICO STATEHOOD AMENDMENTS
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: Committee concluded hearings 
on S. 430, to protect the permanent trust funds of the State of New 
Mexico from erosion due to inflation and modify the basis on which 
distributions are made from those funds, after receiving testimony from 
Phil Archibeck, New Mexico State Investment Council, Santa Fe.
