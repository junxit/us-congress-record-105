# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S. 672

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on S. 672, the 
     supplemental appropriations bill:
         Trent Lott, Ted Stevens, Mike DeWine, Bob Bennett, Tim 
           Hutchinson, Richard G. Lugar, Pete Domenici, Pat 
           Roberts, Connie Mack, Frank H. Murkowski, Richard 
           Shelby, Craig Thomas, Chuck Grassley, Christopher S. 
           Bond, Michael B. Enzi, Jeff Sessions.

                          ____________________
```
