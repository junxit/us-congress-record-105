# SEAN J. WHITE

**Speaking:** Lieberman, Joseph I. (L000304) [D-CT]

```
                             SEAN J. WHITE

● Mr. LIEBERMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to acknowledge Sean 
J. White. Sean has been a member of the King & Low-Heywood Thomas 
School [KLHT] community since his freshman year. That same year he was 
elected treasurer of the student government. He also served as a 
ranking member of the Constitution Committee. Mr. White was a member of 
the school newspaper staff and became editor-in-chief of The Standard 
in 1997. He has been an active member of Model United Nations and 
Political Union, as the vice chairman. At the end of this year his term 
as chairman of the Political Union and as president of Model United 
Nations will begin.●

                          ____________________
```
