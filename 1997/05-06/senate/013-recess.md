# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
p.m., having arrived, the Senate will now stand in recess until the 
hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:31 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Coats).
```
