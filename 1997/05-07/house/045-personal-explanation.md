# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 93

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, earlier today I was detained on 
official business and unfortunately was unable to cast my vote on House 
Resolution 93. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on this 
resolution, which

[[Page H2265]]

expresses the sense of the House that if any adjustment is made to the 
consumer price index that it should be made by the Bureau of Labor 
Statistics.
```
