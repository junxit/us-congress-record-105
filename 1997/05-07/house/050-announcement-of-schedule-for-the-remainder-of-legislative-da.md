# ANNOUNCEMENT OF SCHEDULE FOR THE REMAINDER OF LEGISLATIVE DAY

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

```
     ANNOUNCEMENT OF SCHEDULE FOR THE REMAINDER OF LEGISLATIVE DAY

  (Mr. SOLOMON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, I have an announcement to make.
  The bill that was just on the floor has been pulled, and we are about 
to take up a rule on the Juvenile Crime Control Act. There will be 
about a 45-minute vote on it, and then that will be the last vote of 
the night. In the meantime those that are on the floor now, they are 
welcome to leave or take seats so that we can take up this last matter 
before the House today.

                          ____________________
```
