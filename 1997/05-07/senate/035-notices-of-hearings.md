# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet in SR-301, Russell Senate Office

on the Campaign Finance System for Presidential Elections: The Growth 
of Soft Money and Other Effects on Political Parties and Candidates.
  For further information concerning this hearing, please contact 
Stewart Verdery of the committee staff on 224-2204.

Finance Programs--Part II.'' The hearing will be held on May 15, 1997, 
beginning at 9:30 a.m. in room 428A of the Russell Senate Office 
Building.
  For further information, please contact Paul Cooksey at 224-5175.
```
