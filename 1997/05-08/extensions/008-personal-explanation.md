# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 93

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 8, 1997

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, when the House voted on House 
Resolution 93, expressing the sense of the Congress regarding the 
Consumer Price Index. I was unavoidably detained, and could not record 
my vote on this important resolution. The Consumer Price Index is 
appropriately monitored by the Bureau of Labor Statistics. I would like 
the record to reflect that I would have voted in the affirmative on 
this resolution.

                          ____________________
```
