# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 4

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT. Then, Mr. President, I thank my colleagues for their 
cooperation on the agreement we just reached on S. 4, and I now ask

permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each, with the exception of 
Senator Byrd, who will speak on Mother's Day.

it is so ordered.
```
