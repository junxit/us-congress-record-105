# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Ashcroft, John (A000356) [R-MO]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ASHCROFT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow Senators will speak on the subject of the flex time/comp time 
bill, the Family Friendly Workplace Act, until the hour of 12:30. 
However, no rollcall votes will occur during Friday's session of the 
Senate.
  On Monday the Senate will consider the IDEA legislation and/or the 
CFE Treaty. If an agreement can be reached for the consideration of the 
IDEA bill for Monday, then any votes ordered with respect to that bill 
would be stacked to occur on Tuesday. As always, all Senators will be 
notified when any votes are ordered.

                          ____________________
```
