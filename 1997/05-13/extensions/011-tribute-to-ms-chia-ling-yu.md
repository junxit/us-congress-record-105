# TRIBUTE TO MS. CHIA-LING YU

**Speaking:** Doyle, Michael F. (D000482) [D-PA]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MS. CHIA-LING YU

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL F. DOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 13, 1997

  Mr. DOYLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Chia-Ling Yu from 
Gateway High School. Chia-Ling is the top winner of the 1997 18th 
Congressional District High School Art Competition, An Artistic 
Discovery.
  Chia-Ling's artwork was chosen from an outstanding collection of 
entries. Her mixed-media portrait which is entitled ``Jonay'' 
illustrates her strong individualized style. She is a young woman of 
considerable talent sure to have many successes in her future.
  I look forward to seeing Chia-Ling's artwork displayed along with the 
artwork of the other competition winners from across our country. I am 
pleased to be associated with Chia-Ling's artistic talents.
  Congratulations, Chia-Ling. I wish you all the best of luck in the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
