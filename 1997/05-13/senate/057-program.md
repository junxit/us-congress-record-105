# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, for the information of all Members, 
tomorrow morning, the Senate will resume the IDEA bill under the 
earlier time agreement. All Senators can expect a series of three 
rollcall votes beginning at approximately 9:45 or 9:50 a.m. Senators 
should be prepared to be on the floor for the stacked votes beginning 
early Wednesday morning in that the second and third votes will be 
limited to 10 minutes in length. Following the votes and a short period 
for morning business, the Senate will begin consideration of the 
partial birth abortion ban. The Senate might also consider the CFE 
Treaty during Wednesday's session. As always, Senators will be notified 
as to when any additional votes are scheduled.

                          ____________________
```
