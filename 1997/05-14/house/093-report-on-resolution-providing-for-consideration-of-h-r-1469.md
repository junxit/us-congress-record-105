# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1469, EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS BILL FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 149, H.R. 1469

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1469, 
    EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS BILL FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

  Mr. McINNIS (during the special order of the gentleman from Illinois, 
Mr. Hastert) from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. 105-97) on the resolution (H. Res. 149) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1469) making emergency supplemental 
appropriations for recovery from natural disasters, and for overseas 
peacekeeping efforts, including those in Bosnia, for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1997, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
