# Text of House Amendment 17

**Speaking:** Goodling, William F. (G000291) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1469

```
H.R. 1469

                        Offered By: Mr. Goodling

       Amendment No. 17: Page 2, after line 23, insert the 
     following new section:


   prohibition of funds for national testing program in reading and 
                              mathematics

       Sec. 3003. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to develop, plan, implement, or administer any 
     national testing program in reading or mathematics.
```
