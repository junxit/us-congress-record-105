# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1385, EMPLOYMENT, TRAINING, AND LITERACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 150, H.R. 1385

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1385, 
       EMPLOYMENT, TRAINING, AND LITERACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1997

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-98) on the resolution (H. Res. 150) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1385) to consolidate, coordinate, and 
improve employment, training, literacy, and vocational rehabilitation 
programs in the United States, and for other purposes, which was
```
