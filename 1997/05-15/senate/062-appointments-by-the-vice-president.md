# APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 United States Code 276h-276k, as amended, appoints the 
Senator from Utah [Mr. Hatch], the Senator from Alabama [Mr. Shelby], 
and the Senator from Arizona [Mr. McCain] as members of the Senate 
Delegation to the Mexico-United States Interparliamentary Group meeting 
to beheld in Santa Fe, NM, May 16-18, 1997.
```
