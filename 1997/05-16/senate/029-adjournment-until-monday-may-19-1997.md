# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, MAY 19, 1997

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, MAY 19, 1997

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under 
the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:04 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, May 19, 1997, at 12 noon.
```
