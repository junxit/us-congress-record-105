# AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTIONS TO SUSPEND RULES ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 21, 1997

**Speaking:** Thomas, William M. (T000188) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 63, H.Res. 121, H.R. 911, H.R. 956, H.R. 1306, H.R. 1377

```
AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTIONS TO SUSPEND RULES ON WEDNESDAY, 
                              MAY 21, 1997

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that on Wednesday, 
May 21, 1997, the Speaker be authorized to entertain motions to suspend 
the rules and pass the following bills and resolutions:
  H.R. 1377, Savings Are Vital to Everyone's Retirement Act of 1997;
  H.R. 1306, Riegle-Neal Clarification Act of 1997;
  H.R. 911, Volunteer Protection Act of 1997;
  House Resolution 121, expressing the sense of the House of 
Representatives regarding the March 30, 1997, terrorist grenade attack 
in Cambodia;
  House Concurrent Resolution 63, reaffirming the commitment of the 
United States to the principles of the Marshall Plan;
  H.R. 956, Drug-Free Community Act.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
