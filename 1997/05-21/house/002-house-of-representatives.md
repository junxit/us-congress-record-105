# House of Representatives

```
[[Page H3071]]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

                        House of Representatives

  The House met at 9 a.m.
  The Chaplain, Reverend James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We know, O gracious God, that You have called all people to do the 
works of justice in our communities, our Nation, and in our World. On 
this day, we are especially aware of the contributions of those who 
have served in Government and have used their abilities and gifts in 
ways that have strengthened the common good, enhanced the security of 
every person, and have shown compassion for the neediest among us.
  We acknowledge the high honor that the citizens of this Nation have 
given them, and we pray that Your blessing will be with them now and in 
all the years to come. Amen.
```
