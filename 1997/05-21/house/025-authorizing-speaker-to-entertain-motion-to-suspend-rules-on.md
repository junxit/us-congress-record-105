# AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTION TO SUSPEND RULES ON THURSDAY, MAY 22, 1997

**Speaking:** Kolbe, Jim (K000306) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
 AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTION TO SUSPEND RULES ON THURSDAY, 
                              MAY 22, 1997

  Mr. KOLBE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, May 
22, 1997, the Speaker be authorized to entertain a motion to suspend 
the rules and pass H.R. 956, the Drug-Free Community Act.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Arizona?
  There was no objection.
```
