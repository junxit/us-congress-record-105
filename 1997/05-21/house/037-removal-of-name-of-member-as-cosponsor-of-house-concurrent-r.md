# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 65

**Speaking:** Moakley, John Joseph (M000834) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 65

```
 REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 
                                   65

  Mr. MOAKLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to remove the name 
of the gentleman from Colorado [Mr. Bob Schaffer]) as a cosponsor of 
House Concurrent Resolution 65. The name of gentleman from Colorado was 
inadvertently added by my staff. The correct name should have been the 
gentleman from Colorado [Mr. Dan Schaefer].
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
