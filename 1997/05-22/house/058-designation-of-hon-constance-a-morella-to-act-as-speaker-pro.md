# DESIGNATION OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 3, 1997

```
DESIGNATION OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
   TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 3, 1997

  The Speaker pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                     May 22, 1997.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Constance A. Morella to 
     act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through June 3, 1997.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the designation is 
accepted.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
