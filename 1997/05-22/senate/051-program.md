# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DOMENICI. For the information of all Senators, at 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow morning, the Senate will begin consideration of the resolution 
and begin a lengthy series of rollcall votes. And I cannot stress 
lengthy series of rollcall votes sufficiently. There will be a number 
of votes in order to complete action on the resolution. Senators should 
be prepared to remain on the Senate floor during that period to enable 
us to expedite this process to allow us to finish our business at a 
reasonable hour tomorrow. In addition, during Friday's session, the 
Senate will consider the CWC implementation bill, nominations that may 
be available on the Executive Calendar, and any other items that may be 
cleared for action.
```
