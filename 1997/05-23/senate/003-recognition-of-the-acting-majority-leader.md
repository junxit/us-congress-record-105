# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The able acting majority leader is 
recognized.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Thank you very much, Mr. President. I hope you are 
feeling well this morning, Mr. President.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Fine.
  Mr. DOMENICI. I am feeling well also because we are getting close to 
completion. If we could finish by 12:30 or so, I will feel even better.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. You are doing a good job.
  Mr. DOMENICI. None of that is supposed to occur in the Senate, but 
isn't that nice, that we could do that.

                          ____________________
```
