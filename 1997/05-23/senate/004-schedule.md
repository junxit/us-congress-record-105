# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** S. 27

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOMENICI. For the information of all Senators, today the Senate 
will immediately resume consideration of Senate Concurrent Resolution 
27, the first concurrent budget resolution. Under the previous order, 
all time is expired and the Senate will begin a lengthy series of 
rollcall votes on or in relation to the remaining pending amendments 
that are in order to the resolution. Therefore, Senators can expect to 
begin voting on numerous stacked votes momentarily. Senators are asked 
to remain in the Chamber and in their seats, if possible, to expedite 
this process.
  Again, all Members should be on the floor to begin this series of 
votes. After final passage of the budget resolution, it is the 
intention of the majority leader that the Senate consider the CWC 
implementation bill under the previous order, the supplemental 
appropriation bill, if the House completes action, and any nominations 
that have been cleared for action.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
