# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all 
remaining votes in the stacked sequence after the first amendment, the 
McCain amendment, be limited to 10 minutes each; and, further, there be 
2 minutes of debate, equally divided in the usual form, for each vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Hutchison). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
