# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair declares the 
House adjourned.
  There was no objection.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 3 minutes 
a.m.), under its previous order, the House adjourned until Friday, May 
30, 1997, at 10 a.m.
```
