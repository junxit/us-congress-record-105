# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND THEIR REMARKS IN THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD TODAY

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND THEIR REMARKS IN THE 
                       CONGRESSIONAL RECORD TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, on this day all Members 
are permitted to extend their remarks and to included extraneous 
material in that section of the Record entitled ``Extension of 
Remarks.''
  There was no objection.
```
