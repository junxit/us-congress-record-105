# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M. MONDAY, JUNE 2, 1997

```
             ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M. MONDAY, JUNE 2, 1997

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate now 
stands adjourned until 12 noon, Monday, June 2, 1997.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 o'clock and 14 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Monday, June 2, 1997, at 12 noon.
```
