# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 867

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 867

```
               MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 867

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 867 has arrived from 
the House.
  I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. DeWine). The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 867) to promote the adoption of children in 
     foster care.

  Mr. LOTT. I now ask for its second reading and will object to my own 
request in behalf of the other side of the aisle.
```
