                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

Kennedy Assassination Records Collection Act of 1992 to extend the 
authorization of the Assassination Records Review Board until 
September 30, 1998, 12:00 p.m., 2154 Rayburn.
  Committee on the Judiciary, oversight hearing on the Antitrust

  Subcommittee on Military Installations and Facilities, to mark up

  Committee on Science, Subcommittee on Space and Aeronautics, to

  Committee on Veterans' Affairs, hearing on H.R. 699, the Military

  Committee on Ways and Means, Subcommittee on Health, to mark up

Longworth.
