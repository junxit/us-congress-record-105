# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Miller of Florida). Will the gentleman 
from Arizona [Mr. Stump] come forward and lead the House in the Pledge 
of Allegiance.
  Mr. STUMP led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.
```
