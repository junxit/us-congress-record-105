# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                    select committee on intelligence

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Select 
Committee on Intelligence be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Wednesday, June 4, 1997, at 2:30 p.m. to hold a closed 
hearing on intelligence matters.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                        subcommittee on aviation

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Aviation 
Subcommittee of the Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation be authorized to meet on June 4, 1997, at 2 p.m., on 
bilateral aviation relations with the United Kingdom.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
