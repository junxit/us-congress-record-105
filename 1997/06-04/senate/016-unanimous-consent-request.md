# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                       UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
10 a.m. on Thursday, June 5, and that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer, the routine requests through the morning hour be 
granted.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I object.
  Mr. LOTT. I move that the Senate stand in adjournment, and I ask for 
the yeas and nays.
  Mr. DASCHLE. I note the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There is a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.

                          ____________________
```
