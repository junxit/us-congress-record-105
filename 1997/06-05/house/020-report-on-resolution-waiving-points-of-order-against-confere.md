REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT

RECOVERY FROM NATURAL DISASTERS, AND FOR OVERSEAS PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS,

1997, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar
