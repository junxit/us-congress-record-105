# VOTE ON MOTION TO ADJOURN

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK], Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
                       VOTE ON MOTION TO ADJOURN

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I move the Senate stand in adjournment until 
12 noon on Monday, June 9.
  Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There is a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion. The 
yeas and nays have been ordered. The clerk will call the roll.

Jeffords], the Senator from Pennsylvania

Lieberman] is necessarily absent.
```
