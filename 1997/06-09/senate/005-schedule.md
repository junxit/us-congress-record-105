# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Members, today the Senate will 
be in a period of morning business to allow a number of Senators time 
to speak. The Senate may also take up any executive or legislative 
business cleared for action. As previously announced, if any votes are 
ordered today, they would be set aside, not to occur before 5 p.m. As 
always, all Members will be notified if and when any votes are 
scheduled.
  I appreciate my colleagues' attention. I yield the floor.
```
