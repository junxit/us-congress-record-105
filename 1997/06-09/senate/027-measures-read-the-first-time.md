# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME

**Measures:** H.R. 908, H.R. 1000

```
                      MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME

  The following bills, previously received from the House of 
Representatives for the concurrence of the Senate, were read the first 
time:

       H.R. 908. An act to establish a Commission on Structural 
     Alternatives for the Federal Courts of Appeals.
       H.R. 1000. An act to require States to establish a system 
     to prevent prisoners from being considered part of any 
     household for purposes of determining eligibility of the 
     household for food stamp benefits and the amount of food 
     stamp benefits to be provided to the household under the Food 
     Stamp Act of 1977.
```
