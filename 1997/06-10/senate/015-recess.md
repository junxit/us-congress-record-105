# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 1 p.m. 
having arrived, the Senate will now be in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 1:05 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:16 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Coats).
```
