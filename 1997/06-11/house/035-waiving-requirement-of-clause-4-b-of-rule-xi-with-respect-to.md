# WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4(b) OF RULE XI WITH RESPECT TO SAME DAY CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 165

```
WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4(b) OF RULE XI WITH RESPECT TO SAME DAY 
                  CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

(Rept. No. 105-128) on the resolution (H. Res. 165) waiving a
```
