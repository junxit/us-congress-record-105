**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1757

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 1757, 
    FOREIGN RELATIONS AUTHORIZATION ACT, FISCAL YEARS 1998 AND 1999

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 1757, the Clerk be authorized to correct 
section numbers, punctuation, and cross references and to make such

the actions of the House in amending the bill, H.R. 1757.
```
