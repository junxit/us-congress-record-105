# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 12, 1997

   Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, June 11, 1997, I 
missed rollcall vote No. 178, as I was presenting an acknowledgment to 
students from the University of Illinois at Chicago who were 
participating in the Department of Energy's Future Car Challenge. I was 
unable to make it back to the Capitol in time to cast my vote. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 178.
```
