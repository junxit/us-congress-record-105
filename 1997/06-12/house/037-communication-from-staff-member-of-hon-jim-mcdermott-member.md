# COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF HON. JIM McDERMOTT, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF HON. JIM McDERMOTT, MEMBER OF 
                                CONGRESS

  The Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Taylor of North Carolina) laid before 
the House the following communication from Wilda E. Chisolm, staff 
member of the Hon. Jim McDermott, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, June 11, 1997.
     Hon. Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you pursuant 
     to Rule L (50) of the Rules of the House that I have been 
     served with a subpoena issued by the United States District 
     Court for the District of Columbia.
       After consultation with the General Counsel, I will make 
     the determinations required by Rule L.
           Sincerely,
     Wilda E. Chisolm.

                          ____________________
```
