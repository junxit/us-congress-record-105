# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Hutchinson, Tim (H001015) [R-AR]

**Measures:** S. 32

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
period of morning business be extended until the hour of 2 p.m. today, 
with Senators limited to 5 minutes each, with the following exceptions: 
Senator Graham, 30 minutes; Senator Dorgan, 15 minutes; Senator Lott or 
designee, 45 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, I thank the Chair.
  (The remarks of Mr. Hutchinson pertaining to the submission of Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 32 are located in today's Record under 
``Submission of Concurrent and Senate Resolutions.'')
  Mr. TORRICELLI addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Kyl). The Senator from New Jersey.

                          ____________________
```
