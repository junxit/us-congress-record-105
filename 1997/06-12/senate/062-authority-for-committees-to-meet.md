# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 450

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet in executive session to markup 
S. 450, the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Years 1998 
and 1999.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                     committee on foreign relations

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Thursday, June 12, 1997, at 2 p.m. to hold a markup/business 
meeting.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                   committee on governmental affairs

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent on behalf of the 
Governmental Affairs Committee to meet on Thursday, June 12, at 4 p.m. 
for a business meeting.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
