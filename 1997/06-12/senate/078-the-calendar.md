# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed en bloc to the immediate consideration of the following 
Calendar numbers on today's Legislative Calendar: Calendar Nos. 67 
through 73.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the bills be considered read a 
third time and passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to any of the bills be placed 
at the appropriate place in Record, with the preceding all done en 
bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
