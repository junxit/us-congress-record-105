# SAM NUNN FEDERAL CENTER

**Measures:** S. 347

```
                        SAM NUNN FEDERAL CENTER

  A bill (S. 347) to designate the Federal building located at 100 
Alabama Street NW, in Atlanta, GA, as the ``Sam Nunn Federal Center,'' 
was considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed; as follows:

                                 S. 347

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. DESIGNATION OF SAM NUNN FEDERAL CENTER.

       The Federal building located at 100 Alabama Street NW, in 
     Atlanta, Georgia, shall be known and designated as the ``Sam 
     Nunn Federal Center''.

     SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

       Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, 
     or other record of the United States to the Federal building 
     referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to 
     the ``Sam Nunn Federal Center''.

```
