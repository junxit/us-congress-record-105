# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Roemer, Tim (R000385) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1119

```
H.R. 1119

                         Offered By Mr. Roemer

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of title I (page 23, before 
     line 7), insert the following new section:

     SEC.  . INCREASE IN AMOUNT FOR HIGH-MOBILITY MULTIPURPOSE 
                   WHEELED VEHICLES.

       (a) Increase in Amount for HMMWV Procurement.--The amount 
     provided in section

[[Page H3866]]

     101(5) is hereby increased by $51,300,000, to be available 
     for procurement of High-Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled 
     Vehicles.
       (b) Offset From Amounts for B-2 Program.--The amount 
     provided in section 103(1) is hereby reduced by $51,300,000, 
     to be derived from amounts for the B-2 aircraft program.
```
