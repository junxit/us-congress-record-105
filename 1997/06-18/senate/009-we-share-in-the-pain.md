# WE SHARE IN THE PAIN

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                          WE SHARE IN THE PAIN

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I appreciate the remarks of the Senator 
from Kentucky about the death of the minority leader's father. We all 
share in that pain. There is a sense of sadness in this body, and as we 
contemplate that, maybe it helps us all reflect on the fact that we are 
all human beings who share the same goals for the betterment of this 
country. I think that is a good thing for us to contemplate.
```
