# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                              {time}  1045
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Calvert). Will the gentleman from Ohio 
[Mr. Hall] come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. Hall of Ohio led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.
```
