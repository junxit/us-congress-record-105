# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Thurmond, Strom (T000254) [R-SC]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. THURMOND. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow it is the hope of the majority leader that the Senate will be 
able to consider amendments to the DOD authorization bill. Following 
the DOD bill, the Senate will conduct a period for routine morning 
business. Votes will not occur on Friday of this week. On Monday, the 
Senate will begin the reconciliation bill. However, all votes with 
respect to that bill on Monday will be stacked to occur on Tuesday, 
June 24, at 9:30 a.m. Therefore, rollcall votes will occur beginning at 
9:30 a.m. on Tuesday. I remind all Members that there is a lot of work 
to be done before the Senate adjourns for the July 4 recess. Therefore, 
I would appreciate all Senators' cooperation in order to complete our 
business in a responsible fashion.

                          ____________________
```
