# ADJOURNMENT FROM SATURDAY, JUNE 21, 1997, TO MONDAY, JUNE 23, 1997

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

```
   ADJOURNMENT FROM SATURDAY, JUNE 21, 1997, TO MONDAY, JUNE 23, 1997

  Mr. GUTKNECHT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns on Saturday, June 21, 1997, it adjourn to meet at 10:30 
a.m. on Monday, June 23, 1997, for morning hour debates.

gentleman from Minnesota?
  There was no objection.
```
