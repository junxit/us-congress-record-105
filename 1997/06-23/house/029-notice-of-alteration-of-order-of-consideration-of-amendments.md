**Speaking:** Stump, Bob (S001044) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 169, H.R. 1119

```
  NOTICE OF ALTERATION OF ORDER OF CONSIDERATION OF AMENDMENTS DURING 
FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1119, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT 
                          FOR FISCAL YEAR 1998

169; the amendment printed in section 8(c) of House Resolution 169; 
amendment No. 35 printed in part 2 of House Report 105-137.
```
