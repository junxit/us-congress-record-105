# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the order of the House of January 21, 1997, 
the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by the 
majority and minority leaders for morning hour debates. The Chair will 
alternate recognition between the parties, with each party limited to 
not to exceed 30 minutes, and each Member except the majority and 
minority leader limited to not to exceed 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Michigan [Mr. Bonior] for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
