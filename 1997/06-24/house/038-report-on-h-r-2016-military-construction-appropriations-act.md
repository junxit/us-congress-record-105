# REPORT ON H.R. 2016, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

**Measures:** H.R. 2016

```
  REPORT ON H.R. 2016, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

  Mr. PACKARD, from the Committee on Appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-150) on the bill (H.R. 2016) making 
appropriations for military construction, family housing, and base 
realignment and closure for the Department of Defense for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 1998, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the Union Calendar and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Radanovich). All points of order are 
reserved on the bill.
```
