# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Taylor, Charles H. (T000067) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 79

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. TAYLOR of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask permission to speak 
out of order. On rollcall vote 231, House Resolution 79, to disapprove 
most-favored-nation treatment to the products of the People's Republic 
of China, I was recorded as voting ``no'', it was my intention to vote 
``yes'', to deny MFN to China. I ask that this statement be printed in 
the Congressional Record immediately after rollcall vote 231.

                          ____________________
```
