# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on Wednesday, June 25, 1997 at 
9:30 a.m. to conduct an oversight hearing on the Administration's 
proposal to restructure Indian gaming fee assessments. The hearing will 
be held in room 562 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
