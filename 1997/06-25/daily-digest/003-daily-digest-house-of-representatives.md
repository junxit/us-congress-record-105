Page H4384
Suspension--Cost of Government Day: By a yea-and-nay vote of 386 yeas 
to 20 nays with 13 voting ``present'', Roll No. 237, the House voted to 
suspend the rules and agree to H. Con. Res. 102, expressing the sense 
of the Congress that the cost of government spending and regulatory 
programs should be reduced so that American families will be able to 
keep more of what they earn. The concurrent resolution was debated on 
June 23.
  Pages H4384-85
Balanced Budget Act: By a recorded vote of 270 ayes to 162 noes Roll 
No. 241, the House passed H.R. 2015, to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to subsections (b)(1) and (c) of section 105 of the concurrent 
resolution on the budget for fiscal year 1998.
  Pages H4416-H4606
  By a yea-and-nay vote of 207 yeas to 223 nays, Roll No. 240, rejected 
a Brown of Ohio motion that sought to recommit the bill to the 
Committee on the Budget with instructions to report it back to the 
House forthwith with an amendment that establishes the Child Health 
Insurance Initiative Act of 1997.
Pages H4593-H4601, H4602-05

to recommit the bill to the Committee on the Budget with instructions 
to report it back to the House forthwith with an amendment that 
establishes the Child Health Insurance Initiative Act of 1997 and the 
Balanced Budget Assurance Act of 1997.
