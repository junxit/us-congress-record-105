# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT TUESDAY, JULY 1, 1997, TO FILE REPORT ON BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES, FISCAL YEAR 1998

**Speaking:** Regula, Ralph (R000141) [R-OH]

```
   PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT 
TUESDAY, JULY 1, 1997, TO FILE REPORT ON BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR 
     DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES, FISCAL YEAR 1998

  Mr. REGULA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Appropriations may have until midnight Tuesday, July 1, 1997, to 
file a privileged report on a bill making appropriations for the 
Department of the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1998, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All points of order are reserved on the 
bill.

                          ____________________
```
