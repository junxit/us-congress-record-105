# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF AUSTRALIA

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
         VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF AUSTRALIA

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. On behalf of the distinguished chairman of the 
Committee on Foreign Relations, Mr. Helms, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Senate stand in recess for 3 minutes that we might greet our 
distinguished visitor, the Honorable John Howard, the Prime Minister of 
Australia.
  [Applause.]


                                 Recess

  There being no objection, the Senate, at 11:10 a.m., recessed until 
11:14 a.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer [Mr. Burns].

                          ____________________
```
