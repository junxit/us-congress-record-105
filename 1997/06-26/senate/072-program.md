# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** S. 949

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow at 9 a.m. the Senate will resume consideration of S. 949, the 
Tax Relief Act of 1997 and begin another lengthy series of rollcall 
votes. Following the disposition of the pending amendments, additional 
amendments may be offered. However, it is hoped that Members will 
refrain from offering amendments so that the Senate may complete action 
on this bill at a reasonable time on Friday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
