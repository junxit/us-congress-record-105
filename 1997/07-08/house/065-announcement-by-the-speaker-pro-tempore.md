# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 29, H.R. 748, H.R. 849, H.R. 1658

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5 of 
rule I, the Chair will now put the question on the bill called from the 
Corrections Calendar and each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the order in which 
each question arose.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 849 by the yeas and 
nays, Senate Joint Resolution 29 by the yeas and nays, H.R. 1658 by the 
yeas and nays, and H.R. 748 by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
