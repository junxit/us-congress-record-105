# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Goodling). Pursuant to the provisions of 
clause 5 of rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a 
minimum of 5 minutes the period of time within which a vote by 
electronic device may be taken on each motion to suspend the rules on
```
