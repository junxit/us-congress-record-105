# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hayworth, J. D. (H000413) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 29, H.R. 849

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. HAYWORTH. Mr. Speaker, my airplane was unavoidably detained 
because of avionics difficulties from Chicago into Washington this 
afternoon. Accordingly, Mr. Speaker, I missed two votes held under 
suspensions. I want the Record to reflect that had I been present, I 
would have voted ``yes'' on H.R. 849 and also ``yes'' on Senate Joint 
Resolution 29.
```
