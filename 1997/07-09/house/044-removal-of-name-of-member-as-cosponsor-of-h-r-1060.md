# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1060

**Speaking:** Burr, Richard (B001135) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 1060

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1060

  Mr. BURR of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, due to a clerical error, I 
ask unanimous consent to remove the name of the gentlewoman from New 
York [Mrs. Maloney] from my bill, H.R. 1060. Her name was mistakenly 
entered as a cosponsor instead of the gentleman from Connecticut [Mr. 
Maloney].
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
