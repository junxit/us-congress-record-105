# TRIBUTE TO CHARLES E. SIMPSON

**Speaking:** Towns, Edolphus (T000326) [D-NY]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO CHARLES E. SIMPSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EDOLPHUS TOWNS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 9, 1997

  Mr. TOWNS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to recognize Mr. Charles E. 
Simpson for his consistent and competent efforts in the field of 
corporate reorganizations, divestitures, and workouts.
  Presently, Mr. Simpson is in charge of bankruptcy/creditors' rights 
department of the firm of Windels, Marx, Davies and Ives in New York 
City. During his professional career, Charles has developed an 
impressive track record on behalf of clients such as Bancorp, Pantry 
Pride, Central Railroad, Braniff, Vanguard Oil & Service, and a host of 
other companies.
  Mr. Simpson is a member of numerous boards, including the Brooklyn 
Red Cross. He served in the U.S. Army, attended Harvard Law School, and 
received his undergraduate degree from Pepperdine University. I am 
pleased to highlight Mr. Simpson for his tireless and persistent 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
