# RECESS

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. INOUYE. Madam President, I ask that the Senate stand in recess 
until 2 o'clock.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until 2 p.m. today.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:59 p.m., recessed until 2:03 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer [Mrs. Hutchison].
```
