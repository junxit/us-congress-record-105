# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 29

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on July 14, 1997 he had 
presented to the President of the United States, the following enrolled 
joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 29. Joint resolution to direct the Secretary of 
     the Interior to design and construct a permanent addition to 
     the Franklin Delano Roosevelt Memorial in Washington, D.C., 
     and for other purposes.
```
