# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 21, 1997, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with 
each party limited to not to exceed 30 minutes, and each Member except 
the majority and minority leader limited to not to exceed 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Massachusetts [Mr. Frank] for 
5 minutes.

                          ____________________
```
