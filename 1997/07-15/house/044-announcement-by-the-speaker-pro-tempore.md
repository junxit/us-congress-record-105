# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 1818, H.R. 2035

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will 
now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the order in which 
that motion was entertained, followed by the question de novo on 
approval of the Journal.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 1818 by the yeas and 
nays; H.R. 2035 by the yeas and nays; and on the approval of the 
Journal de novo.
  The Chair may reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
