# BOEING-McDONNELL DOUGLAS MERGER

**Speaking:** Metcalf, Jack (M000669) [R-WA]

```
                    BOEING-McDONNELL DOUGLAS MERGER

  (Mr. METCALF asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. METCALF. Mr. Speaker, yesterday the European Commission's Merger 
Task Force voted to disapprove the Boeing-McDonnell Douglas merger. The 
Economic Community charged Boeing would have to make greater 
concessions if they would receive approval from the EC.
  I have sent a letter, developed a letter to the EC and I have sent it 
to every congressional office. At this time I am urging every Member of 
Congress to send that letter and show solid support for keeping their 
nose out of our business.

                          ____________________
```
