# Text of House Amendment 35

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
H.R. 2159

                      Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee

       Amendment No. 35: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:


                        assistance for ethiopia

       Sec. 572. The Department of State should closely monitor 
     and take into account human rights progress in Ethiopia as it 
     obligates funds appropriated or otherwise made available by 
     this Act for Ethiopia.
```
