speak and my colleague from Arkansas. Could I just for a moment ask 
unanimous consent that an intern, Mara Davis, be allowed to be in the

permitted--I know the Senator from Ohio wants to introduce a bill, and 
I do not want to delay that--but I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted to proceed for 5 minutes in morning business.
