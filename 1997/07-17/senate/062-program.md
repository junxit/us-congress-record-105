# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 1023

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. For the information of all Members, Monday from noon 
until 3 p.m., the Senate will be in a period of routine morning 
business. By previous order, at 3 p.m., the Senate will begin 
consideration of the HUD-VA appropriations bill. Under the previous 
order, at 5:15 p.m., the Senate will resume consideration of S. 1023, 
the Treasury and General Government appropriations bill, with a series 
of votes occurring on the remaining pending amendments to the bill, 
including final passage of S. 1023. Following passage of the Treasury 
and General Government appropriations bill, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the VA-HUD appropriations bill. As a reminder to all 
Members, the Senate will not be in session on Friday. The next rollcall 
will be a series of votes, commencing at 5:15 on Monday afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
