# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2003, BALANCED BUDGET ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 192, H.R. 2003

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2003, BALANCED 
                     BUDGET ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 1997

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-195) on the resolution (H. Res. 192) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2003) to reform the budget process and 
enforce the bipartisan balanced budget agreement of 1997, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
