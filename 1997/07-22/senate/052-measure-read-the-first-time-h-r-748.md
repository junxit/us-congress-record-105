# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 748

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 748

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 748

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 748 has arrived from 
the House.
  I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 748) to amend the prohibition of title 18, 
     United States Code, against financial transactions with 
     terrorists.

  Mr. BOND. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request on behalf of the other side of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will remain at 
the desk and have its second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
