# Text of House Amendment 53

**Speaking:** Bereuter, Doug (B000403) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
H.R. 2159

                        Offered By: Mr. Bereuter

       Amendment No. 53: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.    (a) None of the funds appropriated in this Act may 
     be made available directly to the Government of Cambodia.
```
