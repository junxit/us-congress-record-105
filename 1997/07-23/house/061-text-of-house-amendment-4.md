# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Petri, Thomas E. (P000265) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2203

```
H.R. 2203

                         Offered By: Mr. Petri

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.   . None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to pay the salary of any officer or employee of the 
     Department of the Interior who plans, authorizes, or 
     implements the acquisition of land for, or construction of, 
     the Animas-La Plata Project, in Colorado and New Mexico, 
     pursuant to the Act of April 11, 1956 (43 U.S.C. 620 et seq.) 
     and the Colorado River Basin Project Act (43 U.S.C. 1501 et 
     seq.).
```
