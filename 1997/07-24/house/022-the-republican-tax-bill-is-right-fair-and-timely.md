# THE REPUBLICAN TAX BILL IS RIGHT, FAIR, AND TIMELY

**Speaking:** Sununu, John E. (S001078) [R-NH]

```
           THE REPUBLICAN TAX BILL IS RIGHT, FAIR, AND TIMELY

  (Mr. SUNUNU asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SUNUNU. Mr. Speaker, this week Congress will finalize its 
proposal for tax relief for all Americans and begin discussions on that 
tax proposal with the President. During the past month Republicans have 
worked consistently to lower taxes for all Americans, with a $500-per-
child tax credit, relief for families sending their children to 
college, providing death tax relief for small businesses and family 
farms. In fact, 75 percent of the tax relief in this proposal goes to 
those earning less than $75,000.
  I hope the President will avoid the class warfare rhetoric we have 
heard today, but I am not optimistic. This administration's record 
falls short. In 1993 they pushed through the largest tax increase in 
America's history. This administration has proposed higher taxes on 
health care, taxes on energy, even taxes on camping equipment. They 
have called the reduction in the capital gains tax as being unnecessary 
and suggested that relief from death taxes is selfish.
  The administration's record is one of higher and higher taxes. This 
is wrong. Americans deserve this tax relief. This bill is right, it is 
fair, and the time is now.

                          ____________________
```
