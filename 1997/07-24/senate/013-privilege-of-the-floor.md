# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 98

```
                         PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, in behalf of Mr. Bingaman, I ask unanimous 
consent that privileges of the floor be granted to Dr. Robert Simon on 
detail from the Department of Energy to his staff, during the pendency 
of Senate Resolution 98 or any votes occurring thereupon.
```
