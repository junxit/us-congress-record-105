# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO FILE SUNDRY PRIVILEGED REPORTS

**Speaking:** McDade, Joseph M. (M000399) [R-PA]

```
 PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO FILE SUNDRY PRIVILEGED 
                                REPORTS

  Mr. McDADE. Mr. Speaker, having cleared this with the minority, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Committee on Appropriations may have until 
midnight tonight, July 25, 1997, to file three privileged reports on 
bills making appropriations for the Department of Defense for fiscal 
year 1998; the Departments of Labor, Health, and Human Services, and 
Education, and related agencies for fiscal year 1998; and the 
Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the Judiciary, and related 
agencies for fiscal year 1998.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All points of order are reserved on the 
bills.

                          ____________________
```
