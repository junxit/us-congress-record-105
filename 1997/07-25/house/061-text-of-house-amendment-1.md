# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** DeFazio, Peter A. (D000191) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 2266

```
H.R. 2266

                        Offered By: Mr. DeFazio

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.   . None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be obligated or expended for the public printing or binding 
     of Government publications in contravention of measures 
     established by the Joint Committee on Printing pursuant to 
     section 103 of title 44, United States Code.
```
