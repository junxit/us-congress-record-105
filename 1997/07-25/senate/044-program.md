# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

**Measures:** S. 830

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HELMS. For the information of all Members, on Monday it will be 
the leader's intention to begin consideration of S. 830, the FDA reform 
bill. Following debate on that issue, there will be a period for 
morning business, to be followed by the Transportation appropriations 
bill beginning at 5 p.m.
  By a previous consent, any votes ordered with respect to the 
Transportation bill will be postponed to occur at 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday. Therefore, no votes will occur in Monday's session of the 
Senate. However, it is the hope of the majority leader that the Senate 
could complete debate on the Transportation appropriations bill on 
Monday. And, in addition, as announced by the majority leader, the next 
votes will be a series of votes occurring on Tuesday at 9:30 a.m. on 
the Commerce, Justice, State Department appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
