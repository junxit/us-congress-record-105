# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
28, 1997, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                National Institute of Building Sciences

       Niranjan S. Shah, of Illinois, to be a Member of the Board 
     of Directors of the National Institute of Building Sciences 
     for a term expiring September 7, 1998, vice John H. Miller, 
     term expired, which was sent to the Senate on January 9, 
     1997.
```
