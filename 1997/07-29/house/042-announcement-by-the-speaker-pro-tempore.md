# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 75, H.R. 1348

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule 1, the Chair will 
now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed from Monday, July 28, 1997, in the 
order in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  House Concurrent Resolution 75, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 1348, by the yeas and nays.
  Pursuant to the order of the House of today, the Chair will reduce to 
5 minutes the time for both electronic votes in this series.
```
