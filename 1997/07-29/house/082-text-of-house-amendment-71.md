# Text of House Amendment 71

**Speaking:** Forbes, Michael P. (F000257) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
H.R. 2159

                         Offered By: Mr. Forbes

       Amendment No. 71: Page 94, insert the following after line 
     3:


       prohibition on assistance to plo and palestinian authority

       Sec. 572. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, 
     no funds appropriated or otherwise made available by this Act 
     may be made available to provide assistance, directly or 
     indirectly, for the Palestinian Liberation Organization or 
     the Palestinian Authority.
```
