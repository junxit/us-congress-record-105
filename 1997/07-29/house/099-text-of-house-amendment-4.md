# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Doggett, Lloyd (D000399) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2267

```
H.R. 2267

                        Offered By: Mr. Doggett

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.   . None of the funds provided by this Act shall be 
     available to promote the sale or export of tobacco or tobacco 
     products, or to seek the reduction or removal by any foreign 
     country of restrictions on the marketing of such products.
```
