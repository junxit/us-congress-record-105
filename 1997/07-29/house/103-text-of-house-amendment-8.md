# Text of House Amendment 8

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2267

```
H.R. 2267

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 8: At the end of the bill, insert the 
     following after the last section (preceding the short title):
       Sec.   . None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used for activities to increase 
     foreign market access for tobacco products.
```
