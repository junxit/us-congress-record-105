# Text of House Amendment 79

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
H.R. 2159

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 79: At the end of the bill, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec.   . None of the funds in this Act may be used to pay 
     for NATO Expansion not authorized by law.
```
