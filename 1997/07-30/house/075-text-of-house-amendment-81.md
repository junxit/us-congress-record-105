# Text of House Amendment 81

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
H.R. 2159

                       Offered by: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 81: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 572. Not more than $73,000,000 of the funds 
     appropriated or otherwise made available by this Act may be 
     made available for the Palestine Liberation Organization 
     (P.L.O.), the Palestinian Authority, or successor entities 
     until the President reports to the Congress that an agreement 
     has been concluded among the Palestinian Authority, the Cairo 
     Amman Bank of Gaza, the Overseas Private Investment 
     Corporation, and any and all American companies that provides 
     for any repayment of claims made by the Overseas Private 
     Investment Corporation.
```
