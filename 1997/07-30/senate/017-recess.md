# RECESS

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we stand in 
recess until the hour of 1 o'clock, and that the time continue to run 
on the conference report pursuant to the Budget Act, and it be charged 
equally.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, at 12:29 p.m., the Senate recessed until 1 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Hagel).
```
