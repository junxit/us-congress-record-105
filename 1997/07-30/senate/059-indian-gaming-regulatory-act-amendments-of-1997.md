# INDIAN GAMING REGULATORY ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1997

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 1077

```
            INDIAN GAMING REGULATORY ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1997

● Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, there is an error in the statement 
that I submitted for the Record in introducing S. 1077, a bill to amend 
the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act. The portion of the statement alluding 
to a new process for the negotiation of gaming compacts was 
inadvertently included. There is no section concerning compacting in 
the bill I introduced.●
```
