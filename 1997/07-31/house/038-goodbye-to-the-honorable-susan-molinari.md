# GOODBYE TO THE HONORABLE SUSAN MOLINARI

**Speaking:** Pryce, Deborah (P000555) [R-OH]

```
                GOODBYE TO THE HONORABLE SUSAN MOLINARI

  (Ms. PRYCE of Ohio asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. PRYCE of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, at the risk of losing it on C-SPAN, 
at the risk of having Members miss their planes, I would just like to 
close this by saying how much this body will miss you, Susan, and how 
much I will miss you, too. Your wit and your charm and your grace and 
your grit and everything that I tried to learn from you, I hope we can 
sustain even in your absence.
  You were the first Member that I met outside of Ohio. You taught me 
so much. I hope that you will still be around to keep us going. So do 
not be a stranger. Godspeed, Susan Molinari.

                          ____________________
```
