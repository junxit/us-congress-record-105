# LIMITING AMENDMENTS DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2159, FOREIGN OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED PROGRAMS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

**Speaking:** Callahan, Sonny (C000052) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
LIMITING AMENDMENTS DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2159, FOREIGN 
OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED PROGRAMS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  1998

  Mr. CALLAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
further consideration of the bill (H.R. 2159) making appropriations for 
foreign operations, export financing, and related programs for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 1998, and for other purposes, pursuant 
to the order of the House of July 24, 1997, no other amendment shall be 
in order (except pro forma amendments offered for the purpose of 
debate) unless printed before August 1, 1997 in the portion of the 
Congressional Record designated for that purpose in clause 6 of rule 
XXIII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Alabama?
  There was no objection.
```
