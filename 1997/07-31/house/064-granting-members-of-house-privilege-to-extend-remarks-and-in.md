# GRANTING MEMBERS OF HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

**Speaking:** Hoekstra, Peter (H000676) [R-MI]

```
       GRANTING MEMBERS OF HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND 
          INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

  Mr. HOEKSTRA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that today and 
tomorrow all Members be permitted to extend their remarks and to 
include extraneous material in that section of the Record entitled 
``Extensions of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
```
