# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 1997, AT 11 A.M.

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 136

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 1997, AT 11 A.M.

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 136.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 8 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, September 2, 1997, at 11 a.m., under the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 136.
```
