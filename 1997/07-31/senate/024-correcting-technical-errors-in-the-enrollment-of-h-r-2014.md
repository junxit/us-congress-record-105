# CORRECTING TECHNICAL ERRORS IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 2014

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 138, H.R. 2014

```
       CORRECTING TECHNICAL ERRORS IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 2014

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
turn to the consideration of a House concurrent resolution that 
corrects the enrollment of the tax fairness conference report, that 
there be no amendments in order, that the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, all 
without intervening action or debate.
```
