# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BANKRUPTCY ON EDUCATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Administrative Oversight 
and the Courts concluded hearings to examine the negative impact of 
bankruptcy on education funding, and the proposed Investment in 
Education Act of 1977, after receiving testimony from Connecticut 
Assistant Attorney General Joan E. Pilver, Hartford; Jayne Morrell, 
Dallas Independent School District, and Elizabeth Weller, Blair, 
Goggan, Sampson & Meeks, both of Dallas, Texas; Donald R. Boehm, 
Houston Independent School District, and Michael Deeds, Heard, Goggan, 
Blair, and Williams, both of Houston, Texas; Kent Scroggins, Lake Worth 
Independent School District, and Barbara M. Williams, Rohne, 
Hoodenpyle, Lobert, Myers & Scott, both of Arlington, Texas; Dorothy J. 
Conrad, St. Lucie County Tax Collector, Ft. Pierce, Florida; Sandy 
Hume, Boulder, Colorado, on behalf of the National Association of 
County Treasury and Finance Officers; Lawrence Friedman, Southfield, 
Michigan.
