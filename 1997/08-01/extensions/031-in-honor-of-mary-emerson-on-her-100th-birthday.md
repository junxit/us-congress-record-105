# IN HONOR OF MARY EMERSON ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Kucinich, Dennis J. (K000336) [D-OH]

```
             IN HONOR OF MARY EMERSON ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. DENNIS J. KUCINICH

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 31, 1997

  Mr. KUCINICH. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Mary Emerson on her 
birthday, September 3, 1997. At 100 years young, Mary continues to live 
a fulfilling and happy life. She has prospered in her professional 
career as well as in her personal life.
  Mary is a joy to be around. She is loved by her family and many 
friends in the community. My fellow colleagues, please join me in 
wishing a great lady a very happy birthday and many more delightful 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
