# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2264

```
H.R. 2264

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

              (Substitute Amendment for Amendment No. 24)

       Amendment No. 29: In the item relating to ``Health 
     Resources and Services Administration--health resources and 
     services'', insert after the first dollar amount (before the 
     comma) ``(reduced by $2,000,000)''.
       In the item relating to ``Centers for Disease Control and 
     Prevention--disease control, research, and training'', insert 
     after the first dollar amount (before the comma) ``(increased 
     by $2,000,000)''.
```
