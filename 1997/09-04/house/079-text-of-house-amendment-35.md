# Text of House Amendment 35

**Speaking:** Coburn, Tom (C000560) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2264

```
H.R. 2264

                         Offered by: Mr. Coburn

       Amendment No. 35: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following 
     section:
       Sec. 516. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, 
     none of the funds made available in this Act may be used to 
     carry out or promote any program of distributing sterile 
     needles for the hypodermic injection of any illegal drug.
```
