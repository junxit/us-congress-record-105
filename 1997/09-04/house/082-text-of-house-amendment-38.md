# Text of House Amendment 38

**Speaking:** Filner, Bob (F000116) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2264

```
H.R. 2264

                         Offered By: Mr. Filner

       Amendment No. 38: At the end of title II, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following 
     section:
       Sec. 213. Of the amounts made available in this title for 
     the amount ``Administration for Children and Familes--
     Children and families services programs'', $12,800,000 of the 
     amount available for carrying out the Community Services 
     Block Grant Act is transferred and made available under 
     section 30403 of Public Law 103-322 for the Community Schools 
     Youth Services and Supervision Grant Program Act of 1994.
```
