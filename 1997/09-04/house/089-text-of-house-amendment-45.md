# Text of House Amendment 45

**Speaking:** Smith, Nick (S000597) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2264

```
H.R. 2264

                   Offered By: Mr. Smith of Michigan

       Amendment No. 45: At the end of title V, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following 
     section:
       Sec. 516. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to apply, for any State, formulas under subparts I 
     and II of part B of title XIX of the Public Health Service 
     Act that are different in any numerical respect from the 
     formulas that were applied under such subparts, respectively, 
     for the State for fiscal year 1997 (other than numerical 
     changes necessary to reflect the amounts made available in 
     this Act for carrying out such subparts for fiscal year 
     1998).
```
