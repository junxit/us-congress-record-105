# THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                         THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, it is worth coming to the opening moments of 
the Senate session each day just to hear the Chaplain's prayers. I wish 
to express, again, my sincere appreciation for the beauty and for the 
meaningfulness of those prayers. It gives us the right frame of mind to 
begin a day's work together for the American people.

                          ____________________
```
