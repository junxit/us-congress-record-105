  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Wednesday, September 3, 1997, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,413,621,503,580.39--five trillion, four hundred thirteen billion,

$4,035,387,000,000--four trillion, thirty-five billion, three hundred 
eighty-seven million.
  Ten years ago, September 3, 1987, the Federal debt stood at 
$2,361,615,000,000--two trillion, three hundred sixty-one billion, six 
hundred fifteen million.

$1,110,240,000,000--one trillion, one hundred ten billion, two hundred 
forty million--which reflects a debt increase of more than $4 trillion, 
$4,303,381,503,580.39--four trillion, three hundred three billion,
