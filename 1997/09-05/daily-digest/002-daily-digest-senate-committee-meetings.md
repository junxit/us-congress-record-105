# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
CAMPAIGN FINANCING INVESTIGATION
Committee on Governmental Affairs: Committee continued hearings to 
examine certain matters with regard to the committee's special 
investigation on campaign financing, receiving testimony from David 
Strauss, former Deputy Chief of Staff for Vice President Gore; and 
former Mayor Michael L. Mitoma, Carson, California.
  Hearings continue on Tuesday, September 9.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded hearings on the 
nominations of Marjorie O. Rendell, of Pennsylvania, to be United 
States Circuit Judge for the Third Circuit, A. Richard Caputo, to be 
United States District Judge for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, 
Bruce C. Kauffman, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
District of Pennsylvania, and Richard A. Lazzara, to be United

[[Page D904]]

States District Judge for the Middle District of Florida, after the 
nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf. Mr. 
Lazzara was introduced by Senators Mack and Graham.
```
