# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kind, Ron (K000188) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2159

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RON KIND

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 5, 1997

  Mr. KIND. Mr. Speaker, I wish the Record to state that during 
rollcall vote 364, on H.R. 2159, the foreign operations appropriations 
bill, I was on the floor of the House, I inserted my voting card into 
the electronic voting machine and voted for passage of the bill. For 
some reason my vote was not recorded and therefore I am listed as 
having not voted.
  Please let the Record reflect that I was present for the vote, as 
evidenced by my votes on all of the preceding amendments, and I voted 
in favor of passage of H.R. 2159, the foreign operations appropriations 
bill.

                          ____________________
```
