# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 21, 1997, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each 
party limited to not to exceed 25 minutes, and each Member except the 
majority leader, the minority leader, or the minority whip limited to 
not to exceed 5 minutes each, but in no event shall debate continue 
beyond 9:50 a.m.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Kansas [Mr. Ryun] for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
