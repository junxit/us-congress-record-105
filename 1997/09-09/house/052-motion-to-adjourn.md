# MOTION TO ADJOURN

**Speaking:** Doggett, Lloyd (D000399) [D-TX]

```
                           MOTION TO ADJOURN

  Mr. DOGGETT. Mr. Speaker, in view of the Speaker's failure to 
schedule campaign finance reform, I offer a privileged motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ney). The Clerk will report the motion.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       Mr. Doggett moves that the House do now adjourn.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion to adjourn 
offered by the gentleman from Texas [Mr. Doggett].

the noes appeared to have it.

quorum is not present and make the point of order that a quorum is not 
present.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Evidently a quorum is not present.
  The Sergeant at Arms will notify absent Members.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 29,
```
