# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. RES. 120

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 120

```
                UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. RES. 120

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that at 12 noon today, the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of a resolution regarding Mother Teresa that was 
submitted today by Senators Nickles, Lott, and Daschle. I further ask 
unanimous consent that there be 30 minutes of debate equally divided in 
the usual form. I finally ask unanimous consent that at the hour of 
2:15 p.m. today, the Senate proceed to a vote on the adoption of the 
resolution with no intervening action or debate. This resolution has 
been cleared by the minority leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
