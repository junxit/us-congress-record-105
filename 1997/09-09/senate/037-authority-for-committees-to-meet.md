# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Armed Services be authorized to meet on Tuesday, September 
9, 1997, at 10 a.m. in open session, to consider the nomination of Gen. 
Henry H. Shelton, USA, to be Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                   committee on governmental affairs

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent on behalf of 
the Governmental Affairs Committee Special Investigation to meet on 
Tuesday, September 9, at 10 a.m., for a hearing on campaign financing 
issues.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
