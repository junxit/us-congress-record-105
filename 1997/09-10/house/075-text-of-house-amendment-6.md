# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Lowey, Nita M. (L000480) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2378

```
H.R. 2378

                         Offered By: Mrs. Lowey

       Amendment No. 6: Page 101, after line 18, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 633. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement, administer, or enforce section 9302(k) 
     of the Balanced Budget Act of 1997 (Pub. L. 105-33), as added 
     by section 1604(f)(3) of the Taxpayer Relief Act of 1997 
     (Pub. L. 105-34).
```
