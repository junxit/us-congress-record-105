# RESOLUTION ON NATIONAL HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGE AND UNIVERSITIES

**Measures:** S.Res. 111

```
   RESOLUTION ON NATIONAL HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGE AND UNIVERSITIES

                                 ______
                                 

                      THURMOND AMENDMENT NO. 1128

  Mr. GORTON (for Mr. Thurmond) proposed an amendment to the resolution 
(S. Res. 111) designating the week beginning September 14, 1997, as 
``National Historically Black Colleges and Universities Week,'' and for 
other purposes; as follows:

       On page 1, in the first clause, strike ``116'' and insert 
     ``104''.
       On page 2, line 3, strike ``14'' and insert ``21''.
       Amend the title to read as follows: ``A resolution 
     designating the week beginning September 21, 1997, as 
     `National Historically Black Colleges and Universities Week', 
     and for other purposes.''.

                          ____________________
```
