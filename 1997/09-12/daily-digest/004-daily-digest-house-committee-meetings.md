# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--DEFENSE SURPLUS PROPERTY
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on 
Government Management, Information, and Technology held an oversight 
hearing on Defense Surplus Equipment and the Activities of the Defense 
Reutilization and Marketing Service. Testimony was heard from 
Representative Smith of Michigan; David Warren, Director, Defense 
Management Issues, GAO; the following officials of the Department of 
Defense: Bob Lieberman, Assistant Inspector General, Auditing; Jeff 
Jones, Principal Executive Director, Materiel Management, Defense 
Logistics Agency; and Col. Robert Mansfield, USAF, Commander, Defense 
Reutilization and Marketing Service; and public witnesses.
```
