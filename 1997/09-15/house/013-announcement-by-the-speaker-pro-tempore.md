# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Speaking:** Owens, Major R. (O000159) [D-NY]

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hastings of Washington). The Chair would 
remind Members or caution them not to characterize action of the Senate 
or to quote from publications which are critical of the Senate.
  Mr. OWENS. Mr. Speaker, I am sorry. I did not know that we cannot 
quote from publications.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are not to characterize action of 
the Senate in any way, critical or otherwise.
```
