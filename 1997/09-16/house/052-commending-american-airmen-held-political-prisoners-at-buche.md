# COMMENDING AMERICAN AIRMEN HELD POLITICAL PRISONERS AT BUCHENWALD

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 95

```
   COMMENDING AMERICAN AIRMEN HELD POLITICAL PRISONERS AT BUCHENWALD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is the question de novo 
of suspending the rules and agreeing to the concurrent resolution, 
House Concurrent Resolution 95.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida [Mr. Mica] that the House suspend the rules and 
agree to the concurrent resolution, H.Con.Res. 95.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the concurrent resolution was 
agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
