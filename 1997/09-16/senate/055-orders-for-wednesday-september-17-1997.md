# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, 1997

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

**Measures:** H.R. 2107

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, 1997

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the

hour of 9:45 a.m. on Wednesday, September 17. I further ask that on
```
