# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1609 FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING COSPONSORS AND REQUESTING REPRINTS

**Speaking:** Weller, Jerry (W000273) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1609

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1609 
      FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING COSPONSORS AND REQUESTING REPRINTS

  Mr. WELLER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1609, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Molinari of New York, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprints pursuant to clause 4 of rule 
XXII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Illinois?
  There was no objection.
```
