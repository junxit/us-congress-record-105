# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I inform all Senators that tomorrow 
morning there will be 20 minutes of debate before a vote on or in 
relation to Senator Hutchison's amendment on the National Endowment for 
the Arts. Senators can, therefore, anticipate that the first rollcall 
vote tomorrow will take place at approximately 9:30 a.m. Following that 
vote, I hope that Members will cooperate with the managers of the 
Interior appropriations bill in offering their amendments. The majority 
leader has stated that we will complete action on this bill on 
Thursday. Senators should, therefore, anticipate rollcall votes 
throughout the day on Thursday. I thank all Senators for their 
attention and cooperation.

                          ____________________
```
