**Measures:** H.Res. 232, H.R. 2160

```
    WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2160, 
   AGRICULTURE, RURAL DEVELOPMENT, FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION, AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-255) on the resolution (H. Res. 232) 
waiving points of order against the conference report to accompany the 
bill (H.R. 2160) making appropriations for Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies

purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be
```
