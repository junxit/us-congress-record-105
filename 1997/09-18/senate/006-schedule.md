# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2107

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, this 
morning the Senate will resume consideration of H.R. 2107, the Interior 
appropriations bill, with me being recognized regarding my amendment on 
the NEA. Following 20 minutes of debate on that amendment, the Senate 
will vote on or in relation to that NEA amendment. Therefore, Senators 
can

[[Page S9532]]

anticipate the first rollcall at approximately 9:30 this morning. It 
will be probably around 9:40.
  Following that vote, it is hoped that Members will cooperate with the 
managers of the Interior appropriations bill in offering their 
amendments and working on short time agreements. The majority leader 
has stated that we will complete action on this bill today.
  With that in mind, Senators can anticipate additional rollcall votes 
throughout today's session of the Senate.
  I thank the Members.

                          ____________________
```
