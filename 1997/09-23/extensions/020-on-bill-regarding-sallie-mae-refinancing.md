# ON BILL REGARDING SALLIE MAE REFINANCING

**Speaking:** Skaggs, David E. (S000462) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2511

```
                ON BILL REGARDING SALLIE MAE REFINANCING

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DAVID E. SKAGGS

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 23, 1997

  Mr. SKAGGS. Mr. Speaker, on September 18, when I introduced H.R. 
2511, a bill to correct an unfair practice occurring as a result of the
```
