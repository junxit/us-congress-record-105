# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore [Mr. Gutknecht]. Pursuant to the provisions 
of clause 5 of rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a 
minimum of 5 minutes the period of time within which a vote by 
electronic device may be taken on the next two motions to suspend the 
rules on which the Chair has postponed further proceedings, and on 
which recorded votes may be ordered.
  Votes on additional suspension motions will be postponed until 
approximately 9 p.m.
```
