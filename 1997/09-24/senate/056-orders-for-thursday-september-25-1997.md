**Speaking:** Faircloth, Lauch (F000437) [R-NC]

**Measures:** S. 1156

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 1997

  Mr. FAIRCLOTH. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask
```
