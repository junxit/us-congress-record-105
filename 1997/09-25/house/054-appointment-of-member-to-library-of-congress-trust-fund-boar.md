# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND BOARD

     APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND BOARD

provisions of section 1 of 2 USC 154, as amended by section 1 of Public
