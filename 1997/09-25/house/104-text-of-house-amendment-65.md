# Text of House Amendment 65

**Speaking:** Whitfield, Ed (W000413) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2267

```
H.R. 2267

                       Offered By: Mr. Whitfield

       Amendment No. 65: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 617. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to deport any person who 
     has filed a visa application or other petition with the 
     Immigration and Naturalization Service and is serving as a 
     licensed physician in a federally designated health 
     professionals shortage area as determined by the Department 
     of Health and Human Services.
```
