# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. There being no further requests for morning 
hour debates, pursuant to clause 12, rule I, the Chair declares the 
House in recess until 12 noon.
  Accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 43 minutes a.m.), the House stood in 
recess until 12 noon.
```
