# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR THE CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 244, SUBPOENA ENFORCEMENT IN THE CASE OF DORNAN V. SANCHEZ

**Measures:** H.Res. 244, H.Res. 253

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR THE CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE 
 RESOLUTION 244, SUBPOENA ENFORCEMENT IN THE CASE OF DORNAN V. SANCHEZ

  Mr. McINNIS (during the special order of Mr. Souder), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 105-280) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 253) providing for consideration of the 
resolution (H. Res. 244) demanding that the Office of the United States 
Attorney for the Central District of California file criminal charges 
against Hermandad Mexicana Nacional for failure to comply with a valid 
subpoena under the Federal Contested Elections Act, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
