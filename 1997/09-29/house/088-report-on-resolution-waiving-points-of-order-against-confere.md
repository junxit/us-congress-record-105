# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2203, ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

**Measures:** H.Res. 254, H.R. 2203

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
  ON H.R. 2203, ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

  Mr. McINNIS (during the special order of Mr. Souder), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 105-281) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 254) waiving points of order against the 
conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 2203) making 
appropriations for energy and water development for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1998, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
