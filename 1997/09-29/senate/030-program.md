# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HATCH. Tomorrow morning at 10 a.m. the Senate will begin 1 hour 
of debate prior to the cloture vote on the Coats amendment regarding 
school choice. Following that vote, the Senate will continue 
consideration of the D.C. appropriations bill with the hope of 
finishing action on that bill during Tuesday's session.
  The Senate will also consider the continuing resolution tomorrow as 
well. Therefore, additional votes will occur.
```
