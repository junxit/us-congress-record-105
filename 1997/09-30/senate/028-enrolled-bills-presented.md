# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 871, S. 1211

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on September 30, 1997 he 
had presented to the President of the United States, the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 871. An act to establish in the Oklahoma City National 
     Memorial as a unit of the National Park System; to designate 
     the Oklahoma City Memorial Trust, and for other purposes.
       S. 1211. An act to provide permanent authority for the 
     administration of au pair programs.
```
