              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. ENZI (for himself, Mr. Gregg, Mr. Frist, Mr.

     working environments, and for other purposes; to the

     to prevent the sale and distribution of tobacco products to

       S. 1239. A bill to suspend temporarily the duty on
