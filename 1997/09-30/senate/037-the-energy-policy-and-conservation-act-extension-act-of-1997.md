# THE ENERGY POLICY AND CONSERVATION ACT EXTENSION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 2472

```
      THE ENERGY POLICY AND CONSERVATION ACT EXTENSION ACT OF 1997

                                 ______
                                 

                      MURKOWSKI AMENDMENT NO. 1270

  Mr. LOTT (for Mr. Murkowski) proposed an amendment to the bill (H.R. 
2472) to extend certain programs under the Energy Policy and 
Conservation Act; as follows:

       Strike all after the enacting clause and insert in lieu 
     thereof:

     ``SECTION 1. ENERGY POLICY AND CONSERVATION ACT AMENDMENTS.

       ``The Energy Policy and Conservation Act is amended--
       ``(1) in section 166 (42 U.S.C. 6246) by striking ``for 
     fiscal year'' and inserting in lieu thereof ``through October 
     31,'';
       ``(2) in section 181 (42 U.S.C. 6251) by striking 
     ``September 30'' both places it appears and inserting in lieu 
     thereof ``October 31''; and
       ``(3) in section 281 (42 U.S.C. 6285) by striking 
     ``September 30'' both places it appears and inserting in lieu 
     thereof ``October 31''.''.




                          ____________________
```
