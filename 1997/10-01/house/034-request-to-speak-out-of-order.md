# REQUEST TO SPEAK OUT OF ORDER

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY], Foley, Mark (F000238) [R-FL]

```
                     REQUEST TO SPEAK OUT OF ORDER

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I move to strike the last word.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman is not in order.
  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to speak out of 
order.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  Mr. FOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I object.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Objection is heard.
  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, the Jewish holiday of Rosh Hashana starts in 
5 hours. The House should not be in session.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman is speaking out of order and 
will suspend.

                          ____________________
```
