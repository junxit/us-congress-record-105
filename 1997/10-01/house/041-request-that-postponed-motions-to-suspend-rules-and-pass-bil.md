# REQUEST THAT POSTPONED MOTIONS TO SUSPEND RULES AND PASS BILLS OR AGREE TO RESOLUTIONS BE CONSIDERED PASSED IN FORM CONSIDERED BY HOUSE ON MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 1997, AND THE JOURNAL STAND APPROVED

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
REQUEST THAT POSTPONED MOTIONS TO SUSPEND RULES AND PASS BILLS OR AGREE 
  TO RESOLUTIONS BE CONSIDERED PASSED IN FORM CONSIDERED BY HOUSE ON 
       MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 1997, AND THE JOURNAL STAND APPROVED

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, in my continuing efforts to help Members who 
wish to make their religious observations on this day, I ask unanimous 
consent that the remaining postponed motions to suspend the rules and 
pass bills or agree to resolutions be considered as passed in the form 
considered by the House on Monday, September 29, 1997, and that the 
Journal stand approved today.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. Speaker, reserving the right to object.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Regular order is insisted on. Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from Texas?

                          ____________________
```
