# REQUEST FOR PERMISSION FOR CHAIR TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING ON REMAINING MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX], Watt, Melvin L. (W000207) [D-NC]

```
 REQUEST FOR PERMISSION FOR CHAIR TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING 
               ON REMAINING MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Chair be 
authorized to reduce to not less than 2 minutes the time for voting by 
electronic device on the remaining motions to suspend the rules.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  Mr. WATT. Mr. Speaker, I object.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman from North Carolina [Mr. Watt] 
objects.

                          ____________________
```
