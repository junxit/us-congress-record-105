# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE, PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 1997, THROUGH TODAY

**Speaking:** Livingston, Bob (L000371) [R-LA]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE, PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE 
EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 
                          1997, THROUGH TODAY

  Mr. LIVINGSTON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that for 
Tuesday, September 30, 1997, Wednesday, October 1, 1997, and for today, 
all Members be permitted to extend their remarks and to include 
extraneous material in that section of the Record entitled ``Extensions 
of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Louisiana?
  There was no objection.
```
