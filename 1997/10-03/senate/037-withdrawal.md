# WITHDRAWAL

```

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 3, 1997, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:


                     Office of Personnel Management

       James B. King, of Massachusetts, to be Director of the 
     Office of Personnel Management for a term of 4 years.
```
