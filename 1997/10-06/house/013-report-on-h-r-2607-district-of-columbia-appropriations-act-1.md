# REPORT ON H.R. 2607, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

**Measures:** H.R. 2607

```
   REPORT ON H.R. 2607, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

  Mr. LIVINGSTON, from the Committee on appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-298) on the bill (H.R. 2607) making 
appropriations for the government of the District of Columbia and other 
activities chargeable in whole or in part against the revenues of said 
District for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1998, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the Union Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All points of order are reserved on the 
bill.
```
