# SENATE RESOLUTION 132--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A COLLECTION OF RULES AND AUTHORITIES

**Measures:** S.Res. 132

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 132--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A COLLECTION OF 
                         RULES AND AUTHORITIES

  Mr. WARNER submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 132

       Resolved, That a collection of rules and authorities of 
     special investigatory committees of the Senate, be printed as 
     a Senate document, and there be printed additional copies

[[Page S10434]]

     of such document up to, but not exceeding, $1,200 for use of 
     the Committee on Rules and Administration.

                          ____________________
```
