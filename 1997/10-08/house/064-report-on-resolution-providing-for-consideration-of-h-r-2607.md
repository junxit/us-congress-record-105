# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2607, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

**Measures:** H.Res. 264, H.R. 2607

```
[[Page H8700]]
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2607, DISTRICT 
                  OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1998

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-315) on the resolution (H. Res. 264) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2607) making appropriations for the 
government of District of Columbia and other activities chargeable in 
whole or in part against the revenues of said District for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 1998, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
