# EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

```
                         EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, I rise very briefly to explain my absence 
on rollcall vote No. 271. This was a procedural vote to instruct the 
Sergeant at Arms. I was attending a conference with Ambassador Richard 
Holbrooke and was involved in a discussion of Bosnia and the 
enforcement of the Dayton Peace accords. I was unable to return to the 
Senate for the vote.

                          ____________________
```
