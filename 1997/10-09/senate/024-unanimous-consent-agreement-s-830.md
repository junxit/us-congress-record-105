# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 830

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 830

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 830

  Mr. STEVENS. I now would like to perform a series of missions for the 
leadership. Therefore, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate receives 
a message from the House accompanying S. 830, the Senate would disagree 
with amendment or amendments of the House, and the Senate would insist 
upon its amendment, agree to the request for a conference with the 
House, and finally the Chair would be authorized to appoint conferees 
on the part of the Senate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
