# REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

```
                     REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

  The filing date for 1997 third quarter mass mailings is October 27, 
1997. If your office did no mass mailings during this period, please 
submit a form that states ``none.''
  Mass mailing registrations, or negative reports, should be submitted 
to the Senate Office of Public Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, 
DC 20510-7116.
  The Public Records Office will be open from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the 
filing date to accept these filings. For further information, please 
contact the Public Records Office on (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
