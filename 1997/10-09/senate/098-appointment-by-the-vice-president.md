# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, in 
accordance with 22 U.S.C. 1928a-1928d, as amended, appoints the Senator 
from Utah [Mr. Bennett] as a member of the Senate Delegation to the 
North Atlantic Assembly during the First Session of the 105th Congress, 
to be held in Bucharest, Romania, October 9-14, 1997.
```
