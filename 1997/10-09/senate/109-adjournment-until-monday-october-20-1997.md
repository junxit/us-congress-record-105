# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, 1997

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 169

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, 1997

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under 
the provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 169, until 12 noon on 
Monday, October 20, 1997.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 6:57 p.m., adjourned until Monday, October 
20, 1997, at 12 noon.

                          ____________________
```
