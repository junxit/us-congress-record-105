# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 79, S. 156

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
INDIAN LAND CONVEYANCE/INDIAN TRUST FUND
Committee on Indian Affairs: Committee concluded hearings on the 
following bills:
  H.R. 79, to provide for the conveyance of certain land in the Six 
Rivers National Forest in the State of California for the benefit of 
the Hoopa Valley Tribe, after receiving testimony from Representative 
Riggs; Janice McDougle, Associate Deputy Chief, National Forest 
Systems, Forest Service, Department of Agriculture; and Duane J. 
Sherman, Sr., Hoopa Valley Tribal Council, Hoopa, California; and
  S. 156, to create a trust fund from the proceeds of the integrated 
programs of the Missouri River Basin Pick-Sloan Program to improve 
educational and healthcare facilities, the water system, and other 
basic necessities of the Lower Brule Sioux Tribe of South Dakota, after 
receiving testimony from Senator Johnson; Terry Virden, Director, 
Office of Trust Responsibility, Department of the Interior; and Michael 
B. Jandreau, Dorothy Langdeau Jones, and Altwin Grassrope, all of the 
Lower Brule Sioux Tribe, Lower Brule, South Dakota.
```
