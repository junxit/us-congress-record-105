# RECESS

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, seeing no one else in the Chamber, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate now stand in recess until 2 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:59 p.m. recessed until 2 
p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Roberts).
```
