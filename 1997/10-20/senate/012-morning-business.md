# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
continued period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour 2:30, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up 
to 5 minutes each. Who seeks time?
  The Senator from North Dakota is recognized.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for as 
much time as I consume in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is recognized, without objection.
```
