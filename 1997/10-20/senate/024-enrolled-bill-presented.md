# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 1000

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on October 10, 1997, he had 
presented to the President of the United States, the following enrolled 
bill:

       S. 1000. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     at 500 State Avenue in Kansas City, Kansas, as the ``Robert 
     J. Dole United States Courthouse.''
```
