# REREFERRAL OF H.R. 1249 TO THE COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND OVERSIGHT

**Speaking:** McHugh, John M. (M000472) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1249

```
   REREFERRAL OF H.R. 1249 TO THE COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND 
                               OVERSIGHT

  Mr. McHUGH. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Transportation and Infrastructure be discharged from further 
consideration of the bill, H.R. 1249, and that H.R. 1249 be rereferred 
to the Committee on Government Reform and Oversight.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
```
