# ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE CONFIRMATION OF REPRESENTATIVE THOMAS FOGLIETTA AS AMBASSADOR TO ITALY

**Speaking:** Murtha, John P. (M001120) [D-PA]

```
ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE CONFIRMATION OF REPRESENTATIVE THOMAS FOGLIETTA AS 
                          AMBASSADOR TO ITALY

  (Mr. MURTHA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. MURTHA. Mr. Speaker, it is my pleasure to announce to the House 
of Representatives that the gentleman from Pennsylvania [Mr. Foglietta] 
has now been confirmed by the other body, the Senate, as the Ambassador 
to Italy. The gentleman from Texas [Mr. DeLay] says, ``Make your 
reservations early.''

                          ____________________
```
