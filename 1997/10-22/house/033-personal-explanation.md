# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Shays, Christopher (S001144) [R-CT]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. SHAYS. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall vote No. 518, the Boehlert 
substitute, I was, believe it or not, in the Capitol chapel and missed 
my first vote since I became a Member of this body in 1987. 
Unfortunately, the battery in my pager was dead, and I was unaware that 
there was a vote. I know, ``My dog ate it.'' Had I been present, I 
would have voted ``aye.''
```
