# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, tomorrow morning following the period of 
morning business the Senate will conduct two consecutive rollcall votes 
beginning at 11 a.m. The first vote will be on cloture on the committee 
amendment to the ISTEA legislation, to be followed by a vote on passage 
of the continuing resolution.
  If cloture is not invoked at 11 a.m. on Thursday, it is hoped that 
the second cloture vote will occur Thursday afternoon. Therefore, 
Members can anticipate rollcall votes throughout Thursday's session of 
the Senate.
  It is the leader's hope that the Senate can make progress on the 
highway legislation--it is my hope, too, I might add--during tomorrow's 
session. In addition, if any appropriations conference reports become 
available, it would be expected that the Senate consider those reports 
in short order.

                          ____________________
```
