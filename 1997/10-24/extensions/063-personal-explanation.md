# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Visclosky, Peter J. (V000108) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 276, H.R. 2646

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. PETER J. VISCLOSKY

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 1997

  Mr. VISCLOSKY. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained and unable to 
vote on rollcall Nos. 523 through 525. Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``no'' on rollcall No. 523, the Rangel amendment to H.R. 2646; 
``no'' on rollcall No. 524, passage of H.R. 2646; and ``yes'' on 
rollcall No. 525, in support of House Resolution 276, offered by 
Democratic Leader Gephardt regarding the Sanchez-Dornan case.
```
