# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--CLINGER-COHEN ACT IMPLEMENTATION
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on 
Government Management, Information, and Technology, held a hearing on 
the Oversight of the Implementation of the Clinger-Cohen Act. Testimony 
was heard from Gene Dodaro, Assistant Comptroller General, Accounting 
and Information Management Division, GAO; Alan P. Balutis, Deputy Chief 
Information Officer, Department of Commerce; Liza McClenaghan, Chief 
Information Officer, Department of State; Anne Reed, Chief Information 
Officer, USDA; John P. Simpson, Deputy Assistant Secretary, Regulatory, 
Tariff, and Trade Enforcement, Department of the Treasury; Michael D. 
Cronin, Assistant Commissioner, Inspection, Immigration and 
Naturalization Service, Department of Justice; and Alan Proctor, Chief 
Information Officer, FTC.
```
