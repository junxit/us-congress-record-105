# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, it is my understanding that Senator Harkin 
does expect to address this subject on the Federal Reserve System 
nominations for a further 30 minutes at this time. It is also my hope 
the Senate could consider and confirm the nominations of our former 
colleague Senator Wyche Fowler and Thomas Foley for ambassadorial 
positions immediately following the previously scheduled 5 p.m. vote. I 
anticipate rollcall votes being necessary on these two ambassadorial 
nominations. Therefore, additional votes can be expected following the 
scheduled 5 p.m. vote.
```
