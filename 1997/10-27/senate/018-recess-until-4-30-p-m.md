# RECESS UNTIL 4:30 P.M.

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
[[Page S11233]]
                         RECESS UNTIL 4:30 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until 4:30 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 3:18 p.m., the Senate recessed until 4:29 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Allard).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Colorado, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
