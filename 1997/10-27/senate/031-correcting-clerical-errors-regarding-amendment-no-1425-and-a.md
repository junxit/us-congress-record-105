# CORRECTING CLERICAL ERRORS REGARDING AMENDMENT NO. 1425 AND AMENDMENT NO. 1424

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

```
 CORRECTING CLERICAL ERRORS REGARDING AMENDMENT NO. 1425 AND AMENDMENT 
                                NO. 1424

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I take this opportunity to state for the 
record that there is a misprint in the Congressional Record of October 
23, 1997, at page S11068. Due to a clerical error, my name is 
inadvertently listed as filing amendment No. 1425, which was filed by 
our colleague from Florida, Senator Graham. In addition, the name of 
our colleague from Illinois, Senator Moseley-Braun, was inadvertently 
not listed as an original cosponsor to amendment No. 1424.

                          ____________________
```
