Burt Lake Band as a distinct federally recognized Indian Tribe (H. 
Rept. 105-351);

  H. Res. 284, providing for consideration of H.R. 2493, to establish a 
mechanism by which the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of 
the Interior can provide for uniform management of livestock grazing on

construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy,

Forces.
