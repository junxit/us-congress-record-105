# MOTION TO ADJOURN

**Speaking:** Ensign, John (E000194) [R-NV]

```
                           MOTION TO ADJOURN

  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the motion.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       Mr. Ensign moves that the House do now adjourn.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion to adjourn 
offered by the gentleman from Nevada [Mr. Ensign].
  The question was taken; and the Speaker pro tempore announced that 
the noes appeared to have it.
  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. Speaker, on that, I demand the yeas and nays.
  The yeas and nays were refused.
  So the motion to adjourn was rejected.

                          ____________________
```
