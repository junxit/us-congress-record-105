# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2493, FORAGE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 284, H.R. 2493

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2493, FORAGE 
                        IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1997

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-355) on the resolution (H. Res. 284) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2493) to establish a 
mechanism by which the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of 
the Interior can provide for uniform management of livestock grazing on 
Federal lands, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
