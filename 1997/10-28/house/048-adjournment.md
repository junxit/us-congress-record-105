# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. FAZIO of California. Mr. Speaker, it is with great regret that I 
move that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable 
Walter H. Capps, our dear departed colleague.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 9 o'clock and 12 minutes 
p.m.), the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, October 29, 1997, 
at 10 a.m., in memory of the late Honorable Walter H. Capps of 
California.
```
