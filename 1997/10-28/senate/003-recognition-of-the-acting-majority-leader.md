# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The able acting majority leader, Senator 
Warner of Virginia, is recognized.
  Mr. WARNER. I thank the distinguished Presiding Officer, the 
President pro tempore of the Senate. I join with many others in 
recognizing how our distinguished senior Senator from South Carolina is 
always there present to open the U.S. Senate. That, in and of itself, 
is a record that merits the attention of all.

                          ____________________
```
