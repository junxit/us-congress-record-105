# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 139, H.R. 1479, H.R. 1484

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, 
the Chair will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules 
on which further proceedings were postponed yesterday in the order in 
which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  House Resolution 139, by the yeas and nays; H.R. 1484, de novo; and 
H.R. 1479, de novo.
  Pursuant to the order of the House today, the Chair will reduce to 5 
minutes the time for the first vote in this series.
```
