# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 286

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 286, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable Walter H. 
Capps.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 43 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 286, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, October 30, 1997, at 10 a.m. in memory of the late 
Honorable Walter H. Capps of California.
```
