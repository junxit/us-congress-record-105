# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2493, FORAGE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** Smith, Robert (S000607) [R-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 2493

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2493, 
                     FORAGE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1997

  Mr. SMITH of Oregon. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 2493, the Clerk be authorized to correct 
the table of contents, section numbers, punctuation, citations, and 
cross-references, and to make such other technical and conforming 
changes as may be necessary to reflect the actions of the House in 
amending the bill.

of the gentleman from Oregon?
  There was no objection.
```
