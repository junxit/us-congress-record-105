# REREFERRAL OF S. 459 TO THE COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION AND THE WORKFORCE

**Speaking:** Goodling, William F. (G000291) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S. 459

```
  REREFERRAL OF S. 459 TO THE COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION AND THE WORKFORCE

  Mr. GOODLING. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Resources be discharged from further consideration of the Senate 
bill, S. 459, and that the bill be rereferred to the Committee on 
Education and the Workforce. This bill amends and reauthorizes the 
Native American Programs Act of 1974.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore [Mr. Hefley]. Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
