# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS--S. 1319

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV], Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

**Measures:** S. 1319

```
                     ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS--S. 1319

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the name of Mr. 
Levin, Mr. Jeffords, and Mr. Leahy be added as cosponsors to S. 1319, a 
bill to repeal the Line-Item Veto Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
