# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Visclosky, Peter J. (V000108) [D-IN]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. PETER J. VISCLOSKY

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives
```
