# NO ELECTRONIC THEFT (NET) ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 2265

```
                     NO ELECTRONIC THEFT (NET) ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hansen). The pending business is the 
question de novo of suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 
2265, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from North Carolina [Mr. Coble], that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, H.R. 2265, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
```
