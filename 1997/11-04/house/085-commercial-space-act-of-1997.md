# COMMERCIAL SPACE ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 1702

```
                      COMMERCIAL SPACE ACT OF 1997

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is the question de novo 
of suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 1702, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from California [Mr. Rohrabacher] that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, H.R. 1702, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
