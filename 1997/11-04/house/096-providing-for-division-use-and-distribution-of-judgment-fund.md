# PROVIDING FOR DIVISION, USE, AND DISTRIBUTION OF JUDGMENT FUNDS OF OTTAWA AND CHIPPEWA INDIANS

**Measures:** H.R. 1604

```
  PROVIDING FOR DIVISION, USE, AND DISTRIBUTION OF JUDGMENT FUNDS OF 
                      OTTAWA AND CHIPPEWA INDIANS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is the question de novo 
of suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 1604, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Utah [Mr. Hansen] that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, H.R. 1604, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
