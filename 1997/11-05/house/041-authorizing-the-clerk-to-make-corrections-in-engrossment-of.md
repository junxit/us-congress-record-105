# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2676, INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE RESTRUCTURING AND REFORM ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2676

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2676, 
     INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE RESTRUCTURING AND REFORM ACT OF 1997

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 2676, the Clerk of the House be 
authorized to correct section numbers, punctuation, and cross-
references, and to make such other technical and conforming changes as 
may be necessary to reflect the actions of this House in amending H.R. 
2676.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore [Mr. Pease]. Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
