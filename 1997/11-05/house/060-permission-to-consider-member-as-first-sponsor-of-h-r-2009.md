# PERMISSION TO CONSIDER MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2009

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2009

```
                              {time}  1730
      PERMISSION TO CONSIDER MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2009

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 2009, a bill initially 
introduced by former Representative Capps of California, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 4 of rule XXII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Calvert). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
```
