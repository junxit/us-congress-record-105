# SENATE RESOLUTION 143--TO AUTHORIZE A PRINTING

**Measures:** S.Res. 143

```
             SENATE RESOLUTION 143--TO AUTHORIZE A PRINTING

  Mr. WARNER (for himself and Mr. Ford) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 143

       Resolved, That the Committee on Rules and Administration is 
     directed to prepare a revised edition of the Senate Election 
     Law Guidebook, Senate Document 104-12, and that such document 
     shall be printed as a Senate document.
       Sec. 2. There shall be printed 600 additional copies of the 
     document specified in section 1 of this resolution for the 
     use of the Committee on Rules and Administration.

                          ____________________
```
