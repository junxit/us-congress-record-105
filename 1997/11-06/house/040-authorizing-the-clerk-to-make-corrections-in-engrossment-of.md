# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF ANY MEASURE MADE IN ORDER UNDER HOUSE RESOLUTION 302

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 302

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF ANY MEASURE 
                MADE IN ORDER UNDER HOUSE RESOLUTION 302

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Clerk be 
authorized to make technical corrections in the engrossment of any 
measure made in order under House Resolution 302, to include 
corrections in spelling, punctuation, section numbering, and cross-
referencing, and to make such other technical and conforming changes as 
may be required to reflect the actions of the House.

[[Page H10130]]

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Regula). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
