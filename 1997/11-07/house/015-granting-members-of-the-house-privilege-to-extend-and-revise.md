# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD UNTIL LAST EDITION IS PUBLISHED

**Speaking:** Goss, Porter J. (G000336) [R-FL]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN 
          CONGRESSIONAL RECORD UNTIL LAST EDITION IS PUBLISHED

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that Members may have 
until publication of the last edition of the Congressional Record 
authorized for the first session by the Joint Committee on Printing to 
revise and extend their remarks and to include brief, related 
extraneous material on any matter occurring before the adjournment of 
the first session sine die.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
