# DESIGNATION OF HON. STEVEN C. LaTOURETTE TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE ON TODAY

```
DESIGNATION OF HON. STEVEN C. LaTOURETTE TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
                                ON TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

[[Page H10319]]

                                               Washington, DC.

                                                 November 7, 1997.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Steven C. LaTourette to 
     act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions on this day.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the designation is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
