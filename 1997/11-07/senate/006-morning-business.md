# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10:30 with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each. The time until 10 o'clock shall be under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee; in his absence, the Senator from 
Wyoming may proceed.

                          ____________________
```
