# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE], Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```

  Mr. ROTH. I ask unanimous consent that there now be a period of 
morning business until 1 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up 
to 10 minutes each.

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I make a point of order a quorum is not 
present.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
