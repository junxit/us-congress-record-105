# CONGRATULATIONS TO ANNA TAYLOR CELEBRATING HER 100th BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Ashcroft, John (A000356) [R-MO]

```
     CONGRATULATIONS TO ANNA TAYLOR CELEBRATING HER 100th BIRTHDAY

  Mr. ASHCROFT. Mr. President, I rise today to encourage my colleagues 
to join me in congratulating Anna Taylor of Grandview, MO, who will 
celebrate her 100th birthday on November 22. Anna is a truly remarkable 
individual. Anna has witnessed many of the events that have shaped our 
Nation into the greatest the world has ever known. The longevity of 
Anna's life has meant much more, however, to the many relatives and 
friends whose lives she has touched over the last 100 years.

  Anna's celebration of 100 years of life is a testament to me and all 
Missourians. Her achievements are significant and deserve to be 
recognized. I would like to join Anna's many friends and relatives in 
wishing her health and happiness in the future.

                          ____________________
```
