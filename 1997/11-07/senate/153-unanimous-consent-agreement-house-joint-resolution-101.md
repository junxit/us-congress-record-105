# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--House Joint Resolution 101

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 101

```
        UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--House Joint Resolution 101

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
receives House Joint Resolution 101 making continuing appropriations 
through Sunday, the joint resolution be agreed to and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table, all without further action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
