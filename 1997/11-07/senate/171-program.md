# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. Tomorrow the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business from 12 noon to 1 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate 
intends to consider and complete action on the following: The labor-
HHS. appropriations conference report, D.C. appropriations bill, the 
FDA reform conference report, the adoption-foster-care legislation, and 
any other appropriations legislation cleared for action. Therefore, 
Members can anticipate rollcall votes throughout Saturday's session of 
the Senate. However, I would expect votes would not occur before 1 p.m.
```
