# WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4(b) OF RULE XI WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS REPORTED BY COMMITTEE ON RULES

**Measures:** H.Res. 314

```
     WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4(b) OF RULE XI WITH RESPECT TO 
  CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS REPORTED BY COMMITTEE ON RULES

  Mr. SOLOMON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-394) on the resolution (H. Res. 314) waiving a
```
