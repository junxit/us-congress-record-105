# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet on Saturday, November 7, 1997, 
at 1:30 p.m. in open session, to receive testimony on the nomination of 
William J. Lynn III, to be Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
