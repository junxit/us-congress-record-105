# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, tomorrow it is the hope that the omnibus 
appropriations bill will be cleared for action by the Senate. A 
rollcall vote is anticipated. However, I would not expect that vote to 
occur prior to 1:30 p.m.
  The Senate intends to consider and complete action on the following: 
the FDA reform conference report and legislative or executive items 
cleared for action. Therefore, Members can anticipate rollcall votes 
throughout Sunday's session of the Senate.
```
