# THE 50 STATES COMMEMORATIVE COIN PROGRAM ACT

**Measures:** S. 1228

```
              THE 50 STATES COMMEMORATIVE COIN PROGRAM ACT

                                 ______
                                 

               D'AMATO (AND SARBANES) AMENDMENT NO. 1631

  Mr. SESSIONS (for Mr. D'Amato, for himself and Mr. Sarbanes) proposed 
an amendment to the bill (S. 1228) to provide for a 10-year circulating 
commemorative coin program to commemorate each of the 50 States, and 
for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 14, strike lines 4 through 10.
       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC.  . RULE OF CONSTRUCTION.

       Nothing in this Act or the amendments made by this Act 
     shall be construed to evidence any intention to eliminate or 
     to limit the printing or circulation of United States 
     currency in the $1 denomination.
                                 ______
                                 

                        COATS AMENDMENT NO. 1632

1228, supra; as follows:

       On page 8, line 11, strike ``clad''.
```
