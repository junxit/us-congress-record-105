# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 282, H.R. 681, H.R. 1057, H.R. 1058, H.R. 2129

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed en bloc to the following bills: Calendar No. 259, H.R. 282; 
calendar No. 262, H.R. 681; calendar No. 263, H.R. 2129; calendar No. 
211, H.R. 1057; and calendar No. 212, H.R. 1058.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the bills be considered read 
three times and passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the bills be placed in the 
Record at the appropriate place, with the preceding all done en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
