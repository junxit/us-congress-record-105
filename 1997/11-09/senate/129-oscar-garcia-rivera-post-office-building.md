# OSCAR GARCIA RIVERA POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 282

```
                OSCAR GARCIA RIVERA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 282) to designate the U.S. Post Office Building located 
at 153 East 110th Street, New York, NY, as the ``Oscar Garcia Rivera 
Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, 
read the third time, and passed.
```
