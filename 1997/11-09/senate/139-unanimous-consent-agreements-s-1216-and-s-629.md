# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS--S. 1216 AND S. 629

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S. 629, S. 1216

```
            UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS--S. 1216 AND S. 629

  Mr. SESSIONS. I ask unanimous consent that S. 1216, as reported by 
the Finance Committee, be referred to the Commerce Committee for the 
consideration of matters within its jurisdiction for a period not to 
exceed 10 calendar days. I further ask consent if the bill is not 
reported at that time, the bill be immediately discharged and placed on 
the calendar.
  I further ask unanimous consent that S. 629 be discharged from the 
Commerce Committee and that the bill then be referred to the Senate 
Finance Committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
