# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2513

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2513

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2513

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 2513 is at the 
desk, and I now ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2513) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to restore and modify the provision of the Taxpayer 
     Relief Act of 1997 relating to exempting active financing 
     income from foreign personal holding company and to provide 
     for the nonrecognition of gain on the sale of stock in 
     agricultural processors to certain farmers' cooperatives, and 
     for other purposes.

  Mr. SESSIONS. I now ask for its second reading, and object to my own 
request on behalf of the other side of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
