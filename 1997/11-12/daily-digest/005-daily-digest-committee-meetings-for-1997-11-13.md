that assures destruction of Iraq's ability to produce and deliver 
weapons of mass destruction, and that peaceful and diplomatic 
efforts should be pursued, but that if such efforts fail, 
multilateral military action or, as a last resort, unilateral United

  Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure, Subcommittee on
