# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
the State of Indiana, asks unanimous consent that there now be a period 
of morning business until 3 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  Is there objection? The Chair hears none, and it is so ordered.
  In my capacity as a Senator from the State of Indiana, I suggest the 
absence of a quorum. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Santorum). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
