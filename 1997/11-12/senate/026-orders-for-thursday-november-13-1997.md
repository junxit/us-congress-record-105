# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 1997

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 1997

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand in adjournment until the 
hour of 10 a.m. on Thursday, November 13. I further ask unanimous 
consent that on Thursday, immediately following the opening prayer, the 
routine requests through the morning hour be granted and that the 
Senate proceed to a period of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
