# ANNOUNCEMENT OF LEGISLATION TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE RULES TODAY

**Speaking:** Linder, John (L000321) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 314, S. 562, S. 738, H.R. 3025

```
 ANNOUNCEMENT OF LEGISLATION TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE 
                              RULES TODAY

  Mr. LINDER. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 314, the 
following suspensions are expected to be considered today:
  S. 738, Amtrak Reform and Accountability Act of 1997;
  S. 562, Senior Citizen Home Equity Protection Act;
  H.R. 3025, a bill to repeal the Federal charter of group 
hospitalization and medical services;
  And the FDA reform bill.
```
