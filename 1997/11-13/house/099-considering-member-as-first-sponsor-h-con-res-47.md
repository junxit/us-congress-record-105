# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR H. CON. RES. 47

**Speaking:** Johnson, Jay W. (J000149) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 47

```
          CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR H. CON. RES. 47

  Mr. JOHNSON of Wisconsin. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent I 
might hereafter be considered as first sponsor of House Concurrent 
Resolution 47, a bill originally represented by the gentleman from 
Pennsylvania [Mr. Foglietta] of Pennsylvania, for the purpose of adding 
cosponsors and requesting reprints pursuant to clause 4 of rule XXII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
```
