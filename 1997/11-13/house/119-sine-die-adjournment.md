# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Pease, Edward A. (P000171) [R-IN]

**Measures:** S. 68, H.Con.Res. 68

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. PEASE. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 68, 
and as the designee of the majority leader, I move that the House do 
now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In accordance with the provisions of Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 68, the Chair declares the 1st session of the 
105th Congress adjourned sine die.
  Thereupon (at 10 o'clock and 44 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 68, the House adjourned.

                          ____________________
```
