# SENATE RESOLUTION 161--AMENDING SENATE RESOLUTION 48

**Measures:** S.Res. 48, S.Res. 161

```
          SENATE RESOLUTION 161--AMENDING SENATE RESOLUTION 48

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to.

                              S. Res. 161

       Resolved, That Senate Resolution 48, 105th Congress, agreed 
     to February 4, 1997, is amended--
       (1) in section 1(e), by striking ``$5,000'' and inserting 
     ``$10,000''; and
       (2) in sections 1(e) and 1(g), by striking ``September 30, 
     1997'' and inserting ``September 30, 1998''.

                          ____________________
```
