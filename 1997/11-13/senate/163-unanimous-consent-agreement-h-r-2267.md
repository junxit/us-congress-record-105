# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2267

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2267

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2267

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 2267, the appropriations bill for 
the Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the Judiciary and 
Related Agencies is received, if it is identical to the document filed 
earlier today, it be deemed agreed to and the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, all without further action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
