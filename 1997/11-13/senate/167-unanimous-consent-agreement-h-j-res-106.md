# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 106

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 106

```
               UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 106

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
receives House Joint Resolution 106, the continuing resolution, that it 
be considered read three times and passed, and that the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
