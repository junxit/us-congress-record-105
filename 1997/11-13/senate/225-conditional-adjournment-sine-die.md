# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 39, S.Con.Res. 68

```
                    CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

  Mr. LOTT. Now, Mr. President, before any other bills come to our 
attention, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment 
sine die of the 1st session of the 105th Congress under the provisions 
of S. Con Res. 68 and S. J. Res. 39 until Tuesday, January 27, 1998, 
provided that the House adopts S. Con. Res. 68 and does not alter the 
text of the State-Justice-Commerce Appropriations Conference Report.
  If either action occurs, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
reconvene on Friday, November 14, 1997, at 10 a.m.
  There being no objection, at 7:56 p.m., the Senate adjourned sine 
die.

                          ____________________
```
