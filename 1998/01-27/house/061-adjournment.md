# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Duncan, John J., Jr. (D000533) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 338

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. DUNCAN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 338, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable Sonny 
Bono.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 31 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 338, and under its previous order, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, January 28, 1998, at 1 
p.m. in memory of the late Honorable Sonny Bono.
```
