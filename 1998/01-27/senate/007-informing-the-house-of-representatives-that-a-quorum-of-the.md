# INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A QUORUM OF THE SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 165

```
 INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A QUORUM OF THE SENATE IS 
                               ASSEMBLED

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I now send a second resolution to the desk 
informing the House that a quorum is present and ask that it be 
considered and agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 165) was agreed to, as follows:

[[Page S2]]

                              S. Res. 165

       Resolved, That the Secretary inform the House of 
     Representatives that a quorum of the Senate is assembled and 
     that the Senate is ready to proceed to business.

                          ____________________
```
