# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES TO HEAR AN ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES TO HEAR AN ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that the President of the Senate be 
authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate to join a 
committee on the part of the House to escort the President of the 
United States into the House Chamber for the joint session to be held 
at 9 p.m. this evening, Tuesday, January 27, 1998.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
