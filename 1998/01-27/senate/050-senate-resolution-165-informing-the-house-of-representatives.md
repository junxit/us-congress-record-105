# SENATE RESOLUTION 165--INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A QUORUM OF SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

**Measures:** S.Res. 165

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 165--INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A 
                     QUORUM OF SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

  Mr. LOTT (for himself and Mr. Daschle) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 165

       Resolved, That the Secretary inform the House of 
     Representatives that a quorum of the Senate is assembled and 
     that the Senate is ready to proceed to business.
```
