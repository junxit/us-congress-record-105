# 1997 YEAR END REPORT

```
                          1997 YEAR END REPORT

  The mailing and filing date of the 1997 Year End Report required by 
the Federal Election Campaign Act, as amended, is Saturday, January 31, 
1998. Principal campaign committees supporting Senate candidates file 
their reports with the Senate Office of Public Records, 232 Hart 
Building, Washington, D.C. 20510-7116.
  The Public Records office will be open from 12:00 noon to 4:00 p.m. 
on the filing date to accept these filings. For further information, 
please contact the Public Records office on (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
