# ORDER FOR RECESS AND ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                    ORDER FOR RECESS AND ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it stand in 
recess until the hour of 8:30 p.m. this evening for the joint session 
of Congress.
  Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that immediately 
following the conclusion of the joint session, the Senate automatically 
stand in adjournment until 10:30 a.m., Wednesday, January 28.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
