# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--MESSAGE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--MESSAGE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                                 STATES

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand the Senate will proceed as a 
body to the Hall of the House of Representatives to hear the State of 
the Union address. I believe we will be leaving in about 4 or 5 
minutes, so I will yield the floor so we can assemble and go to the 
other body. I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will proceed to the Hall of the 
House of Representatives.
  Thereupon, at 8:47 p.m., the Senate, preceded by the Secretary of the 
Senate, Gary Sisco, the Vice President, and the President pro tempore 
(Mr. Thurmond), proceeded to the Hall of the House of Representatives 
to hear the address by the President of the United States.
  (The address by the President of the United States, this day 
delivered by him to the joint session of the two Houses of Congress, 
appears in the proceedings of the House of Representatives in today's 
Record.)

                          ____________________
```
