# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE BUDGET

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 342

```
             ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE BUDGET

  Mr. FAZIO of California. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H.Res. 
342) and ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives:
       To the Committee on Budget, David Price of North Carolina.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
