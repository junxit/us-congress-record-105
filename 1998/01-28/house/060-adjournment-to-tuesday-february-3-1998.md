# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 1998

**Speaking:** Owens, Major R. (O000159) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 201

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 1998

  Mr. OWENS. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Snowbarger). Pursuant to the provisions 
of House Concurrent Resolution 201, 105th Congress, the House stands 
adjourned until 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, February 3, 1998, for morning 
hour debates.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 5 o'clock and 25 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 201, the House adjourned 
until Tuesday, February 3, 1998, at 12:30 p.m., for morning hour 
debates.

                          ____________________
```
