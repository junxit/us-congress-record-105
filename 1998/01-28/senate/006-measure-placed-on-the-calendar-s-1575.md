# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1575

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S. 1575

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1575

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Hagel). The clerk will report.

       A bill (S. 1575) to rename the Washington National Airport

     Reagan National Airport.''

bill at this point.
```
