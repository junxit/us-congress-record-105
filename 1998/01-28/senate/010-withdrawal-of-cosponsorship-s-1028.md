# WITHDRAWAL OF COSPONSORSHIP--S. 1028

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 1028

```
                  WITHDRAWAL OF COSPONSORSHIP--S. 1028

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that my name be 
removed from S. 1028 as a cosponsor of that legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. BOXER. Thank you very much, Mr. President. This is a forest bill 
that is very controversial. After I placed my name on it a study came 
out that basically, in my opinion, led me to believe that the bill in 
its current form would not be good for the Nation's forests.

                          ____________________
```
