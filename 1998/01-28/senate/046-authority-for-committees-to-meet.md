# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, the Finance Committee requests unanimous 
consent to conduct a hearing on Wednesday, January 28, 1998 beginning 
at 10:00 a.m. in room 215 Dirksen.
  Also hearing on confirmation on pending nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                          committee on finance

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, 
January 28, 1998, in open session, to receive testimony on the report 
and recommendations of the National Defense.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                    select committee on intelligence

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Select 
Committee on Intelligence be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Wednesday, January 28, 1998 at 9:30 a.m. to hold an open 
hearing and at 2:30 to hold a closed hearing.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
