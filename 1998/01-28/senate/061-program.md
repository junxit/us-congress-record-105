# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Madam President, tomorrow morning the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 12 noon. At 12 noon, it is hoped 
that the Senate will be able to proceed to the consideration of either 
the Ronald Reagan airport legislation or a resolution regarding Iraq. 
Rollcall votes are therefore expected during Thursday's session of the 
Senate. As always, the Senate may also consider any other legislative 
or executive items cleared for action.

                          ____________________
```
