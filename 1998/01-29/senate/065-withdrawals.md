# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive messages transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
January 29, 1998, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:


                  federal emergency management agency

       James Hudson Bailey, of Wisconsin, to be Deputy Director of 
     the Federal Emergency Management Agency, vice Harvey G. 
     Ryland, resigned, which was sent to the Senate on October 8, 
     1997.


 morris k. udall scholarship and excellence in national environmental 
                           policy foundation

       Ronald Kent Burton, of Virginia, to be a Member of the 
     Board of Trustees of the Morris K. Udall Scholarship and 
     Excellence in National Environmental Policy Foundation for a 
     term expiring October 6, 2002, (Reappointment), which was 
     sent to the Senate on January 9, 1997.
```
