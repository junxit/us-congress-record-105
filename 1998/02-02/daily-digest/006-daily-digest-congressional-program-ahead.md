# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** H.R. 1085, H.R. 1271, H.R. 2625, H.R. 2631, H.R. 2846

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                   Week of February 2 through 6, 1998

                             House Chamber

  Monday, the House was not in session.
  Tuesday, Consideration of 2 Suspensions:
  H.R. 1271, FAA Research, Engineering and Development Authorization 
Act of 1997; and
  H.R. 1085, to Revise, Codify and Enact Without Substantive Change 
Certain Laws Related to Patriotic and National Observances, Ceremonies, 
and Organizations;
  Note: The House will meet at 12:30 p.m. for morning hour and at 2:00 
p.m. for legislative business. No recorded votes are expected before 
5:00 p.m.
  Wednesday and Thursday, Consideration of H.R. 2625, ``The Ronald 
Reagan National Airport'' (Subject to a Rule);
  Consideration of H.J. Res.  , Concerning Attorneys' Fees, Costs, and 
Sanctions Payable by the White House Health Care Task Force (Subject to 
a Rule);
  Consideration of H.R. 2846, to Prohibit Spending Federal Education 
Funds on National Testing (Subject to a Rule); and
  Consideration of H.R. 2631, Consideration of President's Veto of Act 
Disapproving his Cancellations on Military Construction Appropriations 
Act.
  Friday, the House is not in Session.
```
