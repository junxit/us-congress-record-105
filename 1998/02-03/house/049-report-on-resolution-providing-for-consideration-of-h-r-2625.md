# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2625, RONALD REAGAN WASHINGTON NATIONAL AIRPORT

**Measures:** H.Res. 344, H.R. 2625

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2625, RONALD 
                   REAGAN WASHINGTON NATIONAL AIRPORT

  Mr. Goss, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-411) on the resolution (H. Res. 344) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2625) to redesignate Washington 
National Airport as ``Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport,'' 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
