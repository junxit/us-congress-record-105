# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 107, CONCERNING ATTORNEYS' FEES, COSTS, AND SANCTIONS PAYABLE BY THE WHITE HOUSE HEALTH CARE TASK FORCE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 107, H.Res. 345

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 107, 
 CONCERNING ATTORNEYS' FEES, COSTS, AND SANCTIONS PAYABLE BY THE WHITE 
                      HOUSE HEALTH CARE TASK FORCE

  Mr. Goss, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-412) on the resolution (H. Res. 345) providing for 
consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 107) expressing the 
sense of the Congress that the award of attorney's fees, costs, and 
sanctions of $285,864.78 ordered by United States District Judge Royce 
C. Lamberth on December 18, 1997, should not be paid with taxpayer 
funds, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
