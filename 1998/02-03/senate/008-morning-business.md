# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for not to 
exceed 5 minutes each. Under the previous order, the time between 9:30 
and 10:30 a.m. shall be under the control of the Senator from North 
Dakota, Mr. Dorgan, or his designee.

  The Senator from North Dakota is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
