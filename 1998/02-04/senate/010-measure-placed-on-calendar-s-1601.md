# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1601

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 1601

```
                  MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1601

  Mr. LOTT. I understand the cloning bill is at the desk awaiting 
second reading by the clerk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is correct. The clerk will 
report the bill.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1601) to amend Title 18 United States Code to 
     prohibit the use of somatic cell nuclear transfer technology 
     for the purposes of human cloning.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I object to further consideration of this 
bill at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
