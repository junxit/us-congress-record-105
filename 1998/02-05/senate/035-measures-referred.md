# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 107, H.R. 2846

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following joint resolution was read the first and second times by 
unanimous consent and referred as indicated:

       H.J. Res. 107. Joint resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Congress that the award of attorney's fees, costs, and 
     sanctions of $285,864.78 ordered by United States District 
     Judge Royce C. Lamberth on December 18, 1997, should not be 
     paid with taxpayer funds; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

  The following bill was read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 2846. An act to prohibit spending Federal education 
     funds on national testing without explicit and specific 
     legislation; to the Committee on Labor and Human Resources.

                          ____________________
```
