# VETO MESSAGE ON H.R. 2631

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2631

```
                       VETO MESSAGE ON H.R. 2631

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the veto 
message to accompany H.R. 2631, the military construction 
appropriations bill, be deemed read and, as the Constitution provides, 
be spread upon the journal; and that the majority leader, after 
consultation with the minority leader, be authorized to proceed to the 
reconsideration of the said bill, the objections of the President of 
the United States to the contrary notwithstanding.

[[Page S466]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Roberts). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I would indicate that that vote will probably not occur 
until after the recess coming up at the end of this week.
  I yield the floor, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
