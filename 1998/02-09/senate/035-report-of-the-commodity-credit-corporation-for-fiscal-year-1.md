# REPORT OF THE COMMODITY CREDIT CORPORATION FOR FISCAL YEAR 1995-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 95

```
   REPORT OF THE COMMODITY CREDIT CORPORATION FOR FISCAL YEAR 1995--
                   MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 95

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, 
and Forestry.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by the provisions of section 13, Public Law 806, 80th 
Congress (15 U.S.C. 714k), I transmit herewith the report of the 
Commodity Credit Corporation for fiscal year 1995.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, February 5, 1998.

                          ____________________
```
