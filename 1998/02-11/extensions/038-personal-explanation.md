# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 348, H.R. 2631, H.R. 2846

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAN BURTON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 11, 1998

  Mr. BURTON of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, on February 5, 1998, I was 
unavoidably detained, therefore, missing roll call votes 8-10. Had I 
been here I would have voted Yea on roll call vote 8 (H. Res. 348) 
providing for the consideration of H.R. 2846; Yea on roll call vote 9 
(H.R. 2846) prohibiting spending Federal education funds on national 
testing without explicit and specific legislation; and Yea on roll call 
10 (H.R. 2631) disapproving the cancellations transmitted by the 
President on October 6, 1997, regarding Public Law 105-45.
```
