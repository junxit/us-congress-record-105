# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON MAINTAINING UNITED STATES NUCLEAR WEAPONS EXPERTISE

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON MAINTAINING UNITED STATES 
                       NUCLEAR WEAPONS EXPERTISE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 3162(b) of Public Law 104-201, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members on the 
part of the House to the Commission on Maintaining United States 
Nuclear Weapons Expertise:
  Mr. Robert B. Barker of California and Mr. Roland F. Herbst of 
California.
  There was no objection.
```
