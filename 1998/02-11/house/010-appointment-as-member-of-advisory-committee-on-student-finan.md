# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL 
                               ASSISTANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 491 of the Higher Education Act, as amended by 
section 407 of Public Law 99-498, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Member on the part of the House to the 
Advisory Committee on Student Financial Assistance for a 3-year term.
  Mr. Henry Gibbons of Missouri.
  There was no objection.
```
