# WELCOME BACK TO MS. HELEN SEWELL

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

```
                    WELCOME BACK TO MS. HELEN SEWELL

  (Mr. SOLOMON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, as we welcome everybody back, I would like 
to make note that back in the kitchen, in the Republican cloakroom on 
this side, we have a wonderful woman who has been an employee of this 
House of Representatives for more than 65 years. Sixty-five years. She 
was sick over the break and she has returned in good health and we just 
want to welcome Mrs. Helen Sewell back. A wonderful, wonderful woman.

                          ____________________
```
