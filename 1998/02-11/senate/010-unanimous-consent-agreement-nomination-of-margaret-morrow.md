# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF MARGARET MORROW

**Speaking:** Snowe, Olympia J. (S000663) [R-ME]

```
       UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF MARGARET MORROW

  Ms. SNOWE. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that at 1 p.m. today the Senate proceed to executive session to 
consider the nomination of Margaret Morrow and a vote occur at 6 p.m. 
this evening with the time equally divided between Senators Hatch and 
Ashcroft or their designees.
  This request has been cleared by the minority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
