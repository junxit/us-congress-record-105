# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 1998

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 201

```
               ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 1998

  Mr. PAUL. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Pitts). Pursuant to the provisions of 
House Concurrent Resolution 201, 105th Congress, the House stands 
adjourned until 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, February 24, 1998.
  Thereupon (at 5 o'clock and 35 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 201, the House adjourned until Tuesday, February 
24, 1998, at 12:30 p.m. for morning hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
