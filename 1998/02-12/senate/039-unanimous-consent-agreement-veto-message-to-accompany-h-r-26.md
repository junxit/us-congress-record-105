# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--VETO MESSAGE TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 2631

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2631

```
    UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--VETO MESSAGE TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 2631

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 11:30 
a.m. on Wednesday, February 25, the Senate proceed to the consideration 
of the veto message to accompany H.R. 2631, the Military Construction 
Appropriations bill. I further ask unanimous consent that there be one 
hour for debate on the message, equally divided between the chairman 
and the ranking Member, with an additional hour under the control of 
Senator McCain. I further ask unanimous consent that following the 
expiration or yielding back of time, the Senate proceed to a vote on 
the veto message with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
