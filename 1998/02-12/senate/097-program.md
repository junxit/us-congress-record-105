# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, in conjunction with the previous 
unanimous consent agreements, tomorrow the Senate will be in a pro 
forma session only. Upon the return from the President's Day recess on 
February 23, the Senate will reconvene at 12 noon, and following 
Senator Landrieu's reading of George Washington's Address, the Senate 
will be in a period for morning business until 3 p.m. No rollcall votes 
will occur during the Monday, February 23, session of the Senate. 
Members can anticipate rollcall votes after 2:15 p.m. on Tuesday, 
February 24.

                          ____________________
```
