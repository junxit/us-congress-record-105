# SENATOR RIBICOFF

**Speaking:** Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN]

```
                            SENATOR RIBICOFF

Connecticut, I imagine he came to the floor to speak about Senator 
Ribicoff. I will not be long. I will say, although I did not have a

great Senator. I pass on my sympathy and love to the State of
```
