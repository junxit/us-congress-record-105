# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AT CEREMONIES IN OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY.

```
     APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AT CEREMONIES IN OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE 
                         WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House on 
Thursday, February 12, 1998, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to represent the 
House of Representatives at wreath-laying ceremonies at the Washington 
Monument for the observance of George Washington's birthday held on 
Monday, February 23, 1998:
  Mr. Davis of Virginia.
  Mr. Hoyer of Maryland.

                          ____________________
```
