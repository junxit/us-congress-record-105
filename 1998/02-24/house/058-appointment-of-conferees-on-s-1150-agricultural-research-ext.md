# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON S. 1150, AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH, EXTENSION, AND EDUCATION REFORM ACT of 1998

**Measures:** S. 1150

```
APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON S. 1150, AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH, EXTENSION, 
                    AND EDUCATION REFORM ACT of 1998

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair appoints the 
following conferees on the Senate bill (S 1150) to ensure that 
federally funded agricultural research, extension, and education 
address high-priority concerns with national multistate significance, 
to reform, extend, and eliminate certain agricultural research 
programs, and for other purposes:
  Messrs. Smith of Oregon,
  Combest,
  Barrett of Nebraska,
  Stenholm, and
  Dooley of California.
  There was no objection.
```
