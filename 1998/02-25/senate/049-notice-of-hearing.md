# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet in SR-301, Russell Senate Office 
Building, on Wednesday, March 4, 1998 at 9:30 a.m. to conduct an 
oversight hearing on the FY '99 budget and operations of the Library of 
Congress, and to review the reauthorization of the American Folklife 
Center.
```
