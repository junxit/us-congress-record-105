# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON SMALL BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Bonior, David E. (B000619) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 370

```
         ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON SMALL BUSINESS

  Mr. BONIOR. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 370), and ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 370

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and that 
     they are hereby, elected to the following standing committees 
     of the House of Representatives:
       Committee on Small Business: Ms. Velazquez to rank directly 
     above Mr. LaFalce.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
