# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 371

```
          ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 371), and ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

       Resolved, That the following Member be, and he is hereby, 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on the Judiciary: Mr. Graham of South Carolina.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
