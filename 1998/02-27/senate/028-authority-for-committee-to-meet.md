# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** S. 1578

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Rules and Administration be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Friday, February 27, 1998, beginning at 9:30 a.m. until 
business is completed, to receive testimony on S.1578, and to hold an 
oversight hearing on the budget requests and operations of the 
Government Printing Office, the National Gallery of Art, and the 
Congressional Research Service.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
