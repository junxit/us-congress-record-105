# 1998 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1997 ANNUAL REPORT ON TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
  1998 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1997 ANNUAL REPORT ON TRADE AGREEMENTS 
        PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Coble) laid before the House the 
following message from the President of the United States; which was 
read and, together with the accompanying papers, without objection, 
referred to the Committee on Ways and Means:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1998 Trade Policy Agenda and 1997 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, February 28, 1998.

                          ____________________
```
