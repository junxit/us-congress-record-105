# REPORT OF THE 1998 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1997 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 101

```
 REPORT OF THE 1998 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1997 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE 
      TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 101

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1998 Trade Policy Agenda and 1997 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, February 28, 1998.

                          ____________________
```
