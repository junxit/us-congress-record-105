# RECESS

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having arrived, the Senate now stands in recess.
  Thereupon, at 12:34 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Coats).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Indiana, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
