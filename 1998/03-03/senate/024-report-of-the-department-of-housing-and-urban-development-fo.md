# REPORT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 103

```
REPORT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT FOR CALENDAR 
             YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 103

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
Urban Affairs.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the requirements of 42 U.S.C. 3536, I transmit herewith 
the 32d Annual Report of the Department of Housing and Urban 
Development, which covers calendar year 1996.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 3, 1998.

                          ____________________
```
