# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2369, WIRELESS PRIVACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1998

**Measures:** H.Res. 377, H.R. 2369

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2369, WIRELESS 
                    PRIVACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1998

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-427) on the resolution (H. Res. 377) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2369) to amend the 
Communications Act of 1934 to strengthen and clarify prohibitions on 
electronic eavesdropping, and for other purposes, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
