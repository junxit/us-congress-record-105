# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO AMTRAK REFORM COUNCIL

```
          APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO AMTRAK REFORM COUNCIL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 203(b)(1) of Public Law 105-134, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following individuals on the 
part of the House to the Amtrak Reform Council for a term of 5 years:
  Mrs. Christine Todd Whitman of New Jersey;
  Mr. Bruce Chapman of Washington; and
  Mr. Christopher Gleason of Pennsylvania.
  There was no objection.
```
