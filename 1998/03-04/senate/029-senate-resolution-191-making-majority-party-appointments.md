# SENATE RESOLUTION--191--MAKING MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS

**Measures:** S.Res. 39, S.Res. 191

```
       SENATE RESOLUTION--191--MAKING MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS

  Mr. CHAFEE (for Mr. Lott) submitted the following resolution; which 
was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 191

       Resolved,
       Sec. 1. That the following be the majority membership on 
     the Committee on Governmental Affairs for the remainder of 
     the 105th Congress, or until their successors are appointed, 
     pursuant to section 2 of this resolution:
       Governmental Affairs: Mr. Thompson (Chairman), Mr. Roth, 
     Mr. Stevens, Ms. Collins, Mr. Brownback, Mr. Domenici, Mr. 
     Cochran, Mr. Nickles, and Mr. Specter.
       Sec. 2. That section 1 of this resolution shall take effect 
     immediately upon the filing of the report by the Committee on 
     Governmental Affairs as required by Senate Resolution 39, 
     agreed to March 11, 1997.

                          ____________________
```
