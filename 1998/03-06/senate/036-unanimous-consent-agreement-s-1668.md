# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1668

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 1668

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1668

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader, after consultation with the Democratic leader, may proceed to 
the consideration of S. 1668, relating to disclosure of certain 
classified information. I further ask unanimous consent that there be 
20 minutes for debate on the bill, equally divided between the chairman 
and ranking member. I ask unanimous consent that no amendments or 
motions be in order to the bill and, at the conclusion or yielding back 
of debate time, the bill be read the third time and set aside. I 
finally ask unanimous consent that a vote on passage of S. 1668 occur 
at a time to be determined by the majority leader, after consultation 
with the Democratic leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
