# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                   Washington, DC, March 10, 1998.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Tim 
     Hutchinson, a Senator from the State of Arkansas, to perform 
     the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Strom Thurmond,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. HUTCHINSON thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
