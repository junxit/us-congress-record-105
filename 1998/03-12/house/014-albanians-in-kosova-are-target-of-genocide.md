# ALBANIANS IN KOSOVA ARE TARGET OF GENOCIDE

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
               ALBANIANS IN KOSOVA ARE TARGET OF GENOCIDE

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, systematic, brutal genocide has once 
again reared its ugly head. Ethnic Albanians in Kosova are being 
slaughtered. And after all this, an official at

[[Page H1117]]

the State Department referred to Albanian freedom fighters as 
terrorists. Shame, Mr. Speaker.
  I hope I am wrong. I hope I am wrong. But it appears that the State 
Department is justifying the brutal killing behavior of a dictator 
called Milosevic and by doing so is legitimizing the slaughter of 
innocent men, women and children of Albanian descent.
  Beware, Congress. This matter in Kosova can be the next Bosnia. I 
would also like to add that Albanian men, women and children are God's 
children as well.
  One last reminder. England referred to George Washington years and 
years ago as a terrorist.

                          ____________________
```
