# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, FRIDAY, MARCH 13, 1998, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 2870, TROPICAL FOREST CONSERVATION ACT OF 1998

**Speaking:** Campbell, Tom (C000100) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2870

```
   PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 
MIDNIGHT, FRIDAY, MARCH 13, 1998, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 2870, TROPICAL 
                    FOREST CONSERVATION ACT OF 1998

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on International Relations have until midnight, Friday, March 
13, 1998, to file a report to accompany the bill (H.R. 2870) to amend 
the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 to facilitate protection of tropical 
forests through debt reduction with developing countries with tropical 
forests.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
