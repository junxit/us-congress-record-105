# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT FRIDAY, MARCH 13, 1998, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 1704, CONGRESSIONAL OFFICE OF REGULATORY ANALYSIS CREATION ACT

**Speaking:** Campbell, Tom (C000100) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1704

```
   PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT 
  FRIDAY, MARCH 13, 1998, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 1704, CONGRESSIONAL 
               OFFICE OF REGULATORY ANALYSIS CREATION ACT

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on the Judiciary have until midnight on Friday, March 13, 
1998 to file a report on the bill (H.R. 1704) to establish a 
Congressional Office of Regulatory Analysis.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
