# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 992, H.R. 1432, H.R. 2883

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4:15 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 992. An act to end the Tucker Act shuffle, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 1432. An act to authorize a new trade and invest 
     policy for sub-Saharan Africa.
       H.R. 2883. An act to amend provisions of law enacted by the 
     Government Performance and Results Act of 1993 to improve 
     Federal agency strategic plans and performance reports.
```
