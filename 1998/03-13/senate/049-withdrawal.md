# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
13, 1998, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                     Social Security Administration

       Jane G. Gould, of New York, to be Deputy Commissioner of 
     Social Security for the term expiring January 19, 2001, which 
     was sent to the Senate on September 2, 1997.
```
