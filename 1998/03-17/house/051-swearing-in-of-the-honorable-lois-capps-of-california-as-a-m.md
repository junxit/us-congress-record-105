# SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE LOIS CAPPS, OF CALIFORNIA, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

```
SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE LOIS CAPPS, OF CALIFORNIA, AS A MEMBER OF 
                               THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the Members of the California 
delegation escort the gentlewoman from California, the Member-elect, to 
the rostrum to receive the oath of office.
  Mrs. Capps appeared at the bar of the House and took the oath of 
office, as follows:
  Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
Constitution of the United States against all enemies, foreign and 
domestic; that you will bear true faith and allegiance to the same; 
that you take this obligation freely and without any mental reservation 
or purpose of evasion, and that you will well and faithfully discharge 
the duties of the office on which you are about to enter. So help you 
God?

[[Page H1208]]

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Congratulations, you are now a Member of the 
Congress of the United States.

                          ____________________
```
