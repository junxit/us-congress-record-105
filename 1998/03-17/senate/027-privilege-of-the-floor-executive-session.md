# Privilege of the Floor (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE], Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                         Privilege of the Floor

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Kurt Volker, a 
legislative fellow in Senator McCain's office; Bob Nickle and Ian 
Brzezinski of my office; and Stan Sloan, who is a member of the CRS, be 
granted the privilege of the floor throughout the entire debate and any 
vote on the protocols to the North Atlantic Treaty on Hungary, Poland 
and the Czech Republic.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ROTH. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
