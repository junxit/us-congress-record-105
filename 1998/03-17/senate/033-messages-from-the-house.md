# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 386

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:49 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following resolution:

       H. Res. 386. Resolved that the Honorable Richard K. Armey, 
     a Representative from the State of Texas, be, and he is 
     hereby, elected Speaker pro tempore on this day.
```
