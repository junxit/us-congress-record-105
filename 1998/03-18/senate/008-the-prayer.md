# THE PRAYER

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                               THE PRAYER

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, first of all, I wish to comment on the 
prayer of the Chaplain of the Senate this morning. It is a prayer that 
is needed, because there is more than just one member of the family 
involved in the Senate. It is needed because family is very much 
involved. More importantly, our Chaplain practices what he preaches in 
the sense that he is truly a person who is a pastor not only for 100 
Senators and their families but for a bigger family, the staff of the 
Senate. So I know that his prayer is from the heart as well as from the 
Scripture.

                          ____________________
```
