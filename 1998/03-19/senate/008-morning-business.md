# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for not to extend beyond 
the hour of 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for not to 
exceed 5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the hour of 9:30 a.m. having arrived, the 
Senator from Georgia, or his designee, is recognized to speak for up to 
30 minutes.
```
