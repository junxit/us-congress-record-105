# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will resume 
consideration of NATO expansion and its treaty, with amendments to the 
resolution of ratification being offered throughout the day.
  As previously stated, any Senators with amendments are encouraged to 
contact the managers of the treaty with their amendments. As earlier 
stated, it is hoped that the Senate will be able to make considerable 
progress on the treaty today.
  In addition, the Senate may consider any other legislative or 
executive business cleared for Senate action.
  As previously announced, no rollcall votes will occur during today's 
session. The next vote will occur at 5:30 p.m. on Monday, hopefully in 
relation to an amendment to the NATO treaty. Also, the second cloture 
vote in connection with the Coverdell A+ bill has been postponed, to 
occur on Tuesday, March 24, in an effort to work on an agreement 
towards orderly handling of that bill. Therefore, a second cloture vote 
will occur on the Coverdell A+ bill on Tuesday, if an agreement cannot 
be reached in the meantime.
```
