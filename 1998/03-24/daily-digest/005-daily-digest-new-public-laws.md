# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 347, H.R. 595, H.R. 3116

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D219)
  H.R. 595, to designate the Federal building and United States 
courthouse located at 475 Mulberry Street in Macon, Georgia, as the

Street NW, in Atlanta, Georgia, as the ``Sam Nunn Federal Center''. 
Signed March 20, 1998. (P.L. 105-165)
```
