# APPOINTMENT TO NATIONAL SUMMIT ON RETIREMENT SAVINGS

```
          APPOINTMENT TO NATIONAL SUMMIT ON RETIREMENT SAVINGS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 517(e)(3) of the Employee Retirement Income 
Security Act, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the 
following participant on the part of the House to the National Summit 
on Retirement Savings to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Jack Ulrich from Pennsylvania.
```
