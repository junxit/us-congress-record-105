# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3096, H.R. 3211, H.R. 3412

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will 
now put the question on the approval of the Journal, on each motion to 
suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed earlier 
today, and then on the bill on the Corrections Calendar, in the order 
in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: approval of the Journal, 
de novo; H.R. 3211, by the yeas and nays; H.R. 3412, by the yeas and 
nays; and H.R. 3096, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
