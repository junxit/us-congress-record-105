# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2589, COPYRIGHT TERM EXTENSION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 390, H.R. 2589

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2589, 
                      COPYRIGHT TERM EXTENSION ACT

  Mr. SOLOMON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-460) on the resolution (H. Res. 390) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2589) to amend the provisions of 
title 17, United States Code, with respect to the duration of 
copyright, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
