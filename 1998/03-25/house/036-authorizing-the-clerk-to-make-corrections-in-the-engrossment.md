# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2589, COPYRIGHT TERM EXTENSION ACT

**Speaking:** Coble, Howard (C000556) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2589

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 
                   2589, COPYRIGHT TERM EXTENSION ACT

  Mr. COBLE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Clerk be 
authorized in the engrossment of the bill, H.R. 2589, to insert ``Sonny 
Bono'' before ``Copyright Term Extension Act'' each place it appears; 
in other words, the bill bear Sonny's name.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
