# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND INFRASTRUCTURE TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 2400, BUILDING EFFICIENT SURFACE TRANSPORTATION AND EQUITY ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** Petri, Thomas E. (P000265) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2400

```
 PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND INFRASTRUCTURE TO FILE 
  REPORT ON H.R. 2400, BUILDING EFFICIENT SURFACE TRANSPORTATION AND 
                           EQUITY ACT OF 1997

  Mr. PETRI. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on 
Transportation and Infrastructure have until midnight, Wednesday, March 
25, 1998, to file a report to accompany the bill (H.R. 2400) to 
authorize funds for federal-aid highways, highway safety programs, 
transit programs, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
```
