# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Pappas, Michael (P000049) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 395

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 395, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable Steven 
Schiff.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 48 minutes 
p.m.) pursuant to House Resolution 395, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, March 26, 1998, at 10 a.m. in memory of the late 
Honorable Steven Schiff of New Mexico.
```
