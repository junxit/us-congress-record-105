**Speaking:** Livingston, Bob (L000371) [R-LA]

```
  PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, 
 FRIDAY, MARCH 27, 1998, TO FILE 2 PRIVILEGED REPORTS ON BILLS MAKING 
 SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS AND EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS 
                          FOR FISCAL YEAR 1998

  Mr. LIVINGSTON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations may have until midnight, Friday, March 27, 
1998 to file two privileged reports on bills, one making emergency 
supplemental appropriations for fiscal year 1998 and the other making 
supplemental appropriations for fiscal year 1998.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kingston). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Louisiana?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bills.
```
