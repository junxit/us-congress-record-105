                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIM McDERMOTT

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

2589 (Rollcall No. 69). I would have voted in favor of the Pombo

of H.R. 2578 (Rollcall No. 71).
