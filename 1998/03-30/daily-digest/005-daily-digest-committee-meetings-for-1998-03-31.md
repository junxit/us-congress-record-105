                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural 
Development, and Related Agencies, to hold hearings on proposed

216.

  Special Committee on Aging, to hold hearings to examine the effect 
on seniors of policy changes to home health care provisions under 
Medicare, focusing on the Interim Payment System, venipuncture, and 
surety bonds, 10 a.m., SD-628.
