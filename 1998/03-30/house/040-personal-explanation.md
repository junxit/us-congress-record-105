# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McNulty, Michael R. (M000590) [D-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. McNULTY. Madam Speaker, due to my attendance at the wake and 
funeral of my good friend, Judge Francis Bergan, I missed rollcall 
votes 75, 76, 77 and 78 on Thursday, March 26, and rollcall votes 79 
and 80 on Friday, March 27, 1998.
  Had I been present, I would have voted in the following manner: 
``No'' on rollcall vote number 75; ``no'' on rollcall vote number 76; 
``yes'' on rollcall vote number 77; ``no'' on rollcall vote number 78; 
``yes'' on rollcall vote number 79; and ``no'' on rollcall vote number 
80.
```
