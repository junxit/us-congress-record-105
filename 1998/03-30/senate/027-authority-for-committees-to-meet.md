# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                   committee on governmental affairs

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent on behalf of the 
Governmental Affairs Committee to meet on Monday, March 30, 1998, at 2 
p.m. for a hearing on the nominations of Elaine D. Kaplan to be the 
special counsel in the Office of Special Counsel, and Ruth Y. Goldway 
to be Commissioner of the Postal Rate Commission.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
