# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Metcalf, Jack (M000669) [R-WA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. METCALF. Mr. Speaker, last night I was tied up in the Committee 
on Rules testifying on my amendment to the Financial Modernization 
Bill.
  Due to this, I arrived on the floor at the very last minute and 
inadvertently voted ``aye'' on rollcall No. 81. My intention was to 
vote ``no'' because of my opposition to the language in the bill. I 
would like the Record to show on rollcall No. 81, my vote would have 
been ``no.''
```
