**Speaking:** McKeon, Howard P. "Buck" (M000508) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION AND THE WORKFORCE TO HAVE UNTIL 5 
    P.M. APRIL 20, 1998, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 6, HIGHER EDUCATION
```
