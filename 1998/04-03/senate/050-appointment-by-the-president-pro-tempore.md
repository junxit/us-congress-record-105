# APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 208

```
[[Page S3237]]
                APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, and upon the recommendation of the Majority Leader, pursuant 
to the provisions of S. Res. 208 of the 105th Congress, appoints the 
Senator from Utah (Mr. Bennett) as Chairman of the Special Committee on
```
