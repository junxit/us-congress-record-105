# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 20, 1998, AT 11 A.M.

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 257

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 20, 1998, AT 11 A.M.

  Mr. LOTT. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under the provisions of 
H. Con. Res. 257.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
stand in adjournment until the hour of 11 a.m., Monday, April 20, 1998.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2:18 p.m., adjourned until Monday, April 
20, 1998, at 11 a.m.
```
