                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                 COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND HUMAN RESOURCES

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I would like to announce for information

10:30 a.m., in SD-106 of the Senate Dirksen Building. The subject of 
the hearing is ``Proposed Individuals With Disabilities Education Act 
Regulations.'' For further information, please call the committee, 
(202) 224-5375.


                 committee on labor and human resources

on Commerce will be held on Thursday, April 23, 1998, 2:30 p.m., in SH-
216 of the Senate Hart Building. The subject of the hearing is The Gift 
of Life: Increasing Bone Marrow Donation and Transplantation. For 
further information, please call the committee, (202) 224-5375.
