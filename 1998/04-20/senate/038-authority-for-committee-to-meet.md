# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


                     subcommittee on youth violence

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Youth Violence, of the Senate Judiciary Committee, be 
authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on Monday, April 
20, 1998, at 10:30 a.m. to hold a field hearing at the Court of Common 
Pleas, Juvenile Court, 16th floor, 800 Broadway, Cincinnati, OH 45020 
on: ``Juvenile Courts of the 21st Century: Violent & Repeat 
Offenders.''
  The Presiding Officer. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
