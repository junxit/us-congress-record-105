# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

**Measures:** S. 414

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COVERDELL. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, 
at 9:40 a.m. on Tuesday, the Senate will resume consideration of S. 
414, the ocean shipping reform bill. At the hour of 10 a.m. the Senate 
will proceed to two consecutive rollcall votes. The first vote will be 
on or in relation to the Gorton amendment No. 2287 pending to the 
shipping bill. The second vote will be on or in relation to the Kennedy 
amendment No. 2289 offered to the Coverdell education bill.
  Also, under a previous consent agreement, following the policy 
luncheons, at 2:15 the Senate will proceed to two stacked rollcall 
votes. The first vote will be on or in relation to the Glenn amendment 
No. 2017, followed by a vote on or in relation to the Mack-D'Amato 
amendment No. 2288. Following the stacked rollcall votes, it is hoped 
that Senators will come to the floor to offer their amendments to the 
Coverdell education bill. Therefore, additional rollcall votes will 
occur throughout Tuesday's session of the Senate in the hope of making 
progress on the bill.

                          ____________________
```
