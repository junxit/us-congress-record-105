# RECESS

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA], Faircloth, Lauch (F000437) [R-NC]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:23 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Coats).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Presiding Officer, in his capacity as a 
Senator from Indiana, notes the absence of a quorum. The clerk will 
call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FAIRCLOTH. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
