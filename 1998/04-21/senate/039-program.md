# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Tomorrow morning, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Coverdell A+ education bill. Amendments will be 
offered and debated throughout Wednesday's session in an attempt to 
finish that legislation.
  I also inform my colleagues that funeral services will be held for 
former Senator Terry Sanford tomorrow in Durham, NC. Therefore, any 
votes ordered tomorrow morning in respect to amendments to the 
Coverdell bill would be stacked to occur at approximately 3 p.m. 
Members will be notified of the exact voting schedule when that becomes 
available.
```
