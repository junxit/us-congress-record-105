# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1757

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1757

```
      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1757

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will begin debate tomorrow on the State Department 
reorganization conference report under a 6-hour time agreement.
  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the vote on the adoption 
of the conference report now occur at 2:25 p.m. on Tuesday, April 28, 
with the previously ordered 10 minutes to commence at 2:15 Tuesday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
