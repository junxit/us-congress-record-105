# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Wise, Robert E., Jr. (W000654) [D-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3546

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. WISE. Mr. Speaker, because I was picking my children up from 
school I was unable to get back to the capitol to vote on H.R. 3546, 
the National Dialogue on Social Security Act.
  I ask that the Record reflect that had I been here I would have 
supported the motion to recommit. I also ask that the Record reflect 
that had I been here I would have supported final passage of this 
measure and voted ``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
