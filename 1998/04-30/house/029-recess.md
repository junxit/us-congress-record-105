# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 12 of rule I, the Chair 
declares the House in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  Accordingly (at 3 o'clock and 14 minutes p.m.), the House stood in 
recess subject to the call of the Chair. The bells will be rung 15 
minutes prior to reconvening.
```
