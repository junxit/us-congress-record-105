# WEST LAFAYETTE GIRLS' BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN]

```
                 WEST LAFAYETTE GIRLS' BASKETBALL TEAM

● Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize the West 
Lafayette girls' basketball team in West Lafayette, Indiana as the 1998 
Indiana Class 3A Girls' Basketball State Champion.
  On Saturday, March 14, 1998, the West Lafayette Red Devils rallied 
under coach Steve Dietrich and assistant coaches Alyson Sautter and 
Corissa Yasen, to defeat the Franklin Community Grizzly Cubs, 62-45, to 
win Indiana's first girls' Class 3A title. The Red Devils finished 
their season with an impressive 24-4 record.
  I congratulate the West Lafayette Red Devils on their season of 
excellence in the Hoosier tradition of basketball. I commend the 
players, coaches and supporters for their dedication and enthusiasm, 
which has fostered an outstanding girls' basketball program.
  Members of the State Championship team are: Rachael Anderson, 
Captain; Abbie Erickson, Captain; Kuleni Gebisa, Captain; Keaton Brumm; 
Lello Gebisa; Lea Musselman; Joni Woods; Hannah Anderson; Johanna 
Smith; Megan Stacker; Kristen Aaltonen; Ebba Gebisa; and Jeannine 
Mellish.●
```
