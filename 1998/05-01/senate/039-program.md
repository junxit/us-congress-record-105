# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. KYL. For the information of all Senators, when the Senate 
reconvenes on Monday, it is the leader's intention to begin 
consideration of the IRS reform bill. It is hoped that Members will 
come to the floor, offer their opening statements and debate this 
important piece of legislation. As a reminder, any votes ordered with 
respect to the IRS reform bill will be postponed to occur following the 
vote on the job training partnership bill ordered for 5:30 p.m. on 
Tuesday, May 5.
```
