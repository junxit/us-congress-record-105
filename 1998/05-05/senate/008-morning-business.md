# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT], Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10:30 a.m, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Utah, Mr. Hatch, is 
recognized to speak for up to 30 minutes.
  Mr. HATCH. Without losing my right to the floor, I am happy to yield 
time to the distinguished Senator from Idaho. Then I would like to make 
my statement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Idaho.
  Mr. CRAIG. I ask unanimous consent the time I use would not take away 
from the allocated time of the Senator from Utah.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
