                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                   senate committee on indian affairs

Committee on Indian Affairs will meet to conduct a hearing on

Senate Office Building.

in room 342 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building. For further 
information, please contact Timothy J. Shea of the subcommittee staff 
at 224-3721.
