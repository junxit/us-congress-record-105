# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 220, H.Res. 267, H.R. 1872

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. VITO FOSSELLA

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 6, 1998

  Mr. FOSSELLA. Mr. Speaker, I wanted to take this opportunity to 
inform you that I was unable to attend the session of the House of 
Representatives on May 5, 1998 and for a portion for May 6, 1998. My
```
