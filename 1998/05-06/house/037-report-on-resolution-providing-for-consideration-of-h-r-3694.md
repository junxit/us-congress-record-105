# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3694, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 420, H.R. 3694

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3694, 
          INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-511) on the resolution (H. Res. 420) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 3694) to authorize appropriations for 
fiscal year 1999 for intelligence and intelligence-related activities 
of the United States Government, the Community Management Account, and 
the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
