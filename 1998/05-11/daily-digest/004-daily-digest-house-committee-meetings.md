# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2635

```
Committee Meetings
HUMAN RIGHTS INFORMATION ACT
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on 
Government Management, Information, and Technology held a hearing on 
H.R. 2635, Human Rights Information Act. Testimony was heard from Lee 
Strickland, Information and Privacy Coordinator, CIA; Steven Garfinkel, 
Director, Information Security Oversight Office, National Archives and 
Records Administration; and public witnesses.
```
