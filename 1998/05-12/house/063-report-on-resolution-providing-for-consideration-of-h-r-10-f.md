# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 10, FINANCIAL SERVICES ACT OF 1998

**Measures:** H.R. 10, H.Res. 428

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 10, FINANCIAL 
                          SERVICES ACT OF 1998

  Mr. SOLOMON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-531) on the resolution (H. Res. 428) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 10) to enhance competition in the 
financial services industry by providing a prudential framework for the 
affiliation of banks, securities firms, and other financial service 
providers, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
