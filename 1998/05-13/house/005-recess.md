# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the order of the House of Thursday, May 7, 
1998, the House declares the House in recess subject to the call of the 
Chair, to receive the former Members of Congress.
  Accordingly (at 9 o'clock and 5 minutes a.m.), the House stood in 
recess subject to the call of the Chair.
```
