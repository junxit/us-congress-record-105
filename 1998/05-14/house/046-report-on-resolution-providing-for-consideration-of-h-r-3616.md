# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3616, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 435, H.R. 3616

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3616, NATIONAL 
             DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

  Mr. SOLOMON (during the special order of Mr. Doolittle) from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 105-535) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 435) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 3616) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1999 for 
military activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe military 
personnel strengths for fiscal year 1999, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
