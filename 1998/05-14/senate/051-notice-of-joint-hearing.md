# NOTICE OF JOINT HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                        NOTICE OF JOINT HEARING


  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources and Committee on Foreign 
                               Relations

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a joint hearing has been 
scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources and the 
Committee on Foreign Relations.
  The hearing will take place on Thursday, May 21, 1998, beginning at 
10 a.m. in Room SD-419 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on the subject of 
Iraq: Are Sanctions Collapsing?
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Foreign Relations, United States Senate, Washington, D.C. 
20510. For further information, please contact Ms. Danielle Pletka of 
the Foreign Relations Committee staff at (202) 224-4651 or Mr. Howard 
Useem of the Energy & Natural Resources Committee staff at (202) 224-
6567.

                          ____________________
```
