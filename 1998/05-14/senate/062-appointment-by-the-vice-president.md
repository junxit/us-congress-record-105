# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, in 
accordance with 22 U.S.C. 1928a-1928d, as amended, appoints the Senator 
from Arkansas (Mr. Hutchinson) as a member of the Senate Delegation to 
the North Atlantic Assembly during the Second Session of the 105th 
Congress, to be held in Barcelona, Spain, May 22-27, 1998.
```
