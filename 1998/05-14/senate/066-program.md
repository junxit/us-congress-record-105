# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

**Measures:** S. 1723

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow morning at 9:30 a.m. the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until 12 noon. As a reminder, there will be no votes during 
Friday's session. A cloture motion was filed today on the motion to 
proceed to the tobacco legislation. That vote will occur on Monday at a 
time to be determined by the two leaders, but not prior to 5 p.m.
  Also, at noon on Monday, the Senate will begin consideration of S. 
1723, the Abraham immigration legislation. Therefore, Members can 
expect a rollcall vote on cloture and additional votes with respect to 
the immigration legislation Monday evening.
```
