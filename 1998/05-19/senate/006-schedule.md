# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hutchinson, Tim (H001015) [R-AR]

**Measures:** S. 1415

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
today there will be a period of morning business until 10 a.m. 
Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of S. 
1415, the tobacco legislation. It is hoped that Members will come to 
the floor to debate this important legislation and other amendments 
under short time agreements. Rollcall votes may occur prior to the 
12:30 policy luncheons, and Members should expect those throughout 
today's session in order to make good progress on the tobacco bill.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The able minority leader is recognized.
  (Mr. HUTCHINSON assumed the Chair.)
```
