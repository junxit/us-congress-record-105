# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie, offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, we press on with renewed hope for the debate over 
crucial issues before us. We know that if we trust You and proceed with 
honest exchange and civility, You will help us succeed together.
  Make us so secure in Your love that our egos will not get in the way; 
grant us Your power, so we will not need to manipulate in a power 
struggle; free us from secondary loyalties, so we can focus on the 
future of our Nation as our primary concern. Thank You for the strength 
and vitality that You provide. We commit this day and our lives to You. 
Through our Lord and Saviour. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
