# REPORT ON RESOLUTION DISPOSING OF THE CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 1150, AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH, EXTENSION, AND EDUCATION REFORM ACT OF 1998

**Measures:** H.Res. 446, S. 1150

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION DISPOSING OF THE CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 1150, 
   AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH, EXTENSION, AND EDUCATION REFORM ACT OF 1998

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-548) on the resolution (H. Res. 446) disposing of 
the conference report to accompany the bill (S. 1150) to ensure that 
federally funded agricultural research, extension, and education 
address high-priority concerns with national or multistate 
significance, to reform, extend, and eliminate certain agricultural 
research programs, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
