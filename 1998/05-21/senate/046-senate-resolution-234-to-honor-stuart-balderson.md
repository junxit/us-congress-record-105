# SENATE RESOLUTION 234--TO HONOR STUART BALDERSON

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 234

```
            SENATE RESOLUTION 234--TO HONOR STUART BALDERSON

  Mr. STEVENS (for himself, Mr. Lott, Mr. Daschle, Mr. Byrd, and Mr. 
Warner): submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 234

       Resolved, That Stuart Balderson is named Financial Clerk 
     Emeritus of the United States Senate.
       Sec. 2. That Rule XXIII is amended by adding after 
     ``Parliamentarian Emeritus;; the following ``and the 
     Financial Clerk Emeritus.''

                          ____________________
```
