# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R.1385, EMPLOYMENT, TRAINING, AND LITERACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** McKeon, Howard P. "Buck" (M000508) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1385

```
    APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R.1385, EMPLOYMENT, TRAINING, AND 
                    LITERACY ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1997

  Mr. McKEON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 1385) to consolidate, coordinate and 
improve employment, training, literacy, and vocational rehabilitation 
programs in the United States, and for other purposes, with a Senate 
amendment thereto, disagree to the Senate amendment thereto, disagree 
to the Senate amendment,

[[Page H3966]]

and agree to the conference asked by the Senate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California? The Chair hears none and, without objection, 
appoints the following conferees: For consideration of the House bill 
and Senate amendment and modifications committed to conference Messrs. 
Goodling, McKeon, Riggs, Graham, Bob Schaffer of Colorado, Clay, 
Martinez, and Kildee.
  There was no objection.
```
