# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 99--AUTHORIZING THE FLYING OF THE POW/MIA FLAG

**Measures:** S. 99, S.Con.Res. 99

```
SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 99--AUTHORIZING THE FLYING OF THE POW/MIA 
                                  FLAG

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                            S. Con. Res. 99

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That, for the purpose of section 1082(b)(1)(B) 
     of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 
     1998, the display of the POW/MIA flag at the Capitol shall 
     begin at 6:30 p.m. on Sunday, May 24, 1998. As used in this 
     section, the term

[[Page S5457]]

     ``POW/MIA flag'' has the same meaning as in section 1082 of 
     such Act.
       Sec. 2. The Architect of the Capitol may prescribe 
     regulations with respect to the first section of this 
     resolution.

                          ____________________
```
