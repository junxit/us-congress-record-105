# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

**Measures:** S. 1253

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the public 
that a hearing previously announced for June 11, 1998, has been 
rescheduled before the Subcommittee on Forests and Public Land 
Management of the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
  The hearing will take place Wednesday, June 17, 1998, at 2:00 p.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in Washington, DC.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on S. 1253, the 
Public Land Management Improvement Act of 1997.
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC 
20510. For further information, please call Amie Brown or Mark Rey at 
(202) 224-6170.

                          ____________________
```
