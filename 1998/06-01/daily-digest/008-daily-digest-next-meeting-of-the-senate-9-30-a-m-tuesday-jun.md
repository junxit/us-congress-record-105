# Daily Digest/Next Meeting of the SENATE 9:30 a. m., Tuesday, June 2

**Measures:** H.R. 1270, S. 1415, H.R. 2604, H.R. 2798, H.R. 2799, H.R. 3433, H.R. 3504, H.R. 3630, H.R. 3808

```

                       Next Meeting of the SENATE
                      9:30 a. m., Tuesday, June 2

                               
                             Senate Chamber
Program for Tuesday: After the recognition of three Senators for 
speeches and the transaction of any morning business (not to extend 
beyond 10 a.m.), Senate will resume consideration of S. 1415, Universal 
Tobacco Settlement Act.
  Senate will also consider a motion to proceed to consideration of 
H.R. 1270, Nuclear Waste Policy Act, with a cloture vote on the motion 
to proceed to occur thereon at 6 p.m., following which Senate will 
consider the nomination of Rosemary S. Pooler, of New York, to be U.S. 
Circuit Judge for the Second Circuit, with a vote to occur thereon.
  (Senate will recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for respective 
party conferences.)
    
    
    
    

              Next Meeting of the HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
                       2 p.m., Wednesday, June 3

                               
                             House Chamber
Program for Wednesday: Consideration of 6 Suspensions:
  (1) H.R. 2604, Religious Liberty and Charitable Donation Protection 
Act of 1997;
  (2) H.R. 3504, John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts 
Authorization Act;
  (3) H.R. 3808, Designating the Carl D. Pursell Post Office;
  (4) H.R. 3630, Designating the Steven Schiff Post Office;
  (5) H.R. 2798, Designating the Nancy B. Jefferson Post Office 
Building; and
  (6) H.R. 2799, Designating the Reverend Milton R. Brunson Post Office 
Building; and
  Consideration of H.R. 3433, Ticket to Work and Self-Sufficiency Act 
of 1998 (Modified Closed Rule).
  Note: No Recorded Votes are expected before 5:00 p.m.
```
