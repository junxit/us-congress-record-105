# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet in open session of the Senate on 
Wednesday, June 3, 1998 beginning at 9:30 a.m. to conduct an oversight 
hearing on Tribal Justice Programs. Focus on joint Department (DOJ/DOI) 
Indian Country Law Enforcement Initiative and other related tribal 
justice issues. The hearing will be held in room G-50 of the Dirksen 
Senate Office Building. Those wishing additional information should 
contact the Committee on Indian Affairs at 202/224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
