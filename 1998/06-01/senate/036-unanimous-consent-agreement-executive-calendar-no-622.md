# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR NO. 622

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
        UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR NO. 622

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that 
following the cloture vote scheduled for Tuesday at 6 p.m., regardless 
of the outcome, the Senate then proceed to executive session for the 
consideration of Calendar No. 622. I further ask unanimous consent that 
the nomination be confirmed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. Calendar No. 622 will be considered tomorrow, which would 
be Rosemary Pooler to be U.S. Circuit Judge for the Second Circuit in 
New York.

                          ____________________
```
