# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--NUCLEAR WASTE POLICY ACT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
         UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--NUCLEAR WASTE POLICY ACT

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 4 p.m. on 
Tuesday, June 2nd, there be 2 hours of debate equally divided between 
the opponents and proponents of the nuclear waste legislation. I 
further ask unanimous consent that the vote occur on the motion to 
invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to that bill at 6 p.m. on 
Tuesday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
