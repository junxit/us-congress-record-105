Joint Meetings
RETIREMENT SAVINGS
Joint Hearing: Senate Special Committee on Aging and the House 
Committee on Education and the Workforce Subcommittee on Employer-
Employee Relations concluded joint hearings to examine certain 
retirement savings barriers for individuals and ways to educate 
Americans on how to prepare for retirement, after receiving testimony 
from Olena Berg, Assistant Secretary of Labor for Pension Welfare 
Benefits; Dallas L. Salisbury, Employee Benefit Research Institute, 
Washington, D.C.; Sharon Dillon Robinson, Center for Retirement 
Education/Variable Annuity Life Insurance Company, Houston, Texas; 
Dennis L. Stone, Western Manufacturing Corp., Marshalltown, Iowa; and 
Jan Owens Bruene, Des Moines, Iowa.
