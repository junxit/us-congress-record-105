# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bass, Charles F. (B000220) [R-NH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3616

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. CHARLES F. BASS

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 4, 1998

  Mr. BASS. Mr. Speaker, the Record currently indicates that I did not 
vote during rollcall No. 170, on the Hunter Amendment to H.R. 3616, the 
FY99 Defense Authorization Act. It is my recollection that, in fact, I 
voted in favor of this important amendment. I therefore ask unanimous 
consent that the Record indicate my support for this amendment.
```
