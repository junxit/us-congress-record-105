# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Upton). Pursuant to the provisions of 
clause 5, rule I, the Chair announces that he will postpone further 
proceedings today on each motion to suspend the rules on which a 
recorded vote or the yeas or nays are ordered or on which the vote is 
objected to under clause 4 of rule XV.
  Such rollcall votes, if postponed, will be taken after debate is 
concluded on all motions to suspend the rules, but not before 6 p.m. 
today.
```
