# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2888, SALES INCENTIVE ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 461, H.R. 2888

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2888, SALES 
                             INCENTIVE ACT

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-572) on the resolution (H. Res. 461) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2888) to amend the Fair Labor 
Standards Act of 1938 to exempt from the minimum wage recordkeeping and 
overtime compensation requirements certain specialized employees, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
