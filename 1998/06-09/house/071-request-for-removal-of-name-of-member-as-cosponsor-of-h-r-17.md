# REQUEST FOR REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1704

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1704

```
    REQUEST FOR REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1704

  Mrs. MALONEY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to 
withdraw my name as a cosponsor from H.R. 1704.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unanimous consent request of the 
gentlewoman to remove her name as a cosponsor of H.R. 1704 cannot be 
granted because H.R. 1704 has been reported to the House and referred 
to the Union Calendar.
```
