# EXPRESSING SYMPATHY TO MARY-ALYCE JONES ON THE PASSING OF HER MOTHER

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
  EXPRESSING SYMPATHY TO MARY-ALYCE JONES ON THE PASSING OF HER MOTHER

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Duncan). Without objection, the 
gentleman from Ohio is recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, I take this time today to notify Members 
of the House that we could be expressing our condolences to Mary-Alyce 
Jones on the death of her mother this past Sunday.
  Many in the Congress will recognize Mary-Alyce as a longtime employee 
of the Clerk, whose professional attitude and quiet dignity here on the 
floor serves as a model for all employees to follow, and Members as 
well.

                              {time}  1130

  So on behalf of all the Congress to not only notify them, we say to 
Mary-Alyce Jones and the family to please accept our deepest sympathy 
and know that our thoughts and prayers are with you and your family on 
this day of loss.

                          ____________________
```
