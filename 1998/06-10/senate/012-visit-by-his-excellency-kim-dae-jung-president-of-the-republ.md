# VISIT BY HIS EXCELLENCY KIM DAE-JUNG, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF SOUTH KOREA

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
  VISIT BY HIS EXCELLENCY KIM DAE-JUNG, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF 
                              SOUTH KOREA

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the distinguished Senator from 
Massachusetts has made my speech for me. The distinguished and honored 
guest from the Republic of Korea is with us, and I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate stand in recess for a couple minutes so that 
Senators and others may greet him.


                                 recess
```
