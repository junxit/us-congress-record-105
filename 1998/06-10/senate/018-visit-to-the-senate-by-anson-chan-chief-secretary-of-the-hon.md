# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY ANSON CHAN, CHIEF SECRETARY OF THE HONG KONG SPECIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REGION

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
  VISIT TO THE SENATE BY ANSON CHAN, CHIEF SECRETARY OF THE HONG KONG 
                     SPECIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REGION

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Madam President, it gives me a great deal of pleasure 
to introduce to this body, the U.S. Senate, Mrs. Anson Chan. Anson Chan 
is the Chief Secretary of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, 
known to many Senators in this body.
  Anson Chan is the head of Hong Kong's 190,000-strong Civil Service. 
She was appointed to the position back in 1993 by then-Governor Chris 
Patten and has continued to serve in this capacity under C.H. Tung, the 
Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
```
