**Speaking:** Gekas, George W. (G000121) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3494

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3494, 
      CHILD PROTECTION AND SEXUAL PREDATOR PUNISHMENT ACT OF 1998

  Mr. GEKAS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the

other changes as may be necessary in reflecting the actions of the
```
