            NATIONAL TOBACCO AND YOUTH SMOKING REDUCTION ACT

                Amendment No. 2689, As Further Modified

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Kerry 
amendment No. 2689 be further modified with the changes at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

     follows through line 14, and insert a period after (b)(2) on 
     line 7.
       At the appropriate place insert the following:
       (h) Assistance for Children.--A State shall use not less 
     than 50 percent of the amount described in subsection (b)(2) 
     for each fiscal year to carry out activities under the Child 
     Care and Development Block Grant Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 9858 
     et seq.).
