# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2888, H.R. 3494

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12 noon, a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2888. An act to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
     1938 to exempt from the minimum wage recordkeeping and 
     overtime compensation requirements certain specialized 
     employees.
       H.R. 3494. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, 
     with respect to violent sex crimes against children, and for 
     other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
