# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Res. 401, H.R. 1847

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TODD TIAHRT

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 17, 1998

  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I was unavoidably detained and 
missed two votes. The first, agreeing to the Senate amendments on H.R. 
1847, the Telemarketing Fraud Prevention Act; and the second, passage 
of House Resolution 401, the Sense of the House that Social Promotion 
Should Be Ended. I support both measures and had I been present I would 
have voted ``yes'' on rollcall No. 232 and No. 233.

                          ____________________
```
