# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McIntosh, David M. (M000481) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3097

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. McINTOSH. Mr. Speaker, I ask that the Record reflect concerning 
H.R. 3097, the Tax Code Termination Act, that I was detained in 
subcommittee hearings and unable to make the vote on final passage. I 
am delighted that the measure passed 219 to 209, and, as an original 
cosponsor of that bill, I would have voted yes, and I ask the Record 
reflect that.
```
