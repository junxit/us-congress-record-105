# APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276h-276k, as amended, appoints the following 
Senators as members of the Senate Delegation to the Mexico-U.S. 
Interparliamentary Group Meeting during the Second Session of the 105th 
Congress, to be held in Morelia, Mexico, June 19-21, 1998: the Senator 
from Kansas (Mr. Roberts) and the Senator from Alabama (Mr. Sessions).
```
