# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4101, DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 482, H.R. 4101

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4101, 
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

  Mr. Solomon (during the special order of the gentleman from Michigan, 
Mr. Bonior) from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-593) on the resolution (H. Res. 482) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 4101) making appropriations for 
Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, and 
Related Agencies programs for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
1999, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
