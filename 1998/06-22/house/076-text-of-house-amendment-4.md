# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Foley, Mark (F000238) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4101

```
H.R. 4101

                         Offered by: Mr. Foley

       Amendment No. 4: Page 69, after line 14, insert the 
     following section:
       Sec. 739. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Food and Drug Administration may be expended to implement 
     or enforce any rule that prohibits the manufacture, 
     distribution, or sale of metered-dose inhalers that use 
     chlorofluorocarbons.
```
