# Text of House Amendment 9

**Speaking:** Petri, Thomas E. (P000265) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 4101

```
H.R. 4101

                         Offered By: Mr. Petri

         Amendment No. 9: Add after the final section the 
     following new section:

       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to assist or cooperate with, or to pay the salaries 
     of personnel of the Department of Agriculture who assist or 
     cooperate with, the Northeast Interstate Dairy Compact 
     referred to in section 147 of the Agricultural Market 
     Transition Act (7 U.S.C. 7256) after April 4, 1999.
```
