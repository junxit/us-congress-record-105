# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Smith, Linda (S000587) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4101

```
H.R. 4101

               Offered By: Mrs. Linda Smith of Washington

       Amendment No. 15: Add after the final section the following 
     new section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Department of Agriculture may be used to make available 
     or administer, or to pay the salaries of personnel of the 
     Department of Agriculture who make available or administer, 
     any crop insurance under the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 
     U.S.C. 1501 et seq.) or noninsured crop disaster assistance 
     under section 196 of the Federal Agriculture Improvement and 
     Reform Act of 1996 (7 U.S.C. 7333) for tobacco.
```
