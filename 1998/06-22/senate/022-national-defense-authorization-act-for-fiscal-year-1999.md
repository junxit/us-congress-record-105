  The Senate continued with the consideration of the bill.

to call up amendment No. 2813 relative to tax compensation at Fort

enact this legislation.
  I yield the floor.
  Mr. FEINGOLD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to speak as in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
