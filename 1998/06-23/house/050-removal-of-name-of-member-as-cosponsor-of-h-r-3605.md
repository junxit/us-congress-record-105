# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3605

**Speaking:** Dingell, John D. (D000355) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3605

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3605

  Mr. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the gentleman 
from Texas (Mr. Brady) be removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 3605. His name 
was mistakenly added to the list of cosponsors. I regret the error, and 
I express my apologies to him.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
