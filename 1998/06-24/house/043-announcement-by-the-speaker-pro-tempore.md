# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Blunt). The Chair wishes to remind 
Members that in order to maintain decorum and dignity in the Hall of 
the House, proper dress for male Members should include the wearing of 
a coat and tie. The Chair encourages Members to adhere to this historic 
standard.
```
