# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND 
                         DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of Section 206 of the Juvenile Justice and Delinquency 
Prevention Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 5616) as amended by Section 2(d) of 
Public Law 102-586, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of 
the following members on the part of the House to the Coordinating 
Council on Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention:
  Mr. William Robert Byars, Jr., South Carolina, to a one year term;
  Ms. Adele L. Grubbs, Georgia, to a three year term.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
