**Speaking:** McInnis, Scott (M000477) [R-CO]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND THEIR REMARKS IN THE 
            CONGRESSIONAL RECORD ON THURSDAY, JUNE 25, 1998
```
