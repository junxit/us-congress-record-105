# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Hefley, Joel (H000444) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4104

```
H.R. 4104

                         Offered By: Mr. Hefley

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.   . None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement, administer, or enforce Executive Order 
     13087 of May 20, 1998 (63 Fed. Reg. 30097).
```
