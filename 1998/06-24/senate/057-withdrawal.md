# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
24, 1998, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


            occupational safety and health review commission

       Daniel Guttman, of the District of Columbia, to be a Member 
     of the Occupational Safety and Health Review Commission for a 
     term expiring April 27, 2001, vice Edwin G. Foulke, Jr., term 
     expired, which was sent to the Senate on January 9, 1997.
```
