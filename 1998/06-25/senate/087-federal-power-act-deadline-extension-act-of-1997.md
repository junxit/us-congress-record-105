# FEDERAL POWER ACT DEADLINE EXTENSION ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 652

```
            FEDERAL POWER ACT DEADLINE EXTENSION ACT OF 1997

  The bill (H.R. 652) to extend the deadline under the Federal Power 
Act for the construction of a hydroelectric project located in the 
State of Washington, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
