# AU SABLE HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 848

```
               AU SABLE HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT ACT OF 1997

  The bill (H.R. 848) to extend the deadline under the Federal Power 
Act applicable to the construction of the AuSable Hydroelectric Project 
in New York, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to a third
```
