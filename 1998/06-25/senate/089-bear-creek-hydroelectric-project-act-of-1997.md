# BEAR CREEK HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT ACT OF 1997

**Measures:** H.R. 1184

```
              BEAR CREEK HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT ACT OF 1997

  The bill (H.R. 1184) to extend the deadline under the Federal Power 
Act for the construction of the Bear Creek hydroelectric project in the 
State of Washington, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
