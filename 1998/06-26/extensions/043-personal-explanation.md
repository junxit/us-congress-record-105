# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Ford, Harold E., Jr. (F000262) [D-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4104

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. HAROLD E. FORD, JR.

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 1998

  Mr. FORD. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, June 24, 1998, I was present on 
the House floor during rollcall vote No. 264--final passage of H.R. 
4104, the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, 
and Related Agencies Appropriations Act of 1999. To my knowledge, I 
voted on final passage of H.R. 4104. Apparently, my vote was not 
recorded. Had my vote been recorded, it would have been ``aye.''
```
