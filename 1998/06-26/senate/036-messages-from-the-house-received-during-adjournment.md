# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2646

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          enrolled bill signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 26, during the adjournment of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 2646. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to allow tax-free expenditures from education individual 
     retirement accounts for elementary and secondary school 
     expenses, to increase the maximum annual amount of 
     contributions to such accounts, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Thurmond).

                          ____________________
```
