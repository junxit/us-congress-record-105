# RECESS

**Speaking:** Mikulski, Barbara A. (M000702) [D-MD]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate now 
stands in recess until 2:30 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:54 p.m., recessed until 2:30 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Frist).

[[Page S7343]]

quorum.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.

for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
