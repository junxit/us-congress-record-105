# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK], Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Senators have 
until 10 a.m. in order to file second-degree amendments to the product 
liability substitute.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Inhofe). Without objection, it is so
```
