# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. CON. RES. 107

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 107

```
             UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. CON. RES. 107

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that on Friday, immediately following the opening 
prayer, the Foreign Relations Committee be discharged from further 
consideration of S. Con. Res. 107, a resolution affirming the U.S. 
commitment to Taiwan, and the Senate then proceed to its consideration. 
I further ask that an amendment offered by Senator Lott, which is at 
the desk, then be agreed to and the Senate then proceed to the 
immediate vote on adoption of the resolution, as amended, with no 
motions or additional amendments in order, other than a title 
amendment. I finally ask unanimous consent that, following the vote on 
the resolution, if adopted, the preamble be considered agreed to, an 
amendment to the title be agreed to, and the title, as amended, be 
agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objective, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
