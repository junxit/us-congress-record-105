# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN], Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, if there is no further business the come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order, following the remarks of the 
distinguished Senator from Delaware, Senator Roth.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
