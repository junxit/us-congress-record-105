# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
INDIA AND PAKISTAN
Committee on Foreign Relations: Subcommittee on Near Eastern and South 
Asian Affairs held hearings to examine the developments in United 
States diplomatic exchanges with the Indian and Pakistani governments, 
focusing on non-proliferation issues, receiving testimony from Karl F. 
Inderfurth, Assistant Secretary for South Asian Affairs, and Robert 
Einhorn, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Political-Military Affairs, 
both of the Department of State.
  Hearings were recessed subject to call.
```
