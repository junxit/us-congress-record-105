# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Brady, Kevin (B000755) [R-TX]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KEVIN BRADY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 1998

  Mr. BRADY of Texas. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, June 25, 1998 by 
unanimous consent I was granted a leave of absence for official 
business at the request of House Majority Leader Dick Armey. As a 
member of the House International Relations Committee, I proudly co-
hosted the European Parliament for the 49th United States/European 
Parliament Interparliamentary Meeting in College Station, Texas, of 
which I represent.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``aye'' on roll call vote 267, 
``nay'' on roll call vote 268, ``aye'' on roll call vote 269, ``aye'' 
on roll call vote 270, ``nay'' on roll call vote 271, ``aye'' on roll 
call vote 272, ``nay'' on roll call 273 and ``aye'' on roll call vote 
274.
```
