# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4104, TREASURY, POSTAL SERVICE, AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 498, H.R. 4104

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4104, 
 TREASURY, POSTAL SERVICE, AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  1999

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-622) on the resolution (H. Res. 498) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4104) making 
appropriations for the Treasury Department, the United States Postal 
Service, the Executive Office of the President, and certain Independent 
Agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1999, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
