# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Riggs, Frank (R000252) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4193

```
H.R. 4193

                         Offered by: Mr. Riggs

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill before the short 
     title insert:
       Sec. XX. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act shall be used to pay the salaries and 
     expenses of personnel to carry out the acquisition of lands 
     under section 501 of Public law 105-83 unless, prior to 
     October 1, 1998, the State of California has provided the 
     contribution required under such section 501.
```
