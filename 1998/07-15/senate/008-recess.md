# RECESS

**Speaking:** Cochran, Thad (C000567) [R-MS]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, at 9:26 a.m., the Senate recessed until 11:00; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Hutchinson).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the state of 
Arkansas, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call.
  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
