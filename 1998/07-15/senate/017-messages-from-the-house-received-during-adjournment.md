# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 2282

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on July 14, 1998, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 2282. An act to amend the Arms Export Control Act, and 
     for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro tempore 
(Mr. Thurmond).

                          ____________________
```
