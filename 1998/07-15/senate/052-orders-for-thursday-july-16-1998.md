# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 16, 1998

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 2159

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 16, 1998

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in adjournment until 10 a.m. on Thursday, July 16.
  I further ask that when the Senate reconvenes on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the routine requests through the 
morning hour be granted and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 
2159, the agriculture appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
