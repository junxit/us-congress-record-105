# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, again, on behalf of Majority Leader Lott, 
for the information of all Senators, the Senate will reconvene tomorrow 
morning at 10 a.m. and immediately resume consideration of the 
agriculture appropriations bill.
  It is hoped that Members will come to the floor to offer and debate 
any remaining amendments to the agriculture appropriations bill so that 
the Senate can complete action on this legislation by early afternoon 
tomorrow.
  Following disposition of the agriculture appropriations bill, the 
Senate may resume consideration of the VA-HUD appropriations bill, or 
may begin the legislative branch appropriations bill.
  The Senate may also consider any other legislative or executive items 
cleared for action. Therefore, Senators should expect rollcall votes 
throughout the day and into the evening during Thursday's session.

                          ____________________
```
