# ADDITIONAL COSPONSOR--S. 2022

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

**Measures:** S. 2022

```
                     ADDITIONAL COSPONSOR--S. 2022

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that my name be 
added as a cosponsor of S. 2022, the Crime Identification Technology 
Act of 1998. Mr. President, inadvertently my name was left out and I 
was not included as a cosponsor on July 13, 1998, the day I spoke on 
the Senate floor urging the passage of this bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
