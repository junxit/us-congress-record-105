# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, for information of all Senators, there will 
be a series of votes beginning at 9 a.m. on Friday, with all succeeding 
votes in the series be limited to 10 minutes each. Hopefully, that 
series of votes will include passage of the HUD-VA appropriations bill. 
The Senate is also expected to consider the legislative appropriations 
bill. However, any votes ordered with respect to Legislative Branch 
bill will be postponed to occur on Tuesday, July 21, at a time to be 
determined by the two leaders.
```
