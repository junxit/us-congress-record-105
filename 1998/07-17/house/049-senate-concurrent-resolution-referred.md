# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 88

```
                 SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  A concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following titles was 
taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as 
follows:

       S. Con. Res. 88. Concurrent resolution calling on Japan to
```
