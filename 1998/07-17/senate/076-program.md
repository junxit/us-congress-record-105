# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** S. 2260

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
when the Senate convenes on Monday at 1 p.m., there will be a period 
for the transaction of morning business until 3 p.m. Following morning 
business, the Senate will resume consideration of the legislative 
branch appropriations bill. Following that debate, the Senate will turn 
to the consideration of S. 2260, the Commerce-State-Justice 
appropriations bill. The majority leader has announced there will be no 
rollcall votes during Monday's session. Therefore, any votes ordered 
with respect to the legislative branch or Commerce-State-Justice bills 
will be stacked to occur at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, July 21.

                          ____________________
```
