# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Smith, Adam (S000510) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 452

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ADAM SMITH

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 1998

  Mr. ADAM SMITH of Washington. Mr. Speaker, on June 22 this House 
voted to approve H. Res. 452, expressing the sense of the House that 
the Postal Service should not raise its rates. My vote was mistakenly 
recorded as ``No.'' I would like to express my support for the H. Res. 
452 and emphasize that I do not believe the Postal Service should raise 
its rates at this time.
```
