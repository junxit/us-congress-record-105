# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4193, DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 504, H.R. 4193

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4193, 
  DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  1999

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 105-637) on the resolution (H. Res. 504)
```
