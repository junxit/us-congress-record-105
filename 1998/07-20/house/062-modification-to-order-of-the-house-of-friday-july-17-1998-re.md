# MODIFICATION TO ORDER OF THE HOUSE OF FRIDAY, JULY 17, 1998, REGARDING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2183, BIPARTISAN CAMPAIGN INTEGRITY ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** Smith, Linda (S000587) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2183

```
MODIFICATION TO ORDER OF THE HOUSE OF FRIDAY, JULY 17, 1998, REGARDING 
 FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2183, BIPARTISAN CAMPAIGN INTEGRITY ACT 
                                OF 1997

  Mrs. LINDA SMITH of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
to go out of order, notwithstanding the order of the House agreed to on 
Friday last, and combine amendments listed as 40 to 45 into one, and 
make it as the next thing in order after the Calvert amendment, and 
that debate be limited to five minutes for and five minutes against the 
amendment.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Washington?
  There was no objection.
```
