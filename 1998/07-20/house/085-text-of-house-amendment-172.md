# Text of House Amendment 172

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2183

```
H.R. 2183

                         Offered By: Mr. DeLay

       (To the Amendment Offered By: Mr. Shays or Mr. Meehan)

       Amendment No. 172: Add at the end of title V the following 
     new section (and conform the table of contents accordingly):

     SEC. 510. REQUIRING EXPRESS ADVOCACY TO BE DETERMINED WITHOUT 
                   REGARD TO BACKGROUND MUSIC.

       Title III of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 
     U.S.C. 431 et seq.), as amended by sections 101, 401, and 
     507, is further amended by adding at the end the following 
     new section:


          ``determination of express advocacy without regard to 
                            background music

       ``Sec. 326. In determining whether any communication by 
     television or radio broadcast constitutes express advocacy 
     for purposes of this Act, there shall not be taken into 
     account any background music used in such broadcast.''.
```
