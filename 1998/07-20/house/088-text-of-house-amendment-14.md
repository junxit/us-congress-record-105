# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4193

```
H.R. 4193

                       Offered By: Mr. Gutierrez

       Amendment No. 14: Add after the final section the following 
     new section:
       Sec. ____. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for 
     ``management of lands and resources'' under the heading 
     ``Bureau of Land Management'' and increasing the amount for 
     ``state and private forestry'' under the heading ``Forest 
     Service'' (for removal of trees in Chicago, Illinois, 
     infected with the Asian Longhorn Beetle and for replacement 
     of such trees), both by $1,000,000.
```
