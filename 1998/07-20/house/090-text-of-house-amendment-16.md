# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Miller, George (M000725) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4193

```
H.R. 4193

                  Offered By: Mr. Miller of California

       Amendment No. 16: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. --. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to construct any road in the Tongass National Forest.
```
