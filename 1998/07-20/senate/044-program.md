# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, 
when the Senate reconvenes on Tuesday there will be a period of morning 
business until 10 a.m. Following morning business, the Senate will vote 
on the motion to invoke cloture on the legislative branch 
appropriations bill. After the disposition of the legislative branch 
appropriations bill, the Senate will resume consideration of the 
Commerce-State-Justice appropriations bill. It is hoped that Members 
will come to the floor during Tuesday's session to offer and debate 
amendments as the Senate attempts to make good progress on this bill. 
We may also consider other legislative or executive items that are 
cleared for action on the Senate floor.

                          ____________________
```
