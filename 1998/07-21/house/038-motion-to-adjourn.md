  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Does the gentlewoman from New York (Ms. 
Slaughter) yield for that purpose?
  Ms. SLAUGHTER. Mr. Speaker, I yield.

       Mr. Yates of Illinois moves that the House do now adjourn.

present.
