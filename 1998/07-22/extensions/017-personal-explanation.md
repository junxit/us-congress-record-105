# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Norwood, Charles W. (N000159) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4193

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. CHARLIE NORWOOD

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 22, 1998

  Mr. NORWOOD. Mr. Speaker, on July 21, 1998, I was unavoidably 
detained during the vote on the Johnson amendment (Roll No. 312) to 
H.R. 4193--FY 1999 Interior Appropriations Act to restore the National 
Endowment for the Arts (NEA) funding to its previous level of $98 
million. Had I been in attendance, I would have voted ``No.''
```
