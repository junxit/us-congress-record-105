# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Regula, Ralph (R000141) [R-OH]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. REGULA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. REGULA. Mr. Speaker, just to advise Members, we will return to 
the Interior bill shortly. There will be no more votes tonight. 
However, I repeat, we will take all amendments tonight and stack votes 
until tomorrow. We intend to read through the end of the bill. It is 
important that if Members have amendments, they must offer them 
tonight. Tomorrow will be too late. This understanding has been worked 
out with the minority. Tomorrow we will only vote on the stack of 
amendments, plus final passage.

                          ____________________
```
