# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Roemer, Tim (R000385) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4276

```
H.R. 4276

                         Offered By: Mr. Roemer

       Amendment No. 7: Page 115, after line 8, add the following 
     new section:

         Sec. 620. (a) Designation.--The Federal building located 
     at 10th Street and Constitution Avenue, N.W. in Washington, 
     D.C., shall be known and designated as the ``Robert F. 
     Kennedy Building''.
       (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, 
     document, paper, or other record of the United States to the 
     Federal building referred to in subsection (a) shall be 
     deemed to be a reference to the ``Robert F. Kennedy 
     Building''.
```
