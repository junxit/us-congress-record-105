# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 23, 1998

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 2260

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 23, 1998

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes it business today, it stand in adjournment until 9 
a.m., Thursday, July 23. I further ask that when the Senate reconvenes 
on Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the routine requests 
through the morning hour be granted and the Senate then resume 
consideration of the Craig amendment to S. 2260, the Commerce-State-
Justice appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
