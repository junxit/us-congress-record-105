# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOHN CONYERS, JR.

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 24, 1998

  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, yesterday evening I was at the White House 
and missed three Roll Call votes.
  On rollcall vote No. 330, I was unavoidably detained. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``no,'' and I ask unanimous consent that 
this statement be placed in the appropriate portion of the Record.
  On rollcall vote No. 333, I was unavoidably detained. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``present,'' and I ask unanimous consent 
that this statement be placed in the appropriate portion of the Record.
  On rollcall vote No. 334, I was unavoidable detained. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
