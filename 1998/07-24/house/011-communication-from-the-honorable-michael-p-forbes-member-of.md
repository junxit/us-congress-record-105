```
 COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE MICHAEL P. FORBES, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Michael P. Forbes, Member of Congress:


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 23, 1998.

     Marietta, GA,
```
