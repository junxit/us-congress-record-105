# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

**Measures:** H.R. 1151

```
                          AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

                                 ______
                                 

                   CREDIT UNION MEMBERSHIP ACCESS ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                  GRAMM (AND ENZI) AMENDMENT NO. 3336

  Mr. GRAMM (for himself and Mr. Enzi) proposed an amendment to the 
bill (H.R. 1151) to amend the Federal Credit Union Act to clarify 
existing law and ratify the longstanding policy of the National Credit 
Union Administration Board with regard to field of membership of 
Federal credit unions; as follows:

       Strike section 204 of the bill and renumber the sections 
     accordingly, and beginning on page 45, line 24, strike all 
     through page 46, line 4, and redesignate subparagraphs (E) 
     and (F) on page 46 as subparagraphs (D) and (E), 
     respectively.
```
