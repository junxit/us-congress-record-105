# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 27, 1998

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 27, 1998

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under 
the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:30 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, July 27, 1998, at 12 noon.
```
