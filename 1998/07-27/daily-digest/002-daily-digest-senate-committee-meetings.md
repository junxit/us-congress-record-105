# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
D.C. POLICE DEPARTMENT
Committee on Governmental Affairs: Subcommittee on Oversight of 
Government Management, Restructuring, and the District of Columbia 
concluded hearings to examine the Metropolitan Police Department's 
efforts to reduce crime and improve the quality of life in the District 
of Columbia, focusing on the status of emergency communications and 
community policing, after receiving testimony from Charles H. Ramsey, 
Chief of Police, Terrance W. Gainer, Executive Assistant Chief of 
Police, and Mike Fitzgerald, Assistant Chief, all of the Metropolitan 
Police Department, James F. Foreman, Orange Hats Patrol, and Kirsten 
Oldenburg, Crimemail, all of Washington, D.C.
CALIFORNIA NURSING HOMES
Special Committee on Aging: Committee held hearings to examine the 
quality of care in California nursing homes, focusing on certain 
allegations of abuse and neglect and the adequacy of the Health Care 
Financing Administration's enforcement of federal care requirements for 
nursing homes, receiving testimony from Ellen Curzon, Lakeside, 
California; Leslie Oliva, Whittier, California; Kathleen Duncan, 
Vacaville, California; Kathryn L. Locatell, Sacramento, California; and 
certain other public witnesses.
  Hearings continue tomorrow.
```
