Honoring the Memory of Detective Gibson and Private First Class 
Chestnut for Their Heroism: The House agreed to H. Con. Res. 311, 
honoring the memory of Detective John Michael Gibson and Private First 
Class Jacob Joseph Chestnut of the United States Capitol Police for 
their selfless acts of heroism at the United States Capitol on July 24, 
1998 by a yea and nay vote of 392 yeas with none voting ``nay'', Roll 
No. 340.
  Pages H6459-85
Authorize Printing of Eulogies: The House agreed to S. Con. Res. 112, 
to authorize the printing of the eulogies of the Senate and the House 
of Representatives for Detective John Michael Gibson and Private First 
Class Jacob Joseph Chestnut.

Interior--referred to the Committee on Appropriations and ordered

  Pages H6485-86

H. Con. Res. 311, adjourned at 8:33 p.m. as a further mark of respect
