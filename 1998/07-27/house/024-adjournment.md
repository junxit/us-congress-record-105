# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** LaHood, Ray (L000552) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 311, H.Con.Res. 311

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 311, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Detective John 
Michael Gibson and Private First Class Jacob Joseph Chestnut.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 8 o'clock and 33 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 311, the House adjourned 
until tomorrow, Tuesday, July 28, 1998, at 10 a.m. in memory of the 
late Detective John Michael Gibson and Private First Class Jacob Joseph 
Chestnut.

                          ____________________
```
