# THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                         THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I want to extend our appreciation to the 
Chaplain for his prayer for these two fine Capitol policemen and 
friends that we have lost.
  I would like to ask that we take another moment of silence to 
remember them, and to say our personal word of prayer for their family 
and friends.
  (Moment of silence)

                          ____________________
```
