# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4250

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4250

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4250

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 4250, the Patient 
Protections Act, has arrived from the House and is now at the desk. I 
now ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4250) to provide new patient protections under 
     group health plans.

  Mr. CAMPBELL. I now ask for its second reading, and I object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
