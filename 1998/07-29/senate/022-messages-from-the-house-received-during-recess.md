# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 629

```
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 1997, 
the Secretary of the Senate on July 29, 1998, during the recess of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the House agrees to the report of the committee of conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the Senate 
to the bill (H.R. 629) to grant the consent of the Congress to the 
Texas Low-Level Radioactive Waste Disposal Compact.

                          ____________________
```
