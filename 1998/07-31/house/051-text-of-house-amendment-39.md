# Text of House Amendment 39

**Speaking:** Barr, Bob (B000169) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4276

```
H.R. 4276

                    Offered By: Mr. Barr of Georgia

       Amendment No. 39: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds in this Act may be used to 
     carry out Executive Order 13087 or any regulation issued to 
     carry out such order.
```
