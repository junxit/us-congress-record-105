# PRAYERS FOR THE FAMILY, FRIENDS AND COLLEAGUES OF OFFICER J.J. CHESTNUT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
PRAYERS FOR THE FAMILY, FRIENDS AND COLLEAGUES OF OFFICER J.J. CHESTNUT

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, once again, I want to acknowledge that our 
thoughts this morning are with the family, friends and colleagues of 
Officer J.J. Chestnut. He will pass before the Capitol one last time 
today and be laid to rest. Our hearts continue to be heavy with sorrow 
for the loss of this fine man. We certainly have his family in our 
prayers today.

                          ____________________
```
