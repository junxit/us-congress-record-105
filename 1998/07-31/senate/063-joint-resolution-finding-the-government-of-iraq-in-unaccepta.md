# JOINT RESOLUTION FINDING THE GOVERNMENT OF IRAQ IN UNACCEPTABLE AND MATERIAL BREACH OF ITS INTERNATIONAL OBLIGATIONS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 54

```
  JOINT RESOLUTION FINDING THE GOVERNMENT OF IRAQ IN UNACCEPTABLE AND 
            MATERIAL BREACH OF ITS INTERNATIONAL OBLIGATIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                        LOTT AMENDMENT NO. 3489

  Mr. GORTON (for Mr. Lott) proposed an amendment to the resolution 
(S.J. Res. 54) finding the Government of Iraq in unacceptable and 
material breach of its international obligations; as follow:

       Strike all after the resolving clause and insert the 
     following:
       ``That the Government of Iraq is in material and 
     unacceptable breach of its international obligations, and 
     therefore the President is urged to take appropriate action, 
     in accordance with the Constitution and relevant laws for the 
     United States, to bring Iraq into compliance with its 
     international obligations.''

                          ____________________
```
