# UNANIMOUS CONSENT--H.R. 4354

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4354

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT--H.R. 4354

  Mr. GORTON. I ask unanimous consent when the Senate receives from the 
House H.R. 4354, a bill regarding the U.S. Capitol Police Memorial 
Fund, the bill be considered read the third time and passed, the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any statements relating to 
the bill appear in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GORTON. I further ask consent that if the language of H.R. 4354, 
as amended, as received, is different than that of the bill currently 
at the desk, this consent be vitiated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
