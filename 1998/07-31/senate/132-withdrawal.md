# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
31, 1998, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                             THE JUDICIARY

       Michael D. Schattman, of Texas, to be United States 
     District Judge for the Northern District of Texas, vice 
     Harold Barefoot Sanders, Jr., retired, which was sent to the 
     Senate on March 21, 1997.
```
