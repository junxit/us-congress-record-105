# REPORT ON H.R. 4380, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

**Measures:** H.R. 4380

```
   REPORT ON H.R. 4380, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

  Mr. TAYLOR of North Carolina, from the Committee on Appropriations, 
submitted a privileged report (Report No. 105-670), on the bill (H.R. 
4380), making appropriations for the government of the District of 
Columbia and other activities chargeable in whole or in part against 
revenues of said District for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
1999, and for other purposes, which was referred to the Union Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved.
```
