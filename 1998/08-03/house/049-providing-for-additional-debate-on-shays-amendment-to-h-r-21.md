# PROVIDING FOR ADDITIONAL DEBATE ON SHAYS AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2183, BIPARTISAN CAMPAIGN INTEGRITY ACT OF 1997

**Speaking:** Thomas, William M. (T000188) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 442, H.R. 2183

```
   PROVIDING FOR ADDITIONAL DEBATE ON SHAYS AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2183, 
               BIPARTISAN CAMPAIGN INTEGRITY ACT OF 1997

  Mr. THOMAS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
further consideration of the bill, H.R. 2183, in the Committee of the 
Whole, pursuant to House Resolution 442 and the order of the House of 
July 17, 1998, that the amendment in the nature of a substitute offered 
by the gentleman from Connecticut (Mr. Shays) and the gentleman from 
Massachusetts, (Mr. Meehan) be debatable for not to exceed 40 minutes 
to be equally divided and the time controlled by the gentleman from 
Connecticut (Mr. Shays) and myself.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
