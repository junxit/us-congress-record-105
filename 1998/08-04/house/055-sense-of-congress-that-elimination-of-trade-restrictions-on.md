  Mr. BONIOR and Mr. BOEHNER changed their vote from ``nay'' to 
``yea.''
  Mrs. CHENOWETH changed her vote from ``yea'' to ``nay.''
  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof) the rules were 
suspended and the concurrent resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the concurrent resolution was amended so as to read: 
``Concurrent resolution expressing the sense of the Congress that the 
elimination of restrictions on the importation of United States 
agricultural products by United States trading partners should be a top 
priority in trade negotiations.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
