# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3892, ENGLISH LANGUAGE FLUENCY ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3892, ENGLISH 
                          LANGUAGE FLUENCY ACT

report (Rept. No. 105-675) on the resolution (H. Res. 516) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3892) to amend the Elementary and
```
