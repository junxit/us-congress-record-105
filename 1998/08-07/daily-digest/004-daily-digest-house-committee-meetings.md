# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
PORTALS INVESTIGATION
Committee on Commerce: Subcommittee on Oversight and Investigations

relocation to the Portals. Testimony was heard from Steven A. Grigg, 
Parcel 49C Limited Partnership, Republic Properties Corporation.

EMPLOYMENT-UNEMPLOYMENT

unemployment situation for July, receiving testimony from Katharine G. 
Abraham, Commissioner, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Department of Labor.
```
