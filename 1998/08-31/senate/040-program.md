# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

**Measures:** S. 2334

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HAGEL. For the information of all Senators, when the Senate 
reconvenes on Tuesday at 9:30 a.m., there will be an immediate rollcall 
vote on adoption of the military construction appropriations conference 
report. Following that vote, the Senate will begin consideration of S. 
2334, the foreign operations appropriations bill. Members are 
encouraged to offer and debate amendments to the foreign operations 
bill so that substantial progress can be made on this important piece 
of legislation during Tuesday's session. Also on Tuesday, the Senate 
may consider the Texas compact conference report on a 4-hour time 
agreement, and any other legislative or executive items that may be 
cleared for action.
```
