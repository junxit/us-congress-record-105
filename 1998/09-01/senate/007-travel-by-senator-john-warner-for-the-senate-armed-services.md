# TRAVEL BY SENATOR JOHN WARNER FOR THE SENATE ARMED SERVICES COMMITTEE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
 TRAVEL BY SENATOR JOHN WARNER FOR THE SENATE ARMED SERVICES COMMITTEE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, this is to advise the Senate that Virginia's 
senior Senator, John Warner, is unable to make votes today because of 
work he is undertaking for the Senate Armed Services Committee. As 
second senior member of the committee, Senator Warner has met with 
senior U.S. military officials and government representatives in 
Bosnia, Serbia, and Macedonia. Senator Warner traveled to Sarajevo, 
Belgrade, Skopje, and Pristina in Kosovo. His travel and briefings 
included field visits as well.
  Senator Warner is compiling a first-hand assessment for the Armed 
Services Committee of the military and political situation in this 
troubled and war-torn region of the world. He is scheduled to return 
later today.
```
