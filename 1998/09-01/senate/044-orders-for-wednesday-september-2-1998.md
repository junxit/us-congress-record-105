# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 1998

**Speaking:** Snowe, Olympia J. (S000663) [R-ME]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 1998

  Ms. SNOWE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand in recess until 9:15 a.m 
on Wednesday, September 2. I further ask that when the Senate 
reconvenes on Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, Senator 
Bennett be recognized to speak for up to 15 minutes in morning 
business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. SNOWE. Mr. President, I further ask consent that following the 
statement by Senator Bennett the Senate resume consideration of the 
Texas Compact conference report and there be 40 minutes of debate 
equally divided between Senators Wellstone and Snowe. Further, that 
upon the conclusion or yielding back of time, the Senate proceed to a 
vote on adoption of the conference report without any intervening 
action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
