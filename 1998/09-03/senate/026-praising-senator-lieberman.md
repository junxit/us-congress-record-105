# PRAISING SENATOR LIEBERMAN

**Speaking:** Thurmond, Strom (T000254) [R-SC]

```
                       PRAISING SENATOR LIEBERMAN

  Mr. THURMOND. The Senator from Connecticut, who has just made some 
remarks, is one of the finest and ablest Members of this body. For as 
long as he has been in the Senate, and the longer I have dealt with 
him, I am more impressed with him. He is a member of the opposite party 
from me, but we can't go by party in deciding the merits of a man. We 
have to decide his own qualities. The Senator from Connecticut has 
impressed me as having the right qualities, which we all could emulate.
  Mr. HATCH addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Utah.

                          ____________________
```
