# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, for the information of all our colleagues, 
the Senate will meet tomorrow at 10 a.m., but for a pro forma session 
only. The Senate will then reconvene on Tuesday, September 8, at 10:30 
a.m. During Tuesday's session, the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 12:30 p.m. and then recess until 2:15 p.m. to 
accommodate the weekly policy luncheons. Following those luncheons, it 
is the leader's intention for the Senate to begin consideration of the 
Interior appropriations bill. The Senate may also consider any other 
legislative activity or executive items cleared for action. Members are 
therefore reminded that rollcall votes could occur during Tuesday's 
session, and an announcement will be made when a voting schedule 
becomes available.
```
