# RECESS UNTIL 10:30 A.M., TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 1998

```
          RECESS UNTIL 10:30 A.M., TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 1998

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand recessed until 10:30 a.m., Tuesday, September 8, 1998.
  Thereupon, the Senate at 10 o'clock and 22 seconds a.m. recessed 
until Tuesday, September 8, 1998, at 10:30 a.m.
```
