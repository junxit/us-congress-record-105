# RECESS UNTIL 2:15

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA], Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                           RECESS UNTIL 2:15

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:32 p.m., recessed until 2:16 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer [Mr. Coats].
  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
