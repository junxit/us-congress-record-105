# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3682

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3682

```
               MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3682

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I understand H.R. 3682, the child custody 
protection bill, is at the desk. I now ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3682) to amend title 18, United States Code, 
     to prohibit taking minors across State lines to avoid laws 
     requiring the involvement of parents in abortion decisions.

  Mr. BENNETT. I ask for its second reading and I object to my own 
request on behalf of Senators on the other side of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
