# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 459, H.R. 678, H.R. 1560

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5 of rule I, the Chair 
will now put the question of each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the order in which 
that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 678, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 1560, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 459, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
