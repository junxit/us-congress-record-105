# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2863, MIGRATORY BIRD TREATY REFORM ACT OF 1998

**Measures:** H.Res. 521, H.R. 2863

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2863, 
                MIGRATORY BIRD TREATY REFORM ACT OF 1998

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 105-698) on the resolution (H. Res. 521) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2863) to amend the Migratory Bird 
Treaty Act to clarify restrictions under that Act on baiting, to 
facilitate acquisition of migratory bird habitat, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
