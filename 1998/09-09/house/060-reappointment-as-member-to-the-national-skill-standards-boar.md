# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO THE NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

```
     REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO THE NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

provisions of section 503(b)(3) of Public Law 103-227, the Chair

term:
```
