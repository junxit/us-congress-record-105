# CONGRATULATING FRANK AND TINA LEE UPON THE BIRTH OF WILLIAM FRANK LEE, JR.

**Speaking:** Etheridge, Bob (E000226) [D-NC]

```
CONGRATULATING FRANK AND TINA LEE UPON THE BIRTH OF WILLIAM FRANK LEE, 
                                  JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOB ETHERIDGE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 1998

  Mr. ETHERIDGE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my good 
friends Frank and Tina Lee of Smithfield, North Carolina. On August 17, 
1998 at 3:33 p.m. they welcomed into the world their first son, William 
Frank Lee, Jr. There is nothing more wonderful and joyous than watching 
a child grow and I know that they will treasure every new day with 
their son. Faye joins me in wishing the Lees great happiness during 
this very special time of their lives.

                          ____________________
```
