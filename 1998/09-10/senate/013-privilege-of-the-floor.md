# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

```
                         PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
pendency of the Interior appropriations bill, Scott Conroy be extended 
the privilege of the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Smith of Oregon). Objection is heard.
  The assistant legislative clerk continued with the call of the roll.
  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? The Chair hears none, and 
it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
