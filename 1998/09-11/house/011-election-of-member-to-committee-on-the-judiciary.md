# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 530

```
            ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  Mr. FAZIO of California. Mr. Speaker, at the direction of the 
Democratic Caucus, I offer a privileged resolution (H.Res. 530) and ask 
for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                          House Resolution 530

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       To the Committee on the Judiciary, Thomas M. Barrett of 
     Wisconsin.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
