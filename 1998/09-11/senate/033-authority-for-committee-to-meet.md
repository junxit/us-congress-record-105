# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 10

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


            COMMITTEE ON BANKING, HOUSING, AND URBAN AFFAIRS

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be authorized to meet 
in executive session during the session of the Senate on Friday, 
September 11, 1998, to conduct a markup of H.R. 10, the Financial 
Services Act of 1998.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
