# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow the Senate 
will resume debate on the Interior appropriations bill. Senator Bumpers 
will offer an amendment relating to mining laws with the vote occurring 
on or in relation to that amendment at 2:25 tomorrow afternoon. 
Following that vote, it is hoped that Members who still intend to offer 
amendments to the Interior appropriations bill will work with the 
managers of the legislation to schedule consideration of their 
amendments. I thank my colleagues and remind all Members that the first 
vote will occur on Tuesday beginning at 2:25 in the afternoon.
```
