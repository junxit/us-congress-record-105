# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4300, WESTERN HEMISPHERE DRUG ELIMINATION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 537, H.R. 4300

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4300, WESTERN 
                    HEMISPHERE DRUG ELIMINATION ACT

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-720) on the resolution (H. Res. 537) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4300) to support enhanced drug 
interdiction efforts in the major transit countries and support a 
comprehensive supply eradication and crop substitution program in 
source countries, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
