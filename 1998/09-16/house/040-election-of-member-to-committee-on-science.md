# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 540

```
               ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, at the direction of the Democratic Caucus, 
I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 540) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                          House Resolution 540

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives: Committee on Science, Mr. Sherman.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.


                             SPECIAL ORDERS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Gibbons). Under the Speaker's announced 
policy of January 7, 1997, and under a previous order of the House, the 
following Members will be recognized for 5 minutes each.
```
