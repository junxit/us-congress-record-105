# TRIBUTE TO VIVIAN DUBREUIL

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO VIVIAN DUBREUIL

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, a constituent of mine, Vivian Dubreuil from 
Jackson, MS, passed away this morning. Vivian worked for Senator Jim 
Eastland for more than 22 years. She also worked for the Secretary for 
the Majority's Office and the Secretary of the Senate. After a long and 
successful career in the Senate, she retired to care for her mother in 
Jackson. She was very much a lady who performed many kindnesses for all 
who came in contact with her. She will be missed by her friends here in 
Washington and her family and friends in Jackson.

                          ____________________
```
