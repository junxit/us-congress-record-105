# Text of House Amendment 42

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4569

```
H.R. 4569

                   Offered By: Mr. Davis of Illinois

       Amendment No. 42: Page 141, after line 18, insert the 
     following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS


         prohibition on promotion of tobacco farming in malawi

       Sec. 701. None of the funds appropriated in this Act under 
     the heading ``Development Assistance'' may be made available 
     for the promotion of tobacco farming in Malawi.
```
