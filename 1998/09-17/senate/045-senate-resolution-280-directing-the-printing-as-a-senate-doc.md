# SENATE RESOLUTION 280--DIRECTING THE PRINTING AS A SENATE DOCUMENT OF A COMPILATION OF MATERIALS ENTITLED "HISTORY OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE, NUTRITION, AND FORESTRY"

**Measures:** S.Res. 280

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 280--DIRECTING THE PRINTING AS A SENATE DOCUMENT OF A 
COMPILATION OF MATERIALS ENTITLED ``HISTORY OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE 
          COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE, NUTRITION, AND FORESTRY''

  Mr. LUGAR (for himself and Mr.  Harkin) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 280

        Resolved,

     SECTION 1. PRINTING OF HISTORY OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE 
                   COMMITTEE ON AGRICUTURE, NUTRITION, AND 
                   FORESTRY.

       The Public Printer shall print--
       (1) as a Senate document a compilation of materials, with 
     illustrations, entitled ``History of the United States Senate 
     Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry''; and
       (2) 100 copies of the document in addition to the usual 
     number.
```
