# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 1998

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 1998

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 8:30 
a.m., Friday, September 18. I further ask that when the Senate 
reconvenes on Friday, immediately following the prayer, the journal of 
proceedings be approved, no resolutions come over under the rule, the 
call of the calendar be waived, and the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
