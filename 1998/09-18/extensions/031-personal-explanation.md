# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** King, Peter T. (K000210) [R-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETER T. KING

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 18, 1998

  Mr. KING. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I was unavoidably detained and 
missed several votes. Had I been present I would have voted as follows:
  ``No'' on Rollcall No. 447, the Porter amendment to repeal the 
provisions that repealed Section 907.
  ``No'' on Rollcall No. 448, the Kennedy (MA) amendment to ban funding 
for the School of the Americas.
  ``Yea'' on Rollcall No. 449, Final passage of the Foreign Operations 
Appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
