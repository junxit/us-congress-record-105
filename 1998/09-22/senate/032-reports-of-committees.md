                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

     with respect to radiation-exposed veterans (Rept. No. 105-
     343).

     with an amendment in the nature of a substitute and an 
     amendment to the title:
       S. 1822. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     authorize provision of care to veterans treated with 
     nasopharyngeal radium irradiation (Rept. No. 105-344).
