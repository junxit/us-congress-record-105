# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 81, H.Res. 144, H.Con.Res. 315, H.Res. 505, S. 1355

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5 of rule I, the Chair 
will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the order in which 
that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: House Resolution 144, de 
novo; House Resolution 505, de novo; House Concurrent Resolution 315, 
by the yeas and nays; S. 1355, de novo; and H.R. 81, de novo..
  The first vote will be a 15-minute vote. The subsequent votes will be 
5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
