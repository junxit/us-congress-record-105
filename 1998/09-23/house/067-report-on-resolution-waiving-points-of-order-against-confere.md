# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3616 THE STROM THURMOND NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 549, H.R. 3616

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
ON H.R. 3616 THE STROM THURMOND NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 1999

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-740) on the resolution (H. Res. 549) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 3616) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1999 for 
military activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe military 
personnel strengths for fiscal year 1999, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
