# ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF SENATOR HAGEL'S 100TH PRESIDING HOUR

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
         ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF SENATOR HAGEL'S 100TH PRESIDING HOUR

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I have the pleasure to announce that Senator 
Chuck Hagel is the latest recipient of the Senate's golden gavel award, 
marking his 100th hour of presiding over the U.S. Senate.
  The golden gavel award has long-served as a symbol of appreciation 
for the time that Senators contribute to presiding over the U.S. 
Senate--a privileged and important duty. Since the 1960's, Senators who 
preside for 100 hours have been recognized with this coveted award.
  On behalf of the Senate, I extend our sincere appreciation to Senator 
Hagel and his diligent staff for their efforts and commitment to 
presiding duties during the 105th Congress.
```
