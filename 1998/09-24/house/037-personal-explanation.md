# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Diaz-Balart, Lincoln (D000299) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 144, H.Con.Res. 315, H.Res. 505, H.Res. 545, H.R. 4112

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART. Mr. Speaker, I was absent on rollcall 453, the 
LaHood motion to table H. Res. 545, impeaching Kenneth Starr; rollcall 
454, H. Res. 144, expressing support for the Bicentennial of the Lewis 
and Clark Expedition; rollcall 455, H. Res. 505, expressing the sense 
of the House with respect to Diplomatic Relations with Pacific Island 
Nations; rollcall 456, H. Con. Res. 315, Condemning Atrocities by 
Serbian Police against Albanians; and rollcall 457, the Conference 
Report to accompany H.R. 4112, the Legislative Branch Appropriations 
for FY 99, due to official business. Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``Aye'' on all of these votes.

                          ____________________
```
