**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4569

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 25, 1998

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on September 17, 1998, I was unavoidably

provide for consideration of H.R. 4569, a bill to appropriate funds for
