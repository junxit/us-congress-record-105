# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 1998

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 1998

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that when the Senate completes its business today it stand in 
recess until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, September 30; I further ask 
unanimous consent that the time for the two leaders be reserved.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOMENICI. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate then 
proceed to a period of morning business until 12:30 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes, with the following exceptions: 
The first 20 minutes under the control of Senator Murkowski; 20 minutes 
under the control of Senator Gramm of Texas; 25 minutes under the 
control of Senator Roberts; 30 minutes under the control of Senator 
McCain; 30 minutes under the control of Senator Hagel; 15 minutes under 
the control of Senator Johnson; 1 hour under the control of Senator 
Dorgan or his designee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
