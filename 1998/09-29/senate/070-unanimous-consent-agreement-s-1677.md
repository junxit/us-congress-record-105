# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1677

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** S. 1677

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1677

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, regarding Senate bill 1677, the North 
American Wetlands bill, I ask unanimous consent that it be in order for 
the majority leader, after consultation with the Democratic leader, to 
proceed to the consideration of calendar No. 417, S. 1677, and that it 
be considered under the following limitations:
  The only amendment in order to the bill be an amendment by Senator 
Chafee regarding membership.
  I further ask unanimous consent that there be 1 hour for debate on 
the bill, equally divided in the usual form, and following the 
disposition of the amendment and the expiration of the debate time, the 
bill be read the third time and passed, with the motion to reconsider 
laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
