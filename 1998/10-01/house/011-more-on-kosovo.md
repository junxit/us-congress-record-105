# MORE ON KOSOVO

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
                             MORE ON KOSOVO

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, once again genocide has reared its ugly 
head. Serbian President Milosevic is brutally exterminating ethnic 
Albanians in Kosovo. Women, children, even the elderly are being 
slaughtered. After all this, France says, and I quote, ``We must send a 
strong message.''
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker. The last I heard, NATO did not work for the 
Western Union. It is time for NATO to do their job. It is time for 
France to step up once in a while. It is time for Europe to help us 
out, and it is time for independence in Kosovo.
  One last thing, Mr. Speaker. Milosevic must be stopped. It is about 
time for France to do their job, too.

                          ____________________
```
