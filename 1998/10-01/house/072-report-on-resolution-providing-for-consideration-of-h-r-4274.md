# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4274, DEPARTMENTS OF LABOR, HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, AND EDUCATION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 564, H.R. 4274

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4274, 
    DEPARTMENTS OF LABOR, HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, AND EDUCATION 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1999

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-762) on the resolution (H. Res. 564) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4274) making appropriations for the 
Departments of Labor, Health and Human Services, and Education, and 
related agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1999, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
