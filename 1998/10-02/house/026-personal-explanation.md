# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Taylor, Gene (T000074) [D-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 3150, H.R. 3891, H.R. 4060, H.R. 4103

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. TAYLOR of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, September 2, I was 
unavoidably detained because of Hurricane Georges. All flights out of 
Mobile, New Orleans and Gulfport, Mississippi were cancelled. Had I 
been here, I would have voted ``no'' on H.R. 3891. I would have voted 
``yes'' on H.R. 4103. I would have voted ``yes'' on H.R. 4060, and I 
would have voted ``yes'' on H.R. 3150.
```
