# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 10, S. 442

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, this morning, the pending business will be 
S. 442, the Internet tax bill. An agreement has been reached on the 
bill allowing for relevant amendments, with the addition of a Bumpers 
amendment regarding catalog sales. Rollcall votes are expected during 
today's session on or in relation to amendments offered to the Internet 
bill or possibly an executive nomination. In either case, the first 
rollcall vote today will occur by 10:30 a.m.
  Members are reminded that a cloture motion was filed yesterday on the 
motion to proceed to H.R. 10, the financial services bill. That vote 
will occur at 5:30 p.m. on Monday, October 5. Also, during Monday's 
session the Senate may consider any available appropriations conference 
reports. Therefore, further votes could occur following the 5:30 
cloture on Monday. I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
