# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2186 AND S. 1719

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

**Measures:** S. 1719, H.R. 2186

```
           UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2186 AND S. 1719

  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of the following bills, en bloc: Calendar 
No. 564, H.R. 2186 and Calendar No. 572, S. 1719.
  I ask unanimous consent that amendment No. 3680, to H.R. 2186 and 
amendment No. 3681 to S. 1719 be considered agreed to the appropriate 
bills, en bloc. I further ask consent that any committee amendments be 
agreed to as necessary, the bills be read the third time and passed, 
any title amendments be agreed to, and the motions to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, any statements relating to the measures appear at 
this point in the Record, and the preceding all occur en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
