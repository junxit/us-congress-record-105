# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

clause 5, rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a minimum

device may be taken on each additional motion to suspend the rules in
```
