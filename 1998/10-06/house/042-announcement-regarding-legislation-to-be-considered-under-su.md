ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING LEGISLATION TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF 
             THE RULES TOMORROW, WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 1998

  Mr. WELLER. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 575, I announce 
that the following bills will be considered under suspension of the 
rules on tomorrow:
  H.R. 4679, H.R. 3783, H.R. 8, H.R. 4657, H.R. 4656, S. 2505, H.R. 
2921, H.R. 4616, H.R. 2348, H. Con. Res. 331, S. 2022, S. 512, S. 1976, 
H.R. 804, and H.R. 4293.
  Mr. Speaker, I include for the Record the titles of the legislation 
to be considered.
       Suspensions for Wednesday, October 7:
       1. H.R. 4679--Antimicrobial Regulation Technical 
     Corrections Act of 1998;
       2. H.R. 3783--Child Online Protection;
       3. H.R. 8--Border Smog Reduction Act;
       4. H.R. 4657--Clark County Land Exchange;
       5. H.R. 4656--Clark County Land Exchange;
       6. S. 2505--To Convey Title to the Tunnison Lab Hagerman 
     Field Station in Gooding County, Idaho;
       7. H.R. 2921--Multichannel Video Competition and Consumer 
     Protection Act;
       8. H.R. 4616--Corporal Harold Gomez Post Office;
       9. H.R. 2348--Designating the Mervyn Dymally Post Office 
     Building;
       10. H. Con. Res. 331--Expressing the Sense of Congress 
     Concerning the Inadequacy of Sewage Infrastructure Facilities 
     in Tijuana, Mexico;
       11. S. 2022--Crime Identification Technology Act of 1998;
       12. S. 512--Identity Theft and Assumption Deterrence Act;
       13. S. 1976--Crime Victims With Disabilities Awareness Act;
       14. H.R. 804--To Ensure that Federal Funds Made Available 
     to Hire or Rehire Law Enforcement Officers are used in a 
     Manner that Produces a Net Gain of the Number of Law 
     Enforcement Officers who Perform Nonadministrative Public 
     Safety Services; and
       15. H.R. 4293--To Establish a Cultural and Training Program 
     for Disadvantaged Individuals from Northern Ireland and the 
     Republic of Ireland.
