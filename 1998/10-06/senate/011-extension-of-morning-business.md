# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT], Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended until 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 5 minutes each. That is on behalf of the majority leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DODD. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
