# RECESS

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate stands in recess, under the 
previous order, until 2:15.
  Thereupon, at 12:44 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Hutchinson).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
the State of Arkansas, suggests the absence of a quorum. The clerk will 
call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
