# ANNOUNCEMENT OF BILLS TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE RULES TODAY

**Speaking:** McInnis, Scott (M000477) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.Res. 575, S. 1892, H.R. 4712

```
 ANNOUNCEMENT OF BILLS TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE RULES 
                                 TODAY

  Mr. McINNIS. Madam Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 575, I 
announce the following suspensions to be considered today:
  H.R. 4712, a bill regarding music licensing and copyright protection; 
and
  S. 1892, a bill to provide that a person closely related to a judge 
of a court exercising judicial power under article III of the United 
States Constitution (other than the Supreme Court) may not be appointed 
as a judge of the same court, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
