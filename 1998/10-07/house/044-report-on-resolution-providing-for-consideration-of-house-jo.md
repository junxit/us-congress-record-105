# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 131, WAIVING ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR REMAINDER OF 105TH CONGRESS WITH RESPECT TO ANY BILL OR JOINT RESOLUTION MAKING...

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 131, H.Res. 580

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT 
RESOLUTION 131, WAIVING ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR REMAINDER OF 105TH 
CONGRESS WITH RESPECT TO ANY BILL OR JOINT RESOLUTION MAKING GENERAL OR 
             CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1999

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 105-791) on the resolution (H. Res. 580) providing 
for consideration of the joint resolution (H.J.Res. 131) waiving 
certain enrollment requirements for the remainder of the One Hundred 
Fifth Congress with respect to any bill or joint resolution making 
general or continuing appropriations for fiscal year 1999, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
