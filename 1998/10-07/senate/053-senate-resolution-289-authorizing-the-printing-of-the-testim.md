# SENATE RESOLUTION 289--AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF THE "TESTIMONY FROM THE HEARINGS OF THE TASK FORCE ON ECONOMIC SANCTIONS"

**Measures:** S.Res. 289

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 289--AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF THE ``TESTIMONY FROM 
         THE HEARINGS OF THE TASK FORCE ON ECONOMIC SANCTIONS''

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 289

       Resolved, That the ``Testimony from the Hearings of the 
     Task Force on Economic Sanctions'', be printed as a Senate 
     document, and that there be printed 300 additional copies of 
     such document for the use of the Task Force on Economic 
     Sanctions at a cost not to exceed $16,311.

                          ____________________
```
