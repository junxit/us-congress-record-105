# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT ACCOMPANYING S. 2206

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 2206

```
  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT ACCOMPANYING S. 2206

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate considers the conference report accompanying S. 2206, that the 
reading be waived and that there be 30 minutes for debate on the 
conference report with the time equally divided and controlled between 
Senators Jeffords and Kennedy or their designees, that upon the use or 
yielding back of time the conference report be adopted, and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table, without intervening action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
