# IRRIGATION PROJECT CONTRACT EXTENSION ACT OF 1998

**Measures:** H.R. 2795

```
           IRRIGATION PROJECT CONTRACT EXTENSION ACT OF 1998

  The bill (H.R. 2795) to extend contracts between the Bureau of 
Reclamation and irrigation water contractors in Wyoming and Nebraska 
that receive water from Glendo Reservoir, was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
