# DELAWARE WATER GAP NATIONAL RECREATION AREA CITIZEN ADVISORY COMMISSION

**Measures:** S. 1175

```
DELAWARE WATER GAP NATIONAL RECREATION AREA CITIZEN ADVISORY COMMISSION

  The bill (S. 1175) to reauthorize the Delaware Water Gap National 
Recreation Area Citizen Advisory Commission for 10 additional years, 
was considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed, as follows:

                                S. 1175

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. REAUTHORIZATION OF THE DELAWARE WATER GAP NATIONAL 
                   RECREATION AREA CITIZEN ADVISORY COMMISSION.

       Section 5 of Public Law 101-573 (16 U.S.C. 460o note) is 
     amended by striking ``10'' and inserting ``20''.

                          ____________________
```
