# REREFERRAL OF H.R. 1804, JOHN McKINLEY FEDERAL BUILDING

**Speaking:** Kim, Jay (K000181) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1804

```
        REREFERRAL OF H.R. 1804, JOHN McKINLEY FEDERAL BUILDING

  Mr. KIM. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on 
Transportation and Infrastructure be discharged from further 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1804) to designate the Federal building 
located at 210 Seminary Street in Florence, Alabama, as the ``John 
McKinley Federal Building'' and that the bill be rereferred to the 
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
