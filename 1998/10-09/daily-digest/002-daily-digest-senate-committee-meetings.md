Gleiman, of Maryland, both to be Commissioners of the Postal Rate 
Commission, Gregory H. Friedman, of Colorado, to be Inspector General 
of the Department of Energy, Eljay B. Bowron, of Michigan, to be 
Inspector General, Department of the Interior, and David C. Williams, 
of Maryland, to be Inspector General, Department of the Treasury.
