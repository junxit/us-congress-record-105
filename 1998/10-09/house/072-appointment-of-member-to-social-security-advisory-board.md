# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

```
        APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of Section 703 of the Social Security Act, 42 U.S.C. 903, as 
amended by Section 103 of Public Law 103-296, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's reappointment of the following member to the Social Security 
Advisory Board to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Ms. Jo Anne Barnhart, Arlington, Virginia.
  There was no objection.
```
