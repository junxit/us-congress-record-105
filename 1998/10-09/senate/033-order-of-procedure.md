# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I had a conversation with the distinguished 
Senator from Illinois with regard to his concerns on the bankruptcy 
reform package as it now exists. He agrees and we agree that there is 
no necessity for this to be read over a period of 5 or 6 hours. So I 
think we have something worked out that we will be comfortable with and 
others will be comfortable with to allow us to assure Members what time 
the next vote will be, and we can do some business in the interim and 
have speeches made on this or other issues in the meantime.
```
