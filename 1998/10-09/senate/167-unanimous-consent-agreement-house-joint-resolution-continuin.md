# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION CONTINUING GOVERNMENT FUNDING

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
    UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION CONTINUING 
                           GOVERNMENT FUNDING

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate receives from the House the House joint resolution that will 
continue Government funding until midnight Monday, October 12, 1998, 
with no amendments, it be considered agreed to and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
