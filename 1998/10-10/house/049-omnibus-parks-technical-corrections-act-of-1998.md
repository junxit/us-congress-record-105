     SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE; REFERENCE TO OMNIBUS PARKS AND PUBLIC 
                   LANDS MANAGEMENT ACT OF 1996.

       (a) Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Omnibus 
     Parks Technical Corrections Act of 1998''.
       (b) Reference to Omnibus Parks Act.--In this Act, the term 
     ``Omnibus Parks Act'' means the Omnibus Parks and Public 
     Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104-333; 110 Stat. 
     4093).
