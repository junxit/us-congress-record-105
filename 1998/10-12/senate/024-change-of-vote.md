# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK], Ashcroft, John (A000356) [R-MO]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, on rollcall No. 295, I voted yea. It was 
my intention to vote nay. Therefore, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted to change my vote. This will in no way change the outcome of 
the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? The Chair hears none, and 
it is so ordered.
  Mr. NICKLES. I thank my colleague from Missouri.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Missouri is recognized.
  Mr. ASHCROFT. Mr. President, I thank the Chair.

                          ____________________
```
