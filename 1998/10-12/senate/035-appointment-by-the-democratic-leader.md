# APPOINTMENT BY THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND], Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN]

```
                  APPOINTMENT BY THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Democratic leader, 
pursuant to Public Law 100-696, announces the appointment of the 
Senator from North Dakota, Mr. Dorgan, as a member of the United States 
Capitol Preservation Commission.
  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
