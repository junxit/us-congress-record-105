# ORDER FOR RECESS

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT], Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                            ORDER FOR RECESS

  Mr. JEFFORDS. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess, under 
the previous order, following the remarks of the Democratic leader, 
Senator Daschle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. JEFFORDS. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
