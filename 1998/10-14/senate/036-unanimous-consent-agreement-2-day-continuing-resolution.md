# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--2-DAY CONTINUING RESOLUTION

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
        UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--2-DAY CONTINUING RESOLUTION

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I have been asked to make a unanimous 
consent request on behalf of our leader, Senator Lott.
  I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate begins consideration of 
the two-day continuing resolution, there be 10 minutes equally divided 
between the chairman and ranking minority member of the Appropriations 
Committee, and following the conclusion or yielding back of time, the 
resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, all without additional action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
