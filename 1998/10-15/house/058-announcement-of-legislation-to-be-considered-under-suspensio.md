# ANNOUNCEMENT OF LEGISLATION TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE RULES

**Speaking:** English, Phil (E000187) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S. 83, S. 120, H.Res. 589

```
 ANNOUNCEMENT OF LEGISLATION TO BE CONSIDERED UNDER SUSPENSION OF THE 
                                 RULES

  Mr. ENGLISH of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House 
Resolution 589, I hereby give notice that the following suspensions
```
