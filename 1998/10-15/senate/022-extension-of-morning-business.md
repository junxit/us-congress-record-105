# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
the State of Washington, asks and grants unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended until 3:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 5 minutes each, and suggests the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
the State of Washington, rescinds the order for the quorum call.

                          ____________________
```
