# SENATE RESOLUTION 300--ELECTING JAMES W. ZIGLAR, OF MISSISSIPPI, AS THE SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 300

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 300--ELECTING JAMES W. ZIGLAR, OF MISSISSIPPI, AS THE 
             SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 300

       Resolved, That James W. Ziglar, of Mississippi, be, and he 
     is hereby, elected Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the 
     Senate effective November 9, 1998.
```
