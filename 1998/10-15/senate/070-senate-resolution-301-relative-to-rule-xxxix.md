# SENATE RESOLUTION 301--RELATIVE TO RULE XXXIX

**Measures:** S.Res. 301

```
             SENATE RESOLUTION 301--RELATIVE TO RULE XXXIX

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 301

       Resolved, That if a Member who is precluded from foreign 
     travel by the provisions of Rule 39 is appointed as a 
     delegate to an official conference to be attended by Members 
     of the Senate, then the appointment of that individual shall 
     constitute an authorization by the Senate and the individual 
     will not be deemed in violation of Rule 39.
       Sec. 2. This resolution shall be applicable only until 
     November 21, 1998.

                          ____________________
```
