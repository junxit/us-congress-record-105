# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5, rule 
I, the Chair announces that he will postpone further proceedings today 
on each motion to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the 
yeas and nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under 
clause 4 of rule XV.
  Such rollcall vote, if postponed, will be taken after debate has 
concluded on the motion to suspend the rules, but not before 5 p.m. 
today.
```
