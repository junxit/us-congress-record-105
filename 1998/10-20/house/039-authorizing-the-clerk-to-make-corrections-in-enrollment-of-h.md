# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 3910, AUTOMOBILE NATIONAL HERITAGE AREA ACT

**Speaking:** Young, Don (Y000033) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 129, H.R. 3910

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 3910, 
                 AUTOMOBILE NATIONAL HERITAGE AREA ACT

  Mr. YOUNG of Alaska. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take 
from the Speaker's table the Senate concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 
129) to correct a technical error in the enrollment of H.R. 3910, and 
ask for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the Senate concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Alaska?
  The Chair has not received assurances of clearance from the minority 
at this time.
  Mr. YOUNG of Alaska. Mr. Speaker, we have been assured of the 
clearance by the minority. There is minority on the floor. They agree 
with it.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The request of the gentleman is withdrawn.

                          ____________________
```
