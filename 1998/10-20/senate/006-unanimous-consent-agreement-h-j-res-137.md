# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 137

**Speaking:** Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 137

```
               UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 137

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate receives H.J. Res. 137, the 1-day continuing resolution, the 
resolution be considered read the third time and passed, and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table.
```
