# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
the State of Montana, seeing no other Senators desiring to speak, asks 
unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until 1:30 p.m. this 
afternoon.
  There being no objection, at 10:24 a.m., the Senate recessed until 
1:29 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Burns).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair recognizes the Senator from Alaska.
```
