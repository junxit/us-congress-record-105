# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will reconvene tomorrow, Wednesday, at 9 a.m. and immediately 
proceed to a rollcall vote on the passage of the omnibus appropriations 
bill. Following that vote, several Members will be recognized to speak 
in relation to the omnibus bill. At the conclusion of those remarks, 
the Senate may consider any legislative or executive items that may be 
cleared for action at that time.
```
