# DESIGNATION OF HON. EDWARD A. PEASE TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 22, 1998

```
 DESIGNATION OF HON. EDWARD A. PEASE TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
   SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 22, 1998

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                 October 21, 1998.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Edward A. Pease to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through October 22, 1998.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
