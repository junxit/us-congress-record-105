# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 353

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House concurrent resolution 353 
and as the designee of the majority leader, I move that the House do 
now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In accordance with the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 353, the Chair declares the second session of the 
105th Congress adjourned sine die.
  Thereupon (at 5 o'clock and 56 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 353, the House adjourned.

                          ____________________
```
