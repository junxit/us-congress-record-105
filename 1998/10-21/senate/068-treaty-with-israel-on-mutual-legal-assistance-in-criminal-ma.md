# TREATY WITH ISRAEL ON MUTUAL LEGAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS

```
   TREATY WITH ISRAEL ON MUTUAL LEGAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS

                                 ______
                                 

                   HELMS EXECUTIVE AMENDMENT NO. 3841

  Mr. DeWINE (for Mr. Helms) proposed an executive amendment to the 
Treaty with Israel on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters 
(Treaty Doc. No. 105-40); as follows:

       On line 5 of the Resolution of Ratification of the Treaty 
     Between the Government of the United States of America and 
     the Government of the State of Israel on Mutual Legal 
     Assistance in Criminal Matters (Exec. Rpt. 105-22), strike 
     ``Tel Aviv'' and insert ``Jerusalem''.
```
