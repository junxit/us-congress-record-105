# INTERNATIONAL FISHERY TREATY AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND POLAND

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 3461

```
 INTERNATIONAL FISHERY TREATY AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND 
                                 POLAND

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed

[[Page S12944]]

to the immediate consideration of H.R. 3461, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3461) to approve a governing international 
     fishery treaty agreement between the United States and 
     Poland.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the bill?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
deemed read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table, and any statements relating to the bill be printed in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 3461) was deemed read a third time, and passed.

                          ____________________
```
