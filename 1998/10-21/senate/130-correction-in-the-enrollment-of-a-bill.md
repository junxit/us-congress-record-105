# CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF A BILL

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 352

```
                 CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF A BILL

  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of H. Con. Res. 352 which was received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (H. Con. Res. 352) directing the clerk of the 
     House of Representatives to make technical corrections in the 
     enrollment of a bill.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent the resolution be agreed to and the 
motion to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (H. Con. Res. 352) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
