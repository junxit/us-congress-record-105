# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF JANE E. HENNEY

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
       UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF JANE E. HENNEY

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to the consideration of calendar No. 810, the 
nomination of Jane E. Henney, of New Mexico, to be Commissioner of Food 
and Drugs for the Department of Health and Human Services. I further 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed immediately to the vote 
on the confirmation of the nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
