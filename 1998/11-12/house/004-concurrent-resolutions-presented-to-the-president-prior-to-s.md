# CONCURRENT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 297, H.Con.Res. 298, H.Con.Res. 310, H.Con.Res. 311, H.Con.Res. 326

```
  CONCURRENT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Oversight reported that that 
committee did on the following date present to the President, for his 
approval, concurrent resolutions of the House of the following titles:

           On October 5, 1998:
       H. Con. Res. 310. Authorizing the use of the Rotunda of the 
     Capitol.
       H. Con. Res. 326. Authorizing the use of the Rotunda of the 
     Capitol.
       H. Con. Res. 311. Honoring the memory of Detective John 
     Gibson and Private Jacob Chesnut of the United States Capitol 
     Police.
       H. Con. Res. 297. Adjournment resolution.
       H. Con. Res. 298. Congress expresses condolences to the 
     State and people of Florida for the losses suffered as a 
     result of the wild land fires occurring in June and July 
     1998.

                          ____________________
```
