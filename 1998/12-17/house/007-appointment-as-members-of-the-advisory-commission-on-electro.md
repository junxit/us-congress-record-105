# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE ADVISORY COMMISSION ON ELECTRONIC COMMERCE

**Measures:** H.Res. 594

```
    APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE ADVISORY COMMISSION ON ELECTRONIC 
                                COMMERCE

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to section 1102(b)(1)(C) of the Internet Tax 
Freedom Act and Section 5 of House Resolution 594, 105th Congress, the 
Speaker on Friday, November 27, 1998 appointed the following 
individuals on the part of the House to the Advisory Commission on 
Electronic Commerce:
  Mr. Grover Norquist, Virginia;
  Mr. Richard D. Parsons, New York;
  Mr. David Pottruck, California;
  Mr. James Gilmore, Virginia; and
  Mr. Dean Andal, California.

                          ____________________
```
