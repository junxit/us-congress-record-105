# A TRIBUTE TO NICHOLAS COLGLAZIER

**Speaking:** Schaffer, Bob (S000112) [R-CO]

```
                    A TRIBUTE TO NICHOLAS COLGLAZIER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOB SCHAFFER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 18, 1998

  Mr. BOB SCHAFFER of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay 
tribute to Nicholas

[[Page E2354]]

Colglazier who was recently selected an FFA Star by 1-800-COLLECT. 
Nicholas will attend either the International Future Farmers of 
America, or FFA, Washington Leadership Conference, or Leaders Shape 
next summer. His fine leadership abilities through the Holyoke FFA 
propelled him to these exceptional honors. Mr. Speaker, I am proud of 
Nicholas Colglazier for his hard work, dedication and accomplishments. 
Through good efforts such as his, the youth of today will become the 
leadership of tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
