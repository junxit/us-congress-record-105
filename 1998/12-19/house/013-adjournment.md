# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 353

```
[[Page H12044]]
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to section 3 of House Concurrent 
Resolution 353, and as the designee for the Majority Leader, I move 
that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In accordance with the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 353, the Chair declares the second session of the 
105th Congress adjourned sine die.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 36 minutes p.m.), pursuant to section 3 
of House Concurrent Resolution 353, the House adjourned.
```
